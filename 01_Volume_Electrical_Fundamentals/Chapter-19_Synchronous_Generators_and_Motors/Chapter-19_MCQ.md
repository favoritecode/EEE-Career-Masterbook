\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

In steady operation, a Synchronous-Machine Rotor runs at:

A. Zero speed
B. Half Synchronous Speed
C. Synchronous Speed
D. A speed determined only by load torque

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*C. Synchronous Speed\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

The Rotor magnetic field remains locked with the fundamental Stator Rotating Magnetic Field.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A describes standstill, not synchronized operation.
\- B has no general basis for a Synchronous Machine.
\- D is false because normal load changes the Power angle, not steady synchronous speed.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Synchronous Machine → zero steady-state slip.
