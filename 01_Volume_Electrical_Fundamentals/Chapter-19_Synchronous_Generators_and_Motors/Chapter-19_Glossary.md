\# Chapter 19

\# Glossary

\## Synchronous Generators and Motors

\---

\## Synchronous Machine

An AC rotating machine whose Rotor field remains in step with the fundamental Stator Rotating Magnetic Field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt-ampere (VA) or watt (W)

\---

\## Synchronous Generator

A machine that converts mechanical Power into AC electrical Power at synchronous frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in VA, kVA or MVA

\---

\## Alternator

The common name for a Synchronous Generator producing alternating Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G or ALT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in VA, kVA or MVA

\---

\## Synchronous Motor

An AC Motor whose Rotor runs at Synchronous Speed during steady operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SynM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Stator

The stationary laminated core and Three-Phase Armature-winding assembly.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Rotor

The rotating field assembly of a conventional Synchronous Machine.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Speed in revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Armature Winding

The winding in which AC EMF is induced or through which AC input Current flows.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A, U/V/W or R/Y/B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Field Winding

A Rotor winding supplied with DC to establish the main magnetic poles.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Salient-Pole Rotor

A Rotor with physically projecting magnetic poles, usually used at low or medium speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SPR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cylindrical Rotor

A smooth, mechanically strong Non-Salient Rotor commonly used in high-speed turbo-Alternators.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Air Gap

The physical clearance separating the Stator and Rotor magnetic surfaces.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: g

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m), commonly millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Synchronous Speed

The mechanical speed corresponding to electrical frequency and pole number.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ns

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Pole Number

The total number of north and south magnetic poles in a rotating machine.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless count

\---

\## Electrical Frequency

The number of complete AC cycles occurring each second.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f

\*\*Unit\*\*: hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Slip

The normalized difference between Synchronous Speed and Rotor speed; it is zero in steady synchronous operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: s

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Prime Mover

A mechanical source, such as a turbine or engine, that drives a Generator shaft.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Mechanical Power in watt (W)

\---

\## Generated EMF

The internal alternating Voltage induced in an Alternator Armature winding.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E or Eph

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Winding Factor

The product of distribution and pitch factors accounting for winding arrangement in the generated-EMF equation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: kw

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Flux per Pole

The total Magnetic Flux associated with one machine pole.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: weber (Wb)

\---

\## Synchronous Reactance

The combined effective per-phase reactance used in simplified steady-state Synchronous-Machine analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xs

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Power Angle

The electrical angular displacement between relevant internal and terminal or field quantities associated with Power transfer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: δ

\*\*Unit\*\*: degree (°) or radian (rad)

\---

\## Pull-Out Torque

The maximum load torque a Synchronous Motor can sustain without losing synchronism under specified conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tpo

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Excitation

The controlled establishment of Rotor Magnetic Flux using field Current or another field source.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Exc

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Field Current

The DC Current flowing through a wound Rotor field circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: If

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Automatic Voltage Regulator

A controller that adjusts excitation according to measured Voltage and operating limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: AVR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Static Excitation

An excitation arrangement using stationary controlled rectifiers and Rotor connections through Slip Rings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Static Exciter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field output in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Brushless Excitation

An excitation system using a shaft-mounted exciter and rotating rectifiers without main-field Brushes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BEX

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field output in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Rotating Rectifier

A rotating diode assembly that converts exciter AC into DC for the main Rotor field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Slip Ring

A continuous conducting ring providing electrical connection to a rotating field winding through Brushes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current rating in ampere (A)

\---

\## Synchronization

The controlled matching and connection of an incoming Alternator to an energized AC system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Sync

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Phase Sequence

The order in which Three-Phase waveforms reach corresponding positive values.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ABC or ACB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Phase Angle Difference

The angular separation between corresponding incoming and busbar Voltage phasors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Δθ

\*\*Unit\*\*: degree (°) or radian (rad)

\---

\## Synchroscope

An instrument indicating relative phase angle and frequency difference between two AC systems.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SY

\*\*Unit\*\*: Angle in degree (°) and relative rotation indication

\---

\## Synch-Check Relay

A protective relay that permits breaker closing only when synchronizing quantities are within set limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ANSI 25

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Busbar

A common conductor system to which Generators, transformers and loads are connected.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BUS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage in volt (V), Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Real Power

The average Power converted into useful work or energy transfer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Reactive Power

The alternating exchange of energy associated mainly with electric and Magnetic Fields.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere reactive (var)

\---

\## Power Factor

The ratio of real Power to apparent Power in an AC system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF or cosφ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Underexcited Motor

A Synchronous Motor operating with relatively low field excitation and generally drawing lagging reactive Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: UE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Overexcited Motor

A Synchronous Motor operating with relatively high field excitation and capable of supplying leading reactive Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## V-Curve

A curve of Armature Current against field Current at constant mechanical load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ia–If curve

\*\*Unit\*\*: Both axes commonly in ampere (A)

\---

\## Synchronous Condenser

An overexcited Synchronous Machine used mainly for controllable reactive-Power support.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SC

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere reactive (var)

\---

\## Hunting

Oscillation of Rotor angle about its steady synchronous position after a disturbance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rotor-angle oscillation

\*\*Unit\*\*: Angle in degree (°), oscillation frequency in hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Damper Winding

A short-circuited cage-like Rotor winding used for damping oscillation and assisting Motor starting.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Loss of Excitation

An abnormal condition in which Rotor field excitation is lost or becomes insufficient.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOE or ANSI 40 protection

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Loss of Synchronism

An abnormal condition in which the Rotor can no longer remain locked with the system Rotating Magnetic Field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Out-of-step

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Reverse Power

Power flow into a Generator that tends to drive it as a Motor after loss of prime-mover input.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ANSI 32 protection

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Negative-Sequence Current

An unbalanced Three-Phase Current component rotating opposite to the positive-sequence field and causing Rotor heating.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I2

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Governor

A control system that regulates prime-mover input, speed and real-Power response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GOV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Speed in rpm or Power in watt (W)

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled procedure for isolating hazardous energy and preventing unauthorized re-energization.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## End of Chapter 19 Glossary
