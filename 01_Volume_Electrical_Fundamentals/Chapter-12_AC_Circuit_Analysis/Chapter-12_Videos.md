\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review AC waveforms, Frequency, RMS Value and Phase Difference.

3\. Watch animations of R, L and C Voltage-Current relationships.

4\. Learn how Frequency affects XL and XC.

5\. Practice Reactance and Impedance calculations.

6\. Draw Impedance Triangles and Phasor Diagrams.

7\. Analyze Series RLC Circuits step by step.

8\. Study the Resonance condition and its practical effects.

9\. Compare Active, Reactive and Apparent Power.

10\. Practice Power Factor and Power Triangle calculations.

11\. Review safe AC measurement and Power-Factor correction methods.

12\. Solve the MCQs.

13\. Practice the Viva questions.

14\. Review the chapter summary and glossary.
