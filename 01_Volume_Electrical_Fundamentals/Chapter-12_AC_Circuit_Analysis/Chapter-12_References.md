\# Reference Notes

\- Inductive Reactance increases with Frequency and Inductance according to XL = 2pi fL.

\- Capacitive Reactance decreases as Frequency or Capacitance increases according to XC = 1/(2pi fC).

\- AC Impedance combines Resistance and net Reactance and may be written as Z = R + jX.

\- In a Series RLC Circuit, Resonance occurs when XL = XC and the net Reactance becomes zero.

\- For sinusoidal quantities, Power Factor is PF = P/S = cos(phi).

\- The Power Triangle relationship is S^2 = P^2 + Q^2, using consistent units.

\- Harmonics, high-Q Resonance and component tolerances can cause actual Circuit behavior to differ from ideal calculations.

\- Practical AC measurements must use correctly rated RMS instruments and follow applicable safety procedures, standards and manufacturer instructions.
