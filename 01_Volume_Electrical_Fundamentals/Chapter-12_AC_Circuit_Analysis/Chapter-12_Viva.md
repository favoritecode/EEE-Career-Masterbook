\# Basic Viva

\### Viva-001

\*\*Question\*\*

How does a pure resistor behave in an AC circuit?

\*\*Answer\*\*

For an ideal resistor, Voltage and Current are in phase, Impedance equals Resistance and electrical energy is converted into heat.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- V and I are in phase
\- Z=R
\- It consumes real Power

\*\*Remember\*\*

Pure R → V and I In Phase

\*\*Question\*\*

What is Inductance?

\*\*Answer\*\*

Inductance is the property by which changing Current produces an opposing induced EMF. Its symbol is L and unit is Henry.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- It opposes Current change
\- It stores magnetic-field energy
\- Unit: Henry

\### Viva-004

\*\*Question\*\*

Define Inductive Reactance.

\*\*Answer\*\*

Inductive Reactance is the opposition an ideal inductor presents to sinusoidal AC. XL=2πfL and its unit is Ohm.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- Symbol: XL
\- It increases with f and L
\- Unit: Ohm

\### Viva-007

\*\*Question\*\*

State the Phase relationships of ideal R, L and C.

\*\*Answer\*\*

For R, Voltage and Current are in phase. For L, Current lags Voltage by 90°. For C, Current leads Voltage by 90°.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- R: in phase
\- L: I lags V
\- C: I leads V

\*\*Remember\*\*

R In Phase; L Lags; C Leads

\*\*Question\*\*

What is a Series RLC Circuit?

\*\*Answer\*\*

It connects Resistance, Inductance and Capacitance in one Current path. Its Impedance is Z=R+j(XL−XC).

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- Same Current flows through all elements
\- Net Reactance is XL−XC
\- Impedance depends on Frequency

\*\*Remember\*\*

Series RLC: Z=R+j(XL−XC)

\### Viva-009

\*\*Question\*\*

Define Power Factor.

\*\*Answer\*\*

Power Factor is real Power divided by apparent Power. For sinusoidal systems, PF=cosφ, where φ is the Voltage-Current Phase Difference.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- PF=P/S
\- It is dimensionless
\- It may be leading or lagging

\*\*Remember\*\*

Power Factor = P/S = cosφ

\- Q is measured in var
\- S is measured in VA

\### Viva-013

\*\*Question\*\*

How does Frequency affect XL and XC?

\*\*Answer\*\*

XL increases directly with Frequency, while XC decreases inversely with Frequency. Therefore, inductors and capacitors provide opposite frequency responses.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- XL=2πfL
\- XC=1/(2πfC)
\- Frequency changes circuit Impedance

\*\*Remember\*\*

f Up: XL Up, XC Down

\### Viva-014

\*\*Question\*\*

Explain Series Resonance.

\*\*Answer\*\*

Series Resonance occurs when XL=XC. Net Reactance is zero, Impedance is minimum and equal to R, Current is maximum and Power Factor is unity in the ideal model.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- XL and XC cancel
\- Z=R
\- Current is maximum

\*\*Remember\*\*

Series Resonance → XL=XC

\### Viva-016

\*\*Question\*\*

Why does low Power Factor increase losses?

\*\*Answer\*\*

For fixed real Power and Voltage, lower PF requires higher Current. Higher Current increases I²R loss, Voltage drop and equipment loading.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- P=VI PF for single phase
\- Low PF means higher Current
\- Higher Current increases thermal loss

\*\*Remember\*\*

Low PF → High Current → High Loss

\### Viva-018

\*\*Question\*\*

Differentiate real, reactive and apparent Power.

\*\*Answer\*\*

Real Power performs net work and is measured in W. Reactive Power represents periodic energy exchange and is measured in var. Apparent Power is RMS VA and combines both.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- P: Watt
\- Q: var
\- S: VA

\*\*Remember\*\*

P + jQ = Complex Power; |S|²=P²+Q²

\### Viva-019

\*\*Question\*\*

Why can capacitor banks create resonance problems?

\*\*Answer\*\*

System inductance and installed capacitance can resonate near a fundamental or harmonic Frequency, amplifying Current or Voltage and overstressing equipment.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- The network has L and C
\- Harmonics see frequency-dependent Reactance
\- Detuning may be required

\*\*Remember\*\*

Check Harmonics and Resonance Before Correction

\### Viva-020

\*\*Question\*\*

How should a practical AC Circuit be analyzed safely?

\*\*Answer\*\*

Identify Frequency and waveform, use RMS phasors consistently, calculate R, X, Z, Phase and Power, verify ratings and harmonics, and use suitably rated instruments with safe isolation procedures.

\*\*Key Points\*\*

\- Use one RMS/phasor convention
\- Check Power and Phase
\- Respect instrument and equipment ratings

\*\*Remember\*\*

Calculate, Verify and Work Safely
