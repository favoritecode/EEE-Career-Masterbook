\# Chapter 12

\# Glossary

\## AC Resistance

Opposition that converts electrical energy into heat in an AC circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Inductance

The property by which changing Current produces an opposing induced EMF.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: L

\*\*Unit\*\*: Henry (H)

\## Capacitance

The ability to store electric charge and energy in an Electric Field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C

\*\*Unit\*\*: Farad (F)

\## Inductive Reactance

Opposition an ideal inductor presents to sinusoidal AC.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL=2πfL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Capacitive Reactance

Opposition an ideal capacitor presents to sinusoidal AC.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XC=1/(2πfC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Reactance

The imaginary component of Impedance associated with energy storage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Impedance

The total complex opposition to AC, combining Resistance and Reactance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z=R+jX

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Impedance Magnitude

The absolute value of complex Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: |Z|

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Impedance Angle

The Phase Angle of Impedance between its real and imaginary components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Inductor Impedance

The ideal complex Impedance of an inductor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ZL=jXL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Capacitor Impedance

The ideal complex Impedance of a capacitor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ZC=−jXC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Phasor

A complex rotating-vector representation of sinusoidal magnitude and Phase at one Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V̅, Ī or Z

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on quantity

\## Phase Angle

Angular displacement between two same-Frequency sinusoidal quantities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## In Phase

Condition where corresponding points of two waveforms occur together.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: φ=0°

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°)

\## Leading Current

Current that reaches a corresponding point before Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I leads V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Lagging Current

Current that reaches a corresponding point after Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I lags V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Pure Resistive Circuit

An ideal AC circuit containing only Resistance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z=R

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Pure Inductive Circuit

An ideal AC circuit containing only Inductance and no loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z=jXL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Pure Capacitive Circuit

An ideal AC circuit containing only Capacitance and no loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z=−jXC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Series RLC Circuit

A circuit with R, L and C connected in one Current path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z=R+j(XL−XC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Net Reactance

The algebraic combination of Inductive and Capacitive Reactance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X=XL−XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Resonance

Condition where Inductive and Capacitive Reactances cancel.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL=XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Resonant Frequency

Frequency at which an ideal RLC Circuit is resonant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f₀=1/(2π√LC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Quality Factor

A dimensionless measure of resonance sharpness and energy storage relative to loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Bandwidth

Difference between upper and lower half-power Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BW=f₂−f₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Selectivity

Ability of a resonant circuit to respond strongly to a desired Frequency range.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Real Power

Average Power converted into useful work or heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Reactive Power

Power representing periodic energy exchange with reactive elements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt-Ampere Reactive (var)

\## Apparent Power

Product of RMS Voltage and Current magnitudes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt-Ampere (VA)

\## Complex Power

Complex quantity combining real and reactive Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S=P+jQ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt-Ampere (VA)

\## Power Factor

Ratio of real Power to apparent Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF=P/S=cosφ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Unity Power Factor

Condition where Voltage and Current are in phase and PF equals one.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF=1

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Lagging Power Factor

Power Factor condition where Current lags Voltage, commonly caused by inductive loads.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF lag

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Leading Power Factor

Power Factor condition where Current leads Voltage, commonly caused by capacitive behavior.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF lead

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Power Triangle

Right-triangle representation relating P, Q and S.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S²=P²+Q²

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Reactive Energy

Time-integrated reactive Power in an AC system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: var-hour (varh)

\## Power Factor Correction

Reduction of reactive demand to improve Power Factor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PFC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Capacitor Bank

A group of capacitors used for reactive compensation or Voltage support.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: kvar

\## Overcompensation

Excessive capacitive correction that creates an undesired leading Power Factor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Harmonic

A sinusoidal component at an integer multiple of Fundamental Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: h or n

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Fundamental Frequency

Lowest Frequency component of a periodic waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## RMS Phasor

A Phasor whose magnitude is expressed as RMS Value.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on quantity

\## Admittance

Reciprocal of Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Y=1/Z

\*\*Unit\*\*: Siemens (S)

\## Conductance

Real component of Admittance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Siemens (S)

\## Susceptance

Imaginary component of Admittance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Siemens (S)

\## True-RMS Meter

Instrument that measures RMS from the actual waveform within specified limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: True RMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on quantity

\## Power Analyzer

Instrument measuring Voltage, Current, Power, Power Factor and related AC quantities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Voltage Drop

Reduction in Voltage caused by Current through circuit Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Frequency Response

Variation of circuit magnitude and Phase response with Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H(f)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or application-dependent

\## Detuned Capacitor Bank

A capacitor bank combined with reactors to shift resonance away from troublesome harmonics.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: kvar
