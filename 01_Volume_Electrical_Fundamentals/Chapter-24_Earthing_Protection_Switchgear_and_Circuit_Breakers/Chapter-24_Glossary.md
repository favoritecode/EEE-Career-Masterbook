\# Chapter 24

\# Glossary

\## Earthing, Protection, Switchgear and Circuit Breakers

\---

\## Electrical Protection

The coordinated detection and isolation of abnormal electrical conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Protection Zone

A defined part of a Power system covered by specified protective devices.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Reliability

The ability of protection to operate correctly when required and remain secure otherwise.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Probability or percent (%)

\---

\## Selectivity

The ability to isolate the smallest practical faulted section while retaining healthy supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Sensitivity

The ability of protection to detect the minimum relevant abnormal quantity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on measured quantity

\---

\## Stability

The ability of protection to avoid unwanted operation for external faults or permitted conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Operating Time

The interval from an initiating condition to protective operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: t

\*\*Unit\*\*: second (s) or millisecond (ms)

\---

\## Earthing

Intentional connection of selected system points or conductive parts to Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Grounding

A widely used alternative term for Earthing.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Earth Electrode

A conductive part in direct electrical contact with soil or another conductive medium.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Earthing Conductor

A conductor connecting the Main Earthing Terminal to an Earth Electrode.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Main Earthing Terminal

The principal terminal or bar connecting Protective, bonding and Earthing Conductors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MET

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Protective Conductor

A conductor provided for electrical safety and fault-current return.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Equipotential Bonding

Electrical connection of conductive parts to reduce dangerous potential differences.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Touch Voltage

Voltage between conductive parts that a person may touch simultaneously.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: UT

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Step Voltage

Voltage difference between two points on the ground separated by a person's step.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: US

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Ground Potential Rise

Rise of an Earthing system potential relative to remote Earth during fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GPR

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Soil Resistivity

The intrinsic electrical Resistivity of soil under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ρ

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm metre (Ω·m)

\---

\## Earth Resistance

Effective Power-frequency Resistance between an electrode system and remote Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RA

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Earth Impedance

Opposition of an Earthing path to AC or transient Current, including Resistance and Reactance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ZE

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Plate Earthing

An Earthing method using a buried metallic plate electrode.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Pipe Earthing

An Earthing method using a metallic pipe electrode in soil.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Rod Earthing

An Earthing method using a driven rod electrode.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Strip Earthing

An Earthing method using a buried horizontal strip or wire electrode.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Earth Grid

An interconnected buried conductor network used to bond equipment and control surface potentials.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Mesh size in metre (m); Resistance in Ω

\---

\## Fall-of-Potential Test

An Earth-Resistance test using a Current probe and a Potential probe.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FOP

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Clamp Earth Tester

An instrument measuring Earth-loop Resistance through available parallel paths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Wenner Method

A four-probe method used to measure apparent soil Resistivity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm metre (Ω·m)

\---

\## Equipment Earthing

Connection of exposed conductive equipment parts to the Protective system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance or Impedance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## System Earthing

Intentional Earthing of a source or network point, commonly Neutral.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A); Resistance in Ω

\---

\## Solid Earthing

System Earthing with no intentional significant Impedance between Neutral and Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Fault Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Resistance Earthing

System Earthing through an intentional resistor that limits Earth-Fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NER/NGR

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω); ampere (A)

\---

\## Neutral Earthing Resistor

A resistor connected between system Neutral and Earth to limit Earth-Fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NER/NGR

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Short Circuit

An unintended low-impedance connection between points of different potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Fault Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Open Circuit

A broken or disconnected path that prevents intended Current flow.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Earth Fault

An unintended connection between a live conductor and Earth or Earthed metal.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Fault Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Overload

Current exceeding continuous equipment or conductor rating through the intended path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OL

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Fault Current

Current flowing because of an electrical fault.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: If

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Prospective Fault Current

The Current expected at a point if a fault of specified type occurs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PFC

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Residual Current

Vector sum of Currents in live Conductors at a measurement point.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IΔ

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or milliampere (mA)

\---

\## Fuse

An Overcurrent device whose element melts and opens the circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Fuse Element

The calibrated Current-carrying part intended to melt during Overcurrent.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); I²t in A²s

\---

\## Miniature Circuit Breaker

A resettable Low-Voltage Overcurrent device commonly used for final circuits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Moulded Case Circuit Breaker

A resettable Breaker for larger Current and fault duty, often with adjustable protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Residual Current Circuit Breaker

A switching device that trips for specified residual Current and normally lacks integral Overcurrent protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: milliampere (mA); ampere (A)

\---

\## Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker

A term historically associated with voltage-operated Earth-leakage protection and sometimes used ambiguously.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ELCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V), for voltage-operated type

\---

\## Residual Current Breaker with Overcurrent Protection

A device combining residual-current, overload and Short-Circuit protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCBO

\*\*Unit\*\*: milliampere (mA); ampere (A)

\---

\## Breaking Capacity

The maximum prospective Current a device can interrupt safely under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Icu/Icn

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Making Capacity

The maximum prospective peak Current a device can close onto safely under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Icm

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere peak (kAp)

\---

\## Trip Curve

A device's operating-time relationship to Current or another measured quantity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time versus Current

\---

\## Pickup

The threshold at which a Relay or protective element begins to operate.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I> or setting

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A), volt (V), or other

\---

\## Protective Relay

A device that measures quantities, applies protection logic and issues trip or alarm commands.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Relay

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on function

\---

\## Overcurrent Relay

A Relay operating when Current exceeds a set threshold according to its characteristic.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 50/51

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Earth-Fault Relay

A Relay detecting Current associated with a fault to Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 50N/51N or 50G/51G

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Differential Relay

A Relay comparing quantities entering and leaving a protected zone.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 87

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or per unit

\---

\## Distance Relay

A Relay estimating fault distance from measured Voltage and Current Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 21

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Current Transformer

An instrument Transformer that supplies a scaled Current for metering or protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ratio; ampere (A)

\---

\## Voltage Transformer

An instrument Transformer that supplies a scaled Voltage for metering or protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VT/PT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ratio; volt (V)

\---

\## Trip Circuit

The electrical path that energizes a Breaker's tripping mechanism.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A)

\---

\## Trip Coil

An electromagnetic actuator that releases a Circuit Breaker mechanism.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TC

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A)

\---

\## Secondary Injection

Testing a Relay by applying simulated signals to its secondary inputs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or volt (V)

\---

\## Primary Injection

Testing by passing Current through the primary Power path and associated measurement chain.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Switchgear

An assembly for switching, interruption, isolation, protection, measurement and control.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A); kA

\---

\## Disconnector

A mechanical device providing isolation in the open position and not normally intended to interrupt load Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DS

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Earthing Switch

A mechanical device used to Earth an isolated circuit section within its rating and procedure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ES

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA) withstand

\---

\## Busbar

A conductor or conductor system serving as a common connection for multiple circuits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Bus

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Interlock

A mechanical or electrical arrangement preventing an unsafe operating sequence.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Circuit Breaker

A device capable of making, carrying and interrupting normal and specified fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Air Circuit Breaker

A Circuit Breaker using air at atmospheric pressure as the interrupting medium.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ACB

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Vacuum Circuit Breaker

A Circuit Breaker using sealed vacuum interrupters for arc control.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilovolt (kV); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Oil Circuit Breaker

A Circuit Breaker using insulating oil for arc control and insulation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilovolt (kV); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## SF6 Circuit Breaker

A Circuit Breaker using sulfur hexafluoride gas for dielectric and arc-control performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SF₆ CB

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilovolt (kV); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Arc

A self-sustaining electrical discharge through an ionized medium.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A); Energy in joule (J)

\---

\## Arc Extinction

The process of stopping an electrical arc and restoring dielectric strength.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time in millisecond (ms)

\---

\## Recovery Voltage

Voltage appearing across Breaker contacts after Current interruption.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RV/TRV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V) or kilovolt (kV)

\---

\## Surge

A transient wave of Voltage, Current or Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A)

\---

\## Surge Protection Device

A device that limits transient overvoltage and diverts surge Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SPD

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilovolt (kV); kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Voltage Protection Level

The maximum Voltage expected across an SPD under specified test conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Up

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V) or kilovolt (kV)

\---

\## Nominal Discharge Current

Peak Current through an SPD for a specified Current-wave shape.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: In

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Impulse Current

Peak lightning-current test quantity used particularly for Type 1 SPDs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iimp

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Lightning Protection System

A coordinated system reducing physical damage and internal surge effects from lightning.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LPS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Protection level or class

\---

\## Air Termination

Part of an external LPS intended to intercept lightning strikes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in metre (m)

\---

\## Down Conductor

Part of an LPS carrying lightning Current from air termination to Earth termination.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Earth Termination

Part of an LPS dispersing lightning Current into Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance or Impedance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Lightning Protection Zone

A zone defining the lightning electromagnetic environment and protection boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LPZ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Zone number

\---

\## Protection Coordination

Selection of device ratings and characteristics for safe, selective fault clearing.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time–Current relationship

\---

\## Discrimination

Another term for selective operation between series protective devices.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current range in ampere (A)

\---

\## Backup Protection

Protection that operates if the primary protection or associated interruption fails.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Operating time in second (s)

\---

\## Cascading

Use of verified upstream protection to increase downstream fault-interruption capability.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Arc Flash

A rapid release of thermal and radiant Energy caused by an electrical arc.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Incident Energy in cal/cm² or J/cm²

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled method preventing unauthorized energization during work.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\# End of Chapter 24 Glossary
