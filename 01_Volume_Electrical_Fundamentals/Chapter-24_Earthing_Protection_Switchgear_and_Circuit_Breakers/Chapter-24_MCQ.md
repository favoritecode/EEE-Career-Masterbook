\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main objective of Electrical Protection?

A. Detect abnormal conditions and isolate the affected section
B. Increase every load Current
C. Remove all Earthing
D. Prevent all faults from ever occurring

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Detect abnormal conditions and isolate the affected section\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Protection detects abnormal operation and commands interruption to limit danger and damage.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is incorrect because it does not describe the required protection principle or device function.
\- C is incorrect because it does not describe the required protection principle or device function.
\- D is incorrect because it does not describe the required protection principle or device function.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Protection reduces fault consequences; it does not guarantee that faults never occur.
