\# Reference Notes

\- Electrical equipment should normally be de-energized before work, and only qualified persons should perform work on exposed energized parts under applicable procedures.

\- An OFF switch or open Circuit Breaker does not by itself prove that all hazardous energy has been isolated.

\- Absence of Voltage must be verified using suitable, correctly rated and functional test equipment.

\- Lockout and Tagout help prevent unexpected energization, while stored energy must also be released or controlled.

\- PPE must be selected from the assessed Hazard and does not replace elimination, isolation or engineering controls.

\- An Ammeter is connected in series, while a Voltmeter is connected in parallel.

\- Resistance and continuity measurements require an isolated circuit with stored energy safely discharged.

\- Meter, leads, input protection, range and measurement category must suit the expected electrical environment.

\- IEC 60617 is an official source for standardized graphical symbols used in electrotechnical diagrams.

\- SI prefix and unit symbols are case-sensitive; actual work must follow applicable regulations, standards, workplace procedures and manufacturer instructions.
