\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the primary purpose of Electrical Safety?

A. To increase supply Voltage
B. To prevent injury, fire and equipment damage
C. To eliminate all electrical measurements
D. To make every conductor an insulator

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. To prevent injury, fire and equipment damage\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Electrical Safety uses knowledge, procedures and protective measures to control electrical hazards and prevent harm to people, equipment and property.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* Safety does not increase the supply Voltage.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Safe measurements are necessary in electrical work.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Safety controls hazards; it does not change every material.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Electrical Safety = Hazard Control + Safe Work Practice
