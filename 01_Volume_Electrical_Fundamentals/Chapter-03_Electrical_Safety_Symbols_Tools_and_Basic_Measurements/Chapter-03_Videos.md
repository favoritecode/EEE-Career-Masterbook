\## About This Section

The following educational resources are recommended for strengthening the concepts discussed in this chapter. Select videos that demonstrate de-energized training setups, correct PPE selection, standard symbols, inspected tools, correctly rated instruments, safe probe control and proper engineering terminology.

Electrical demonstrations must be performed using safe training equipment under competent supervision. A video is not a substitute for task-specific training, authorization, standards or workplace procedures.

\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Identify the difference between Hazard and Risk.

3\. Review the hierarchy of electrical safety controls.

4\. Watch a de-energization and verification demonstration.

5\. Compare common PPE types and their limitations.

6\. Practice drawing and recognizing electrical symbols.

7\. Identify basic tools and inspect their condition.

8\. Learn Ammeter, Voltmeter and Ohmmeter connections.

9\. Practice Multimeter function, range, socket and lead selection.

10\. Observe safe Resistance, continuity and Clamp Meter demonstrations.

11\. Practice SI Unit and prefix conversions.

12\. Solve the MCQs.

13\. Practice the Viva questions.

14\. Review the chapter summary and glossary.
