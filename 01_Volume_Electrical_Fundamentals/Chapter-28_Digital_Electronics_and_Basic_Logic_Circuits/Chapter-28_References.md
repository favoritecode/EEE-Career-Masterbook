\## Digital Electronics and Basic Logic Circuits

\---

\## About This Chapter

This chapter was prepared using established Digital Electronics, Digital Logic Design and computer-engineering textbooks together with recognized graphical-symbol and integrated-circuit standards. The references support deeper study of Number Systems, codes, Logic Gates, Truth Tables, Boolean Algebra, De Morgan's Theorems, Karnaugh Maps and basic Combinational Logic Circuits.

Logic symbols, Voltage thresholds, timing, fan-out, Power Consumption and noise tolerance depend on the selected Logic family and device. A practical design must therefore use the current manufacturer datasheet in addition to textbook Logic models.

\# Reference Use Guide

\- Use Floyd or Tocci, Widmer and Moss for a beginner-friendly first study.
\- Use Mano and Ciletti for structured Digital-design theory and examination preparation.
\- Use Brown and Vranesic or Roth and Kinney for deeper Logic design and simplification practice.
\- Use Wakerly for practical electrical, timing and implementation details.
\- Use IEC and IEEE materials when preparing professional symbols and engineering diagrams.
\- Use the current manufacturer datasheet when selecting or connecting an actual Logic IC.
\- Confirm the applicable edition of every standard and local institutional requirement before professional use.
