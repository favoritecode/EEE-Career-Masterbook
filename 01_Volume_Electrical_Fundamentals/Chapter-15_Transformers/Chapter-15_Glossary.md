\# Chapter 15

\# Glossary

\## Transformer

A static electromagnetic device that transfers AC electrical energy between circuits through Mutual Induction, normally at the same Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Primary Winding

The Transformer winding connected to the input supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N₁, V₁ or I₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Turn, Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## Secondary Winding

The Transformer winding connected to the output circuit or load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N₂, V₂ or I₂

\*\*Unit\*\*: Turn, Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## High-Voltage Winding

The winding designed for the higher Voltage level of a Transformer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Low-Voltage Winding

The winding designed for the lower Voltage level of a Transformer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Mutual Induction

The production of EMF in one coil because changing Current in another coupled coil changes linked Magnetic Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: M

\*\*Unit\*\*: Henry (H) for Mutual Inductance

\## Main Flux

The useful alternating core Flux that links both Primary and Secondary windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Weber (Wb)

\## Leakage Flux

Magnetic Flux that does not link both intended windings completely.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φ leakage

\*\*Unit\*\*: Weber (Wb)

\## No-Load Current

The small Primary Current drawn with the Secondary open to establish Flux and supply No-Load Loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I₀

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Magnetizing Current

The component of No-Load Current responsible mainly for producing core Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Im

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Core-Type Transformer

A Transformer construction in which windings surround substantial parts of the core limbs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Shell-Type Transformer

A Transformer construction in which the core surrounds a substantial part of the windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Magnetic Core

The magnetic structure providing a low-Reluctance path for Main Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Lamination

A thin insulated sheet used to construct a power-frequency core and restrict Eddy Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Bushing

An insulated device that allows an energized conductor to pass through an earthed tank or enclosure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Tap Changer

A mechanism that changes effective winding turns to adjust Transformer Voltage Ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Step-Up Transformer

A Transformer intended to provide a Secondary Voltage higher than its Primary Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N₂>N₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Step-Down Transformer

A Transformer intended to provide a Secondary Voltage lower than its Primary Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N₂<N₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Isolation Transformer

A Transformer with separate windings designed to provide galvanic separation, often with approximately 1:1 ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Autotransformer

A Transformer using one tapped winding shared electrically by input and output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: AT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Power Transformer

A Transformer used mainly in generation and transmission systems at substantial power ratings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: VA, kVA or MVA

\## Distribution Transformer

A Transformer that supplies distribution and utilization networks under varying load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DT

\*\*Unit\*\*: kVA

\## Instrument Transformer

A Transformer that supplies scaled Current or Voltage for measurement and protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Current Transformer

An Instrument Transformer that provides a scaled Secondary Current corresponding to Primary Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Voltage Transformer

An Instrument Transformer that provides a scaled Secondary Voltage corresponding to Primary Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VT or PT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## EMF Equation

The sinusoidal-flux relationship between induced RMS EMF, Frequency, turns and maximum Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E=4.44fNΦm

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## EMF per Turn

The induced RMS EMF divided by winding turns under common Main Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Et=E/N

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt per Turn (V/turn)

\## Turns Ratio

The ratio between the numbers of turns in two Transformer windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: a=N₂/N₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Voltage Ratio

The ratio of Secondary to Primary Voltage; ideally equal to the corresponding Turns Ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V₂/V₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Current Ratio

The ratio of winding Currents; ideally inverse to the corresponding Turns Ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I₂/I₁=N₁/N₂

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Impedance Transformation

The apparent change of load Impedance when referred through a Transformer by the square of Turns Ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z′

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Core Loss

Power dissipated in the magnetic core, mainly as Hysteresis and Eddy-Current Loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcore

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Hysteresis Loss

Core loss caused by repeated magnetization through the material's Hysteresis Loop.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ph

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Eddy-Current Loss

Core loss caused by circulating Currents induced in conductive core material.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pe

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Copper Loss

Power dissipated by Transformer winding Resistance, approximately equal to I²R.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcu

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Stray Load Loss

Load-dependent loss caused mainly by Leakage Flux inducing Currents in windings and structural parts.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pstray

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Dielectric Loss

Power dissipated in insulation under alternating electric stress.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pd

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## No-Load Loss

Loss measured with the Secondary open, consisting mainly of Core Loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P₀

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Load Loss

Loss associated with load Current, mainly Copper and Stray Load Loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PLL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Transformer Efficiency

The ratio of useful output Power to input Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: η

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## Maximum Efficiency

The operating condition where variable Copper Loss approximately equals constant Core Loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcu=Pcore

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## All-Day Efficiency

The ratio of output energy to input energy over a 24-hour period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ηall-day

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## Voltage Regulation

The percentage change in Secondary terminal Voltage from No-Load to specified load under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## Equivalent Resistance

Combined winding Resistance referred to one chosen side of a Transformer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Req

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Leakage Reactance

Reactance caused by Leakage Flux that links only part of the winding system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xeq

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Equivalent Impedance

Combined equivalent Resistance and Leakage Reactance referred to one side.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Zeq

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Short-Circuit Test

A test using reduced applied Voltage with one winding shorted to determine load loss and equivalent Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SC Test

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Open-Circuit Test

A test with one winding open to determine No-Load Loss and excitation quantities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OC Test

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Transformer Rating

The specified apparent-power capacity of a Transformer under stated operating and cooling conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S rated

\*\*Unit\*\*: VA, kVA or MVA

\## Percent Impedance

The percentage of rated Voltage required to circulate rated Current with the other winding short-circuited under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: %Z

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## Vector Group

A code describing winding connection and phase displacement of a three-phase Transformer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Cooling Class

A designation describing the Transformer cooling medium and circulation method.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Overfluxing

Operation at excessive magnetic Flux, commonly caused by excessive Voltage-to-Frequency ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V/f

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless ratio or Percent (%)

\## Galvanic Isolation

Absence of a direct conductive connection between two circuits while energy or signals may transfer electromagnetically.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable
