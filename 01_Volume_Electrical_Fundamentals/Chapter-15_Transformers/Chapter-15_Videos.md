\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review Electromagnetic Induction and Faraday's Law.

3\. Watch a Transformer Flux and Mutual-Induction animation.

4\. Identify core, windings, insulation, bushings and cooling parts.

5\. Compare common Transformer types and applications.

6\. Practice the EMF Equation and Turns Ratio calculations.

7\. Practice ideal Voltage, Current and Impedance transformations.

8\. Compare Core, Copper, Stray and Dielectric Losses.

9\. Solve Efficiency and maximum-Efficiency problems.

10\. Solve Voltage Regulation problems at different Power Factors.

11\. Review CT, Autotransformer, isolation and nameplate safety.

12\. Solve the MCQs.

13\. Practice the Viva questions.

14\. Review the chapter summary and glossary.
