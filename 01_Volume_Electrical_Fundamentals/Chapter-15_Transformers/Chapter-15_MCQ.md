\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the operating principle of a transformer?

A. Self-induction only
B. Mutual induction
C. Electrostatic attraction
D. Chemical reaction

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Mutual induction\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Changing primary flux links the secondary and induces EMF.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* Self-induction only does not satisfy the stated transformer principle or calculation.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Electrostatic attraction does not satisfy the stated transformer principle or calculation.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Chemical reaction does not satisfy the stated transformer principle or calculation.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Changing flux and mutual induction are essential.
