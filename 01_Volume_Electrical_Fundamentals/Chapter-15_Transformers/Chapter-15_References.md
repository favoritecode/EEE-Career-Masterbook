\# Reference Notes

\- A conventional Transformer transfers AC energy through Mutual Induction and normally does not change Frequency.

\- For sinusoidal core Flux, the RMS induced EMF is E = 4.44fNΦm.

\- Ideal Voltage Ratio equals Turns Ratio, while ideal Current Ratio is inverse.

\- Core Loss mainly includes Hysteresis and Eddy-Current Loss; Copper Loss varies approximately with Current squared.

\- Maximum Efficiency occurs approximately when variable Copper Loss equals constant Core Loss.

\- Voltage Regulation depends on internal Impedance, load Current and load Power Factor.

\- A Current Transformer Secondary must not normally be open-circuited while Primary Current flows.

\- Actual selection, installation, testing and maintenance must follow applicable standards, regulations and manufacturer instructions.
