\# Chapter 18

\# Glossary

\## Single-Phase Motors

\---

\## Single-Phase Motor

An electric Motor designed mainly to operate from a Single-Phase AC supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 1φ Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Single-Phase Supply

An AC supply represented by one alternating Voltage waveform between active conductors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 1φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V), hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Single-Phase Induction Motor

A Motor in which Stator Flux induces Rotor Current and electromagnetic torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SPIM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Stator

The stationary laminated core and winding assembly of a Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Rotor

The rotating Motor assembly that develops mechanical output torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Speed in revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Squirrel-Cage Rotor

A Rotor containing conductive bars short-circuited by end rings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cage Rotor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Main Winding

The Stator winding that produces the main running Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: M or RUN

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Auxiliary Winding

A spatially displaced Stator winding used to create starting phase displacement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A, AUX or START

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Pulsating Magnetic Field

A field that alternates in magnitude and direction along a fixed spatial axis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φp

\*\*Unit\*\*: Magnetic Flux in weber (Wb)

\---

\## Rotating Magnetic Field

A Magnetic Field whose resultant axis rotates continuously around the Air Gap.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RMF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rotational speed in rpm

\---

\## Double-Revolving-Field Theory

The theory that resolves a pulsating field into equal forward and backward rotating fields.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DRF theory

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Forward Field

The rotating-field component moving in the selected Rotor direction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φf

\*\*Unit\*\*: weber (Wb)

\---

\## Backward Field

The rotating-field component moving opposite to the selected Rotor direction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φb

\*\*Unit\*\*: weber (Wb)

\---

\## Starting Torque

The electromagnetic torque developed at zero Rotor speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tst

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Forward Torque

Torque produced by interaction associated with the forward rotating field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tf

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Backward Torque

Torque produced by interaction associated with the backward rotating field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tb

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Accelerating Torque

The difference between Motor torque and opposing load torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tacc

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Load Torque

The opposing torque required by the driven equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TL

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Breakdown Torque

The maximum torque an induction Motor can develop without a sudden speed reduction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tmax or Tbd

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Synchronous Speed

The rotational speed of the fundamental rotating Magnetic Field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ns

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Slip

The normalized difference between Synchronous Speed and Rotor speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: s

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Phase Splitting

The production of phase-displaced winding Currents to create starting torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Phase angle in degree (°)

\---

\## Split-Phase Motor

A Single-Phase Motor using different main- and auxiliary-winding impedance angles for starting.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Resistance-Start Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Capacitor-Start Motor

A Motor using a large short-time Capacitor in the auxiliary circuit to obtain high starting torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CS Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Capacitor-Start Capacitor-Run Motor

A Motor using separate Capacitor functions for strong starting and improved running performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CSCR Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Permanent-Split-Capacitor Motor

A Motor whose run Capacitor and auxiliary winding remain connected during normal operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PSC Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Start Capacitor

A high-Capacitance, short-time-duty Capacitor used to increase starting phase shift and torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cstart

\*\*Unit\*\*: microfarad (µF)

\---

\## Run Capacitor

A continuous-duty Capacitor that improves running phase relation and performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Crun

\*\*Unit\*\*: microfarad (µF)

\---

\## Centrifugal Switch

A speed-operated switch that disconnects the start circuit after acceleration.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Operating speed in rpm or percent of rated speed

\---

\## Starting Relay

An electromagnetic or electronic device controlling the Motor starting circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: K or SR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Coil Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Shaded-Pole Motor

A small Motor using a short-circuited shading coil to produce weak directional starting torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SP Motor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W)

\---

\## Shading Coil

A short-circuited copper ring surrounding part of a magnetic pole to delay local Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Universal Motor

A series-commutator Motor designed to operate on either AC or DC.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: UM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Commutator

A segmented rotating conductor assembly that works with brushes to control armature Current direction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Brush

A stationary conductive contact transferring Current to or from a rotating Commutator.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current rating in ampere (A)

\---

\## Air Gap

The radial clearance between the Stator and Rotor magnetic surfaces.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: g

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m), commonly millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Frequency Control

A speed-control method that changes supply frequency and therefore Synchronous Speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f-control

\*\*Unit\*\*: hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Voltage Control

A speed-control method that varies applied Voltage and available Motor torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V-control

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Winding Tap

A selected connection point on a winding used to obtain a fixed operating condition or speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tap

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Duty Rating

The specified pattern and duration of Motor loading, running and rest.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S1, S2 and related duty codes

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time in second (s), minute (min) or duty percent (%)

\---

\## Locked Rotor

A condition in which the energized Motor Rotor cannot rotate.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Insulation Resistance

Resistance measured between insulated conductors and earth or other isolated windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω), commonly megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled procedure for isolating hazardous energy and preventing unauthorized re-energization.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Protective Earthing

Connection of exposed conductive parts to earth for fault protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Continuity or Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Nameplate

A permanent Motor label stating essential electrical, mechanical and duty ratings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Contains multiple rated units

\---
