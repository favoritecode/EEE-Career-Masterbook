\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main purpose of a Single-Phase Motor?

A. Convert mechanical energy into only DC energy
B. Convert Single-Phase electrical energy into mechanical energy
C. Convert Three-Phase energy into light
D. Store electrical charge permanently

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Convert Single-Phase electrical energy into mechanical energy\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A Single-Phase Motor receives Single-Phase electrical input and produces rotating mechanical output at its shaft.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A describes generation incorrectly, not Motor action.
\- C is neither the supply nor the output of this Motor.
\- D describes an energy-storage function, not a Motor.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Motor: electrical input → mechanical output.

\### MCQ-002

\*\*Question\*\*

A basic Single-Phase Induction Motor is not self-starting because it has:

A. Zero net starting torque at standstill
B. No Rotor conductors
C. Infinite Synchronous Speed
D. A DC Stator supply

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Zero net starting torque at standstill\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Equal forward and backward field components develop equal opposite torques at standstill, so their net starting torque is zero.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is false because a cage Rotor contains conducting bars.
\- C is false because Synchronous Speed is finite and depends on frequency and poles.
\- D is false because the Stator receives Single-Phase AC.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Equal opposite torques → zero starting torque.

\### MCQ-003

\*\*Question\*\*

According to double-revolving-field theory, a pulsating field is equivalent to:

A. Two equal fields rotating in opposite directions
B. One stationary DC field only
C. Three unequal fields rotating together
D. No Magnetic Field

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Two equal fields rotating in opposite directions\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

The pulsating field can be resolved into equal forward- and backward-rotating components.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B ignores the alternating nature of the field.
\- C incorrectly applies a Three-Phase description.
\- D is false because the energized winding produces Flux.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Pulsating field = forward field + backward field.

\### MCQ-014

\*\*Question\*\*

A 4-pole Single-Phase Induction Motor operates from 50 Hz. What is its Synchronous Speed?

A. 750 rpm
B. 1000 rpm
C. 1500 rpm
D. 3000 rpm

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*C. 1500 rpm\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Ns = 120f/P = (120 × 50)/4 = 1500 rpm.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A corresponds to 8 poles at 50 Hz.
\- B corresponds to 6 poles at 50 Hz.
\- D corresponds to 2 poles at 50 Hz.

\*\*Remember\*\*

At 50 Hz: 2 poles = 3000 rpm; 4 poles = 1500 rpm.

Accelerating torque = Motor torque − load torque.
