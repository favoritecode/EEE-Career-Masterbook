\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the principal function of Power Electronics?

A. Conversion and control of Electrical Power
B. Mechanical machining only
C. Chemical analysis only
D. Civil construction only

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Conversion and control of Electrical Power\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Power Electronics uses solid-state switching devices and control circuits to convert and regulate Voltage, Current, Frequency and Power Flow.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is a manufacturing activity, not the definition of Power Electronics.
\- C belongs to chemical measurement.
\- D belongs to civil engineering work.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Power Electronics combines Power, Electronics and Control.

\### MCQ-005

\*\*Question\*\*

After an SCR has latched ON, how is it normally turned OFF?

A. Reduce its Anode Current below the Holding Current
B. Apply the same Gate pulse continuously
C. Increase the Gate Current without limit
D. Disconnect only the Gate while maintaining large Anode Current

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Reduce its Anode Current below the Holding Current\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

The conventional SCR Gate turns the device ON but does not normally turn it OFF. Main Current must fall below Holding Current or be forced to zero.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is unnecessary after proper latching.
\- C can damage the Gate and does not provide normal turn-OFF.
\- D does not turn OFF a latched SCR while adequate Anode Current continues.

\*\*Remember\*\*

SCR is a latching device.

\### MCQ-016

\*\*Question\*\*

Why can switching conversion achieve high Efficiency?

A. A switch ideally has either very low Current or very low Voltage across it
B. The switch always dissipates maximum Power
C. Every converter operates without losses
D. Semiconductor temperature never changes

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. A switch ideally has either very low Current or very low Voltage across it\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Power loss is the product of device Voltage and Current. In ideal OFF and ON states, one of these quantities is zero, although real devices still have losses.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is opposite to efficient switching.
\- C is false because all practical converters have losses.
\- D is false because losses produce heat.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Real switches have conduction, switching and drive losses.

Why can excessive Voltage reduction by a Soft Starter prevent acceleration of a heavily loaded Induction Motor?

B. Motor pole count instantly becomes zero

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is physically false.
\- C does not result from normal Soft Starter action.
\- D is a fixed mechanical property for the connected system.

Soft Starter settings must match Load Torque requirements.

\### MCQ-028

\*\*Question\*\*

According to the idealized Affinity Laws, approximately what fraction of Power is required by a suitable centrifugal Load at 80% speed?

A. 0.512 or 51.2%
B. 0.800 or 80.0%
C. 0.640 or 64.0%
D. 1.250 or 125.0%

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. 0.512 or 51.2%\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

For applicable centrifugal Loads, Power is approximately proportional to the cube of speed: 0.8³ = 0.512. Actual system savings depend on operating conditions and losses.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B follows a linear rather than cubic relationship.
\- C is the square of 0.8 and relates approximately to pressure.
\- D represents an increase rather than the stated speed reduction.

\*\*Remember\*\*

For suitable fans and pumps: flow ∝ speed, pressure ∝ speed², Power ∝ speed³.
