\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Watch a beginner circuit-path animation.

3\. Observe a practical Series and Parallel circuit demonstration.

4\. Draw each circuit and mark its nodes, branches, Currents and Voltages.

5\. Practice equivalent Resistance and Series-Parallel calculations.

6\. Observe safe measurement and troubleshooting demonstrations.

7\. Solve the MCQs.

8\. Practice the Viva questions.

9\. Review the chapter summary.
