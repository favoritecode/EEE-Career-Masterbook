\# Chapter 04

\# Glossary

\## Electric Circuit

An interconnection of electrical components that provides one or more paths for electric current and transfers electrical energy from a source to a load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Source

A device that supplies electrical energy and establishes a Potential Difference in a circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Load

An electrical device or component that receives electrical energy and converts it into another useful form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Node

A point or continuous conducting region where two or more circuit terminals are electrically connected and have the same electrical potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Branch

A path between two nodes that contains one or more circuit elements and carries a branch Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Open Circuit

A circuit condition in which the conducting path is broken or incomplete, preventing continuous Current flow.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Closed Circuit

A circuit condition in which a complete conducting path allows Current to flow through the intended load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Short Circuit

An abnormal low-impedance connection between points of different potential that can produce very high Fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Fault Current

The Current that flows as a result of an electrical fault such as a Short Circuit or insulation failure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I fault

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Overload

A condition in which excessive Current flows through the normal load path because connected demand exceeds the intended circuit rating.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Series Circuit

A circuit in which components are connected end-to-end to provide only one path for electric current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Parallel Circuit

A circuit in which components are connected across the same two nodes, providing two or more Current paths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Series-Parallel Circuit

A circuit that contains both Series and Parallel connections in the same network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Combination Circuit

Another name for a circuit containing both Series and Parallel connections.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Equivalent Resistance

A single Resistance value that produces the same Voltage-Current relationship as a network of resistors viewed from specified terminals.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R eq or R total

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Total Current

The Current supplied to the complete circuit; in a Parallel Circuit it equals the sum of the branch Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I total

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Branch Current

The Current flowing through an individual branch of a Parallel or combination network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I₁, I₂, I₃, ...

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Supply Voltage

The Potential Difference provided by an electrical source to a circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V s or V total

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Voltage Drop

The Potential Difference across a circuit element caused by Energy transfer or conversion as Current flows through it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V₁, V₂, V₃, ...

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Voltage Divider

A Series resistor network that produces an output Voltage equal to a selected fraction of the applied Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Current Divider

A Parallel network in which total Current divides among branches according to their impedances or Resistances.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Conductance

The reciprocal of Resistance and a measure of how easily electric current can flow through a component or path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Siemens (S)

\## Switch

A control device that intentionally opens or closes a conducting path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S or SW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Fuse

An overcurrent protective device containing an element designed to melt and interrupt the circuit when Current exceeds its intended limit for a sufficient time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Ampere (A), Voltage (V) and Interrupting Capacity

\## Circuit Breaker

A resettable switching and protective device designed to interrupt specified abnormal Current conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB or QF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Ampere (A), Voltage (V) and Interrupting Capacity

\## Ammeter

An instrument used to measure electric Current and normally connected in series with the circuit under test.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Voltmeter

An instrument used to measure Potential Difference and connected in parallel across two points.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Ohmmeter

An instrument used to measure Electrical Resistance, normally on a de-energized circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ω

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Continuity

The presence of a complete conducting path with sufficiently low Resistance for the intended test or operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Continuity Test

A test used to determine whether a complete conducting path exists, normally performed on an isolated and de-energized circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Interrupting Capacity

The maximum prospective fault Current that a protective device can safely interrupt under specified conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A) or Kiloampere (kA)

\## Short-Circuit Current

The Current flowing through a circuit during a Short Circuit, limited in practice by the total system impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I sc

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A) or Kiloampere (kA)

\## Series Connection

A connection in which components share the same Current path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Parallel Connection

A connection in which components share the same two nodes and therefore the same Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable
