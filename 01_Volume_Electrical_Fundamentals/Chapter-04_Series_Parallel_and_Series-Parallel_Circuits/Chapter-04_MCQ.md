\# Basic Level

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is an Electric Circuit?

A. A material that stores only mechanical energy
B. An interconnection that provides a path for Current and transfers electrical energy
C. A device used only to measure Resistance
D. An open switch without a source or load

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. An interconnection that provides a path for Current and transfers electrical energy\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

An Electric Circuit connects a source, conducting path and load so that electrical energy can be transferred and useful work can be performed.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* An Electric Circuit transfers electrical energy, not only mechanical energy.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* A Resistance-measuring instrument is an Ohmmeter.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* An open switch alone does not form a complete Electric Circuit.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Circuit = Source + Path + Load + Control and Protection as Required

\### MCQ-003

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the Current in an ideal Open Circuit?

A. Infinite
B. Equal to the source Voltage
C. Zero
D. Always one Ampere

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*C. Zero\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

An Open Circuit has an incomplete conducting path. Therefore, continuous Current cannot flow and the ideal Current is zero.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* An ideal Short Circuit, not an Open Circuit, may imply extremely high Current.
❌ \*\*B.\*\* Current and Voltage are different quantities.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Open-Circuit Current is not fixed at one Ampere.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Open Path → I = 0 A

Zero Current Does Not Always Mean Zero Voltage

\### MCQ-009

\*\*Question\*\*

Which rule applies to Voltage in a Parallel Circuit?

❌ \*\*A.\*\* An energized parallel network has supply Voltage across its branches.
❌ \*\*B.\*\* Current divides among branches; branch Voltage remains common.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Every connected branch receives the common Voltage.

Parallel → Same Voltage

\### MCQ-014

\*\*Question\*\*

A 3 Ω resistor and a 6 Ω resistor are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent Resistance?

A. 2 Ω
B. 3 Ω
C. 9 Ω
D. 18 Ω

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. 2 Ω\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

For two parallel resistors, R total = R₁R₂/(R₁ + R₂) = (3 × 6)/(3 + 6) = 18/9 = 2 Ω.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*B.\*\* Equivalent Resistance must be lower than 3 Ω.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* This is the Series Resistance.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* This is only the product of the Resistances.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Two Parallel Resistors: R total = R₁R₂/(R₁ + R₂)
