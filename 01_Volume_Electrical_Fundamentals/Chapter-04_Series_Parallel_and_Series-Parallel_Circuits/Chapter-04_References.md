\# Reference Notes

\- Series components share the same Current only when their common node has no additional branch.

\- Parallel components share the same Voltage only when both terminals connect to the same two nodes.

\- Equivalent Resistance in a Parallel Circuit is lower than the smallest branch Resistance.

\- A Short Circuit and an Overload are different abnormal conditions and may require coordinated protection.

\- Continuity and Resistance measurements should normally be performed on an isolated and verified de-energized circuit.

\- Actual installation design, wiring, protection and verification must follow applicable national regulations, local wiring rules and current standards.

\- Manufacturer instructions and equipment ratings must be followed during connection, testing and maintenance.
