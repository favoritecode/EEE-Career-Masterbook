\# Chapter 16

\# Glossary

\## DC Generators and DC Motors

\---

\## DC Machine

A reversible rotating device that converts energy between DC electrical and mechanical forms.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: M or G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Watt (W) or volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## DC Generator

A DC Machine operating from mechanical input to produce DC electrical output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Watt (W) or volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## DC Motor

A DC Machine that converts DC electrical input into rotating mechanical output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: M

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Watt (W)

\---

\## Electromagnetic Induction

The production of EMF when Flux Linkage changes or a conductor cuts Magnetic Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: e or E

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Generated EMF

The average internal EMF induced in a rotating Generator Armature.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Eg

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Back EMF

The Motor Armature EMF that opposes the applied supply Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Eb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Yoke

The outer frame that supports the Poles and provides part of the magnetic return path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Pole Core

The magnetic structure that supports a Field Winding and carries main Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Pole Shoe

The widened pole face that spreads Flux across the Air Gap.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Field Winding

A winding carrying excitation Current to produce the main Magnetic Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Armature

The machine part in which EMF is induced and Armature Current flows; it normally rotates in a conventional DC Machine.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Armature Core

A laminated magnetic core containing slots for the Armature Winding.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Armature Winding

The interconnected conductors in which EMF is induced and electromechanical conversion occurs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Armature Current

The Current flowing through the Armature Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ia

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Armature Resistance

The effective electrical Resistance of the Armature Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ra

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Commutator

A segmented mechanical switch that rectifies Generator output or reverses Motor coil Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Commutation

The reversal of Current in an Armature coil as it passes through the Brush zone.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Brush

A stationary carbon or graphite contact transferring Current to or from the rotating Commutator.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: B or +/− terminal marking

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current rating in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Brush Drop

The Voltage drop across Brush-to-Commutator contacts.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Interpole

A small auxiliary pole used to improve commutation by counteracting reactance Voltage in the commutating zone.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Compensating Winding

A Pole-Shoe winding used to oppose Armature Reaction under load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Armature Reaction

The effect of Armature-produced Magnetic Flux on the main field distribution.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Magnetomotive force in ampere-turn (At)

\---

\## Magnetic Flux per Pole

The total useful Magnetic Flux associated with one machine pole.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Weber (Wb)

\---

\## Parallel Paths

The number of electrically parallel Current paths in an Armature Winding.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless count

\---

\## Lap Winding

An Armature Winding whose simplex form has A = P and suits high-Current, lower-Voltage duty.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Lap; A = P

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Wave Winding

An Armature Winding whose simplex form has two parallel paths and suits higher-Voltage duty.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Wave; A = 2

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Separately Excited Machine

A DC Machine whose Field Winding receives Current from an independent DC source.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Self-Excited Generator

A Generator that obtains its Field Current from its own generated output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Shunt Machine

A DC Machine whose Shunt Field is connected in parallel with the Armature terminals or supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Sh

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Series Machine

A DC Machine whose Series Field carries Armature or load Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Se

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Compound Machine

A DC Machine containing both Shunt and Series Field Windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Comp

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cumulative Compounding

A Compound connection in which Series-Field Flux aids Shunt-Field Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φtotal = Φsh + Φse

\*\*Unit\*\*: Flux in Weber (Wb)

\---

\## Differential Compounding

A Compound connection in which Series-Field Flux opposes Shunt-Field Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φtotal = Φsh − Φse

\*\*Unit\*\*: Flux in Weber (Wb)

\---

\## Residual Magnetism

The small Magnetic Flux remaining in pole material after excitation is removed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φr

\*\*Unit\*\*: Weber (Wb)

\---

\## Critical Field Resistance

The maximum Shunt-Field Circuit Resistance permitting Voltage build-up at a specified Generator speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rc

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Electromagnetic Torque

The internally developed turning effect caused by interaction of field Flux and Armature Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: T or Te

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Starting Current

The Armature Current drawn at standstill when Back EMF is zero.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Istart

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## DC Motor Starter

A device or control system that limits starting Current and may provide protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Starter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Volt (V) and Ampere (A)

\---

\## Base Speed

The rated speed at the transition between Armature-Voltage control and Field-Weakening operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Nb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Revolution per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Armature-Voltage Control

A speed-control method that varies Armature supply Voltage at approximately constant Flux.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Va control

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Field Weakening

A speed-control method that reduces Field Flux to obtain speed above base speed with reduced torque capability.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Φ reduction

\*\*Unit\*\*: Flux in Weber (Wb)

\---

\## Armature-Resistance Control

A speed-control method using added series Resistance to reduce effective Armature Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rext

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Speed Regulation

The percentage change between no-load and full-load speed relative to full-load speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Developed Power

The Motor Power converted electromagnetically before rotational losses.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pdeveloped = EbIa

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Copper Loss

The I²R loss produced in Armature and Field Windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcu

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Iron Loss

The combined Hysteresis and Eddy-Current Loss in magnetic cores.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pfe

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Mechanical Loss

Power loss caused mainly by bearing and Brush friction and air windage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pmech-loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Efficiency

The ratio of useful output Power to input Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: η

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%) or dimensionless

\---

\## PMDC Motor

A DC Motor whose main field is produced by permanent magnets.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PMDC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Volt (V) and Watt (W)

\---

\## BLDC Motor

A permanent-magnet Motor supplied from DC through an electronic commutation controller.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BLDC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in Volt (V) and Watt (W)

\---

\## Brush Sparking

Visible arcing at the Brush–Commutator interface caused by commutation, surface, loading or winding problems.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Neutral Plane

The Armature position where the induced coil EMF is ideally zero during commutation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MNA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Mechanical or electrical degree

\---

\## Insulation Resistance

The Resistance offered by insulation between conductors and Earth or other windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω), commonly megaohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Preventive Maintenance

Planned inspection, testing, cleaning and servicing performed before functional failure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Regenerative Operation

Operation in which a driven Motor acts as a Generator and returns electrical energy to a suitable system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Regeneration

\*\*Unit\*\*: Returned Power in Watt (W)

\---

\## End of Chapter 16 Glossary
