\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter and compare Generator and Motor energy flow.

2\. Review Faraday's Law and electromagnetic force.

3\. Learn the constructional parts and their functions.

4\. Practice the Generator EMF Equation.

5\. Compare Generator excitation types.

6\. Learn Motor torque and Back EMF relationships.

7\. Compare Shunt, Series and Compound Motors.

8\. Practice Back-EMF and converted-Power calculations.

9\. Study the three basic speed-control methods.

10\. Interpret torque–Current and speed–torque curves.

11\. Match Generator and Motor types to applications.

12\. Review Brush, Commutator and bearing maintenance.

13\. Study starting, field-loss and overspeed safety.

14\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

15\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-17 Three-Phase Induction Motors.
