\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

A DC Generator converts:

A. Electrical energy into mechanical energy
B. Mechanical energy into DC electrical energy
C. AC into heat only
D. Chemical energy directly into rotation

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Mechanical energy into DC electrical energy\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A Prime Mover rotates the Armature, and Electromagnetic Induction produces electrical output.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A describes Motor action.
\- C is not the purpose of a Generator.
\- D does not describe direct DC Generator conversion.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Generator: Mechanical input → Electrical output
