\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review sinusoidal waveforms and Phasors.

3\. Draw three equal Phasors separated by 120°.

4\. Study how an alternator generates Three-Phase Voltage.

5\. Learn positive and negative Phase Sequence.

6\. Draw and label a Star connection.

7\. Draw and label a Delta connection.

8\. Memorize Star Voltage and Current relationships.

9\. Memorize Delta Voltage and Current relationships.

10\. Practice Line-to-Phase conversion examples.

11\. Practice balanced P, Q and S calculations.

12\. Study Neutral Current, unbalance and load balancing.

13\. Review motor rotation, Star-Delta starting and safety.

14\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

15\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-15 Transformers.
