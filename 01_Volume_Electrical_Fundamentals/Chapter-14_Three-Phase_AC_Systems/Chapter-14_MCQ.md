\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

In a balanced Three-Phase system, the Phase Voltages are separated by:

A. 30°
B. 60°
C. 90°
D. 120°

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*D. 120°\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Three equal sinusoidal Phase Voltages complete one 360° cycle at equal angular intervals. Therefore their displacement is 360°/3 = 120°.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A is associated with some Line-to-Phase Phasor relationships, not Phase separation.
\- B is not the separation of a balanced three-Phase set.
\- C is the ideal Phase difference between Voltage and Current in a pure Inductor or Capacitor.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Three balanced Phases → 120° apart

\*\*Question\*\*

Which condition describes a balanced Three-Phase source?

A. Equal magnitudes, equal Frequency and 120° separation
B. Different magnitudes and equal angles

D. One Phase Voltage is always zero

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B describes an unequal or incorrectly displaced set.
\- C cannot maintain a fixed Three-Phase relationship.
\- D represents a fault or missing Phase, not normal balanced operation.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Balanced source = Equal magnitude + Same f + 120°
