\# Chapter 17

\# Glossary

\## Three-Phase Induction Motors

\---

\## Three-Phase Induction Motor

An AC Motor whose Stator RMF induces Rotor Current and develops torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in W or kW

\---

\## Asynchronous Motor

A Motor whose Rotor normally runs at a speed different from Synchronous Speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ASM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated speed in rpm

\---

\## Stator

The stationary laminated core and winding assembly connected to the supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Rotor

The rotating assembly in which Current is induced and torque is developed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Air Gap

The radial clearance between Stator and Rotor magnetic surfaces.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: g

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m), commonly millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Stator Winding

The distributed Three-Phase winding that produces the RMF.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A/B/C or U/V/W

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Squirrel-Cage Rotor

A Rotor with conductive bars short-circuited by End Rings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cage Rotor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Rotor Bar

A conductive bar carrying induced Rotor Current in a Cage Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## End of Chapter 17 Glossary

\## Skew

The angular displacement of Rotor bars or slots used to reduce locking, noise and pulsation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Skew angle

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°)

\---

\## Slip-Ring Rotor

A wound Rotor whose terminals connect through Slip Rings and Brushes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Wound Rotor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Slip Ring

A continuous conducting ring providing electrical access to a rotating Rotor winding.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Rotor Resistance

The effective Resistance of one Rotor Phase or equivalent Rotor Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R2

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## External Rotor Resistance

Added Resistance used with a Slip-Ring Rotor for starting or speed control.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rext

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Rotating Magnetic Field

The rotating resultant Flux produced by balanced Three-Phase Stator Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RMF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Flux Density in Tesla (T)

\---

\## Synchronous Speed

The rotational speed of the Stator RMF.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ns

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolution per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Pole Number

The number of magnetic poles established by the Stator winding.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless count

\---

\## Slip

The per-unit difference between Synchronous and Rotor speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: s

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Percentage Slip

Slip expressed as a percentage of Synchronous Speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: s%

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Rotor Speed

The mechanical rotational speed of the Rotor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N or Nr

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolution per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Rotor Frequency

The Frequency of induced Rotor EMF and Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fr=sf

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Standstill

The zero-speed condition at which Slip equals one.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N=0, s=1

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Air-Gap Power

The electromagnetic Power transferred from Stator to Rotor across the Air Gap.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pg

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Rotor Copper Loss

The I²R loss in the Rotor, equal to sPg.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Prcl

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Mechanical Developed Power

Electromagnetically converted Rotor mechanical Power before rotational losses.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pm=(1−s)Pg

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Shaft Output Power

Useful mechanical Power available at the Motor Shaft.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pout

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Electromagnetic Torque

The internally developed turning effect on the Rotor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Te

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Starting Torque

Motor torque developed at standstill.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tst

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Breakdown Torque

The maximum torque developed without abrupt speed collapse.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tmax

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Pull-Out Torque

Another name for Breakdown Torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tpo

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Rated Torque

The Shaft torque corresponding to rated output and speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tr

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Torque-Speed Characteristic

A curve showing developed torque as a function of Rotor speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: T–N curve

\*\*Unit\*\*: N·m versus rpm

\---

\## Direct-On-Line Starter

A starter that applies full Line Voltage directly to the Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DOL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in V and A

\---

\## Star-Delta Starter

A starter that begins in Star and changes suitable windings to Delta.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Y-Δ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in V, A and kW

\---

\## Autotransformer Starter

A reduced-Voltage starter using Autotransformer taps.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ATS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in V, A and kVA

\---

\## Soft Starter

A power-electronic device that controls Motor Voltage during starting and stopping.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in V and A

\---

\## Variable-Frequency Drive

A converter that controls Motor Voltage and Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VFD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in V, A and kW

\---

\## V/f Control

A method maintaining an approximate Voltage-to-Frequency ratio below base speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V/f

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt per Hertz (V/Hz)

\---

\## Base Frequency

The rated transition Frequency beyond which Voltage normally cannot increase further.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Field-Weakening Region

Operation above base speed where Air-Gap Flux and constant-torque capability reduce.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Speed in rpm or Frequency in Hz

\---

\## Pole Changing

Discrete speed control by changing the effective Stator pole number.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P change

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Stator-Voltage Control

Speed or torque control by changing applied Stator Voltage at fixed Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vs control

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Slip-Power Recovery

A wound-Rotor method that recovers or controls Rotor Slip Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SPR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Stator Copper Loss

I²R loss in the Stator windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Psc

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Core Loss

Combined Hysteresis and Eddy-Current Loss in magnetic cores.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcore

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Mechanical Loss

Bearing-friction, windage and fan Power loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pmech-loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Stray-Load Loss

Additional load-dependent loss not included in basic calculated components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pstray

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Efficiency

Useful Shaft output divided by electrical input.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: η

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Power Factor

Real input Power divided by apparent input Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF or cosφ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Single Phasing

Abnormal operation caused by loss of one Phase of a Three-Phase supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Phase loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Voltage Unbalance

Unequal Three-Phase Voltage magnitudes or angular relationships.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: %VU

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Current Unbalance

Unequal Motor Line Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: %IU

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Locked Rotor

A condition in which the Rotor cannot turn while supply is applied.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Soft Foot

A mounting condition where one or more Motor feet do not sit flat on the base.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not fixed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Gap in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Insulation Resistance

Resistance of winding insulation to Earth or other windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω), commonly MΩ

\---

\## Ingress Protection Rating

A code describing enclosure protection against solid objects and water.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Code; no physical unit

\---

\## Duty Cycle

The specified load and rest pattern for which a Motor is rated.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S1, S2, etc.

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time or percent as applicable

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A procedure for isolating and securing hazardous energy sources.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Condition Monitoring

Measurement and trending of vibration, temperature, Current and other health indicators.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on measured quantity

\---

\## Bearing Vibration

Oscillatory motion associated with bearings, Shaft, alignment or electromagnetic forces.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: v or a

\*\*Unit\*\*: mm/s or m/s²

\---

\## End of Chapter 17 Glossary
