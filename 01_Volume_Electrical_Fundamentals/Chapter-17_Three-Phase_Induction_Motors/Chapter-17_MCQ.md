\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What creates the Rotating Magnetic Field in a Three-Phase Induction Motor?

A. Balanced Three-Phase Stator Currents
B. DC Rotor supply only
C. Bearing friction
D. A mechanical Commutator

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Balanced Three-Phase Stator Currents\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Three displaced balanced Stator Currents combine into a rotating resultant field.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B does not match the stated principle or operating condition.
\- C does not match the stated principle or operating condition.
\- D does not match the stated principle or operating condition.

\*\*Remember\*\*

RMF requires balanced spatial and time displacement.
