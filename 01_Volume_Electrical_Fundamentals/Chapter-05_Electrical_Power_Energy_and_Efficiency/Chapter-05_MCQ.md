\# Basic Level

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What does Electrical Power represent?

A. Total electric charge stored
B. Rate of electrical energy transfer
C. Opposition to electric current
D. Time required to use energy

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Rate of electrical energy transfer\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Electrical Power is the rate at which electrical energy is transferred, converted or consumed. It is expressed as P = E/t.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* Stored charge is measured in Coulomb, not Power.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Opposition to current is Resistance.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Time affects Energy use but does not define Power.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Power = Energy per Unit Time

\### MCQ-003

\*\*Question\*\*

Which formula gives DC Electrical Power?

B. P = V/I
C. P = IR

\*\*Answer\*\*

❌ \*\*C.\*\* IR gives Voltage according to Ohm's Law.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Q/t gives Electric Current.

\*\*Remember\*\*

P = V × I
