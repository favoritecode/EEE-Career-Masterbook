\# Chapter 05

\# Glossary

\## Electrical Power

The rate at which electrical energy is transferred, converted or consumed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Watt

The SI unit of Power. One Watt equals one Joule of Energy transferred per second.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: W

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule per Second (J/s)

\## Kilowatt

A unit of Power equal to one thousand Watts.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: kW

\*\*Unit\*\*: 1 kW = 1000 W

\## Megawatt

A unit of Power equal to one million Watts or one thousand kilowatts.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MW

\*\*Unit\*\*: 1 MW = 10⁶ W

\## Electrical Energy

The total electrical work done or Energy transferred, converted or consumed during a period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E or W

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J)

\## Joule

The SI unit of Energy. One Joule equals the Energy transferred by a Power of one Watt in one second.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: J

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt-second (W·s)

\## Watt-hour

The Energy transferred or consumed by a Power of one Watt operating for one hour.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Wh

\*\*Unit\*\*: 1 Wh = 3600 J

\## Kilowatt-hour

The Energy transferred or consumed by a Power of one kilowatt operating for one hour. It is commonly used for electricity billing.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: kWh

\*\*Unit\*\*: 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J

\## Unit of Electricity

A common billing expression for one kilowatt-hour of electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: 1 Unit = 1 kWh

\## Power Rating

The specified Power that electrical equipment consumes, delivers or can safely handle under stated operating conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P rated

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W) or Kilowatt (kW)

\## Input Power

The total Power supplied to a device or energy-conversion system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P in

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Output Power

The useful Power delivered by a device or energy-conversion system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P out

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Power Loss

The difference between input Power and useful output Power, representing the rate of unwanted Energy conversion.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Energy Loss

The Energy converted into unwanted forms such as heat, sound or friction during operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J), Watt-hour (Wh) or Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

\## Efficiency

The ratio of useful output Power or Energy to total input Power or Energy, normally expressed as a percentage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: η

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or Percent (%)

\## Energy Consumption

The amount of electrical Energy used by equipment during a specified operating period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J), Watt-hour (Wh) or Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

\## Energy Meter

An instrument that measures the cumulative electrical Energy consumed by an installation or load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

\## Wattmeter

An instrument used to measure Electrical Power in a circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Electricity Tariff

The pricing structure used by an electricity supplier to calculate charges for electrical Energy and related services.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per Kilowatt-hour or Applicable Tariff Unit

\## Load

Any electrical device or system that receives electrical Energy and converts it into another useful form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Connected Load

The sum of the rated Power of all electrical equipment connected to an installation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P connected

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W) or Kilowatt (kW)

\## Maximum Demand

The highest average Power demand recorded or expected during a specified time interval.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P max

\*\*Unit\*\*: Kilowatt (kW) or Applicable Demand Unit

\## Standby Power

The Electrical Power consumed by equipment while it is not performing its main function but remains connected and ready to operate.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P standby

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Backup Time

The duration for which a stored-energy or backup system can supply a specified electrical load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: t backup

\*\*Unit\*\*: Second (s) or Hour (h)
