\# Reference Notes

\- Use Watt (W) as the SI unit of Electrical Power.

\- Use Joule (J) as the SI unit of Energy and kilowatt-hour (kWh) for practical Energy consumption and billing calculations.

\- One kilowatt-hour equals 3.6 × 10⁶ Joules.

\- Efficiency is dimensionless and is normally expressed as a percentage.

\- Electricity tariffs, taxes and billing rules vary by supplier and location; use the current official tariff when calculating an actual bill.

\- Manufacturer data should be consulted for equipment Power ratings, Efficiency values, operating limits and safety requirements.
