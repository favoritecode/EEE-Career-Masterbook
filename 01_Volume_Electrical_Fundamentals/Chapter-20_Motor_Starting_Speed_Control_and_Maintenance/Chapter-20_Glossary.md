\# Chapter 20

\# Glossary

\## Motor Starting, Speed Control and Maintenance

\---

\## Motor Starter

A switching and control arrangement used to start, stop and help protect a Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Starter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Starting Current

The Current drawn by a Motor while starting from standstill.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ist

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Starting Torque

The electromagnetic torque developed at zero speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tst

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Accelerating Torque

The difference between developed Motor torque and opposing load torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tacc

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Load Torque

The torque required by the driven equipment at a stated speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TL

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton-metre (N·m)

\---

\## Rotational Inertia

The resistance of a rotating mass to change in angular speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: J

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilogram-square metre (kg·m²)

\---

\## Acceleration Time

The time required for a Motor-load system to reach its target speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ta

\*\*Unit\*\*: second (s)

\---

\## Back EMF

The Armature EMF in a running DC Motor that opposes the applied Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Eb

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Starting Resistance

External Resistance inserted temporarily to limit starting Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rstart

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Three-Point Starter

A traditional DC-Motor starter using line, Armature and field terminals.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 3P Starter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Four-Point Starter

A DC-Motor starter whose no-Voltage hold coil is independent of the Shunt-field circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 4P Starter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Direct-On-Line Starter

A starter that applies full line Voltage directly to a Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DOL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Star-Delta Starter

A reduced-Voltage starter that connects suitable windings in Star for starting and Delta for running.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Y-Δ Starter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Autotransformer Starter

A starter that applies selectable reduced Motor Voltage through an Autotransformer.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ATS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Rotor-Resistance Starter

A starter that inserts external Resistance into a wound-Rotor Induction Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RRS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Slip-Ring Motor

A wound-Rotor Induction Motor whose Rotor terminals are accessible through Slip Rings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: WRIM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Split-Phase Starting

A Single-Phase starting method using different winding impedance angles to create phase displacement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Resistance Start

\*\*Unit\*\*: Phase angle in degree (°)

\---

\## Start Capacitor

A short-time-duty Capacitor used to improve phase displacement and starting torque.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cstart

\*\*Unit\*\*: microfarad (µF)

\---

\## Run Capacitor

A continuous-duty Capacitor used to improve Single-Phase Motor running performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Crun

\*\*Unit\*\*: microfarad (µF)

\---

\## Armature-Voltage Control

A DC-Motor speed method that varies Armature terminal Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Va control

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Field Weakening

Reduction of Motor field Flux to obtain speed above base speed with reduced torque capability.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Field Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Base Speed

The rated transition speed between constant-torque and field-weakening regions of a drive.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Nb

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Synchronous Speed

The rotating-field speed determined by frequency and pole number.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ns

\*\*Unit\*\*: revolutions per minute (rpm)

\---

\## Slip

The normalized difference between Synchronous Speed and Induction-Motor Rotor speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: s

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Pole Changing

A speed-control method that changes the effective number of Motor poles.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P-change

\*\*Unit\*\*: Pole count

\---

\## Constant V/f Control

An AC-drive method maintaining an approximately constant Voltage-to-frequency ratio below base speed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V/f

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt per hertz (V/Hz)

\---

\## Soft Starter

A solid-state controller that ramps Motor RMS Voltage during start and often stop.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Thyristor

A controllable semiconductor switch used in many Soft Starters and Power converters.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SCR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Voltage Ramp

A programmed gradual change of applied RMS Voltage during starting or stopping.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vramp

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt per second (V/s) or time in second (s)

\---

\## Current Limit

A controller setting that restricts Motor Current during acceleration or operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ilim

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or percent (%)

\---

\## Bypass Contactor

A contactor that carries Motor Current after a Soft Starter completes its ramp.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: KB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A)

\---

\## Variable Frequency Drive

A Power-Electronic drive supplying controlled Voltage and frequency to an AC Motor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VFD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W), volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Rectifier

A converter stage changing AC input into DC.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: REC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## DC Link

The intermediate DC energy-storage and filtering section of a VFD.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vdc

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Inverter

A Power-Electronic stage converting DC into controlled AC output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: INV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Pulse-Width Modulation

A switching method controlling effective output Voltage and waveform through pulse widths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PWM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Switching frequency in hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Braking Resistor

A resistor that dissipates VFD DC-Link braking energy as heat through a braking chopper.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rbr

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω) and watt (W)

\---

\## Routine Maintenance

Regular inspection, cleaning, measurement and minor servicing of equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Interval in hour (h), day or month

\---

\## Preventive Maintenance

Maintenance performed at planned time, operating-hour or start-count intervals.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Scheduled interval

\---

\## Predictive Maintenance

Maintenance planned from measured condition and trend information.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PdM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Corrective Maintenance

Repair or restoration performed after a defect or failure is identified.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Condition Monitoring

Repeated observation and measurement used to identify developing equipment changes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CMON

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on measured condition

\---

\## Vibration Analysis

Measurement and interpretation of machine vibration to assess mechanical condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VA

\*\*Unit\*\*: millimetre per second (mm/s), metre per second squared (m/s²) or displacement

\---

\## Infrared Thermography

Non-contact imaging of surface-temperature patterns for condition assessment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR

\*\*Unit\*\*: degree Celsius (°C)

\---

\## Motor Current Signature Analysis

Analysis of Motor Current frequency components to identify selected electrical and mechanical conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCSA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A), frequency in hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Insulation Resistance

Resistance measured between insulated conductors and earth or other isolated windings.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω), commonly megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Voltage Unbalance

The unequal magnitude or phase condition of Three-Phase supply Voltages.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VU

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%)

\---

\## Phase Failure

Loss of one phase in a Three-Phase supply or circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Phase Loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Overload

Operation requiring Current or torque above the permitted continuous condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A) or percent (%)

\---

\## Fuse

A sacrificial overcurrent device whose element melts according to its time-Current characteristic.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A) and breaking capacity

\---

\## Miniature Circuit Breaker

A resettable thermal-magnetic overcurrent protective device for lower-current circuits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A)

\---

\## Moulded Case Circuit Breaker

A circuit breaker for higher Current ratings, often with adjustable protection functions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A) and interrupting capacity

\---

\## Thermal Overload Relay

A device that responds to sustained Motor overcurrent and opens the control circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OLR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current setting in ampere (A)

\---

\## Residual Current Circuit Breaker

A device that trips when residual Current imbalance exceeds its threshold.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Residual operating Current in milliampere (mA) or ampere (A)

\---

\## Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker

A term historically associated with Voltage-operated earth-leakage protection and sometimes used broadly for leakage devices.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ELCB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on device principle

\---

\## Residual Current Breaker with Overcurrent

A device combining residual-current and overcurrent protective functions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCBO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A) and residual Current (mA)

\---

\## Breaking Capacity

The maximum prospective fault Current a protective device can interrupt safely under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Icn, Icu or related standard symbol

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Trip Class

A standardized overload-relay operating-time classification related to Motor starting duty.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Class 10, 20, 30 or applicable designation

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled process for isolating hazardous energy and preventing unauthorized re-energization.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Root-Cause Analysis

A structured investigation that identifies the fundamental causes of a fault or failure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---
