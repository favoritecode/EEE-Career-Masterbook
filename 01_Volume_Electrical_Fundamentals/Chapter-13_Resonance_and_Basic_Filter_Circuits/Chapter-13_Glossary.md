\# Chapter 13

\# Glossary

\## Resonance

The AC Circuit condition in which Inductive and Capacitive Reactances are equal in magnitude and their net reactive effects cancel.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL = XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Resonant Circuit

An electrical Circuit containing Inductance and Capacitance arranged to produce a tuned response around a Resonant Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RLC or LC Circuit

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Resonant Frequency

The Frequency at which Inductive and Capacitive Reactances are equal in the ideal basic RLC model.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f₀ = 1/(2π√LC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Angular Resonant Frequency

The angular Frequency corresponding to Resonant Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ω₀ = 2πf₀ = 1/√LC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Radian per Second (rad/s)

\## Series Resonance

The condition in a Series RLC Circuit where net Reactance is zero, input Impedance minimum and supply Current maximum.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL = XC; Z = R

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Parallel Resonance

The condition in a Parallel RLC or LC network where reactive input Current cancels and input Impedance becomes maximum under the ideal tuned condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BL + BC = 0

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Acceptor Circuit

A common name for a Series Resonant Circuit because it offers low Impedance to its tuned Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Rejector Circuit

A common name for a Parallel Resonant Circuit because it offers high input Impedance around its tuned Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Tank Circuit

A Parallel LC Circuit in which energy is exchanged between Capacitor Electric Field and Inductor Magnetic Field.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LC Tank

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Inductive Reactance

The Frequency-dependent opposition of an ideal Inductor to sinusoidal Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL = 2πfL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Capacitive Reactance

The Frequency-dependent opposition of an ideal Capacitor to sinusoidal Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XC = 1/(2πfC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Net Reactance

The algebraic combination of Inductive and Capacitive Reactances in a Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X = XL − XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Reactive Cancellation

The condition in which equal and opposite Inductive and Capacitive Phasor effects produce zero net Reactance or Susceptance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL = XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Voltage Magnification

The production of Inductor or Capacitor Voltage greater than supply Voltage in a Series Resonant Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VL/V or VC/V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless Ratio

\## Current Magnification

The circulation of Inductive and Capacitive branch Currents greater than supply Current in a Parallel Resonant Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IL/Is or IC/Is

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless Ratio

\## Quality Factor

A dimensionless measure of Resonance sharpness and relative energy loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Series Quality Factor

The Quality Factor of a simple Series RLC Circuit at Resonance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q = ω₀L/R = 1/(ω₀CR)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Loaded Quality Factor

The effective Quality Factor of a Resonant Circuit after source, load and other external losses are included.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: QL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Unloaded Quality Factor

The Quality Factor before external loading, determined mainly by internal component and Circuit losses.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q₀ or QU

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Bandwidth

The Frequency interval between upper and lower cutoff Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BW = fH − fL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Lower Cutoff Frequency

The lower Frequency boundary of a defined Passband or Resonant response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Upper Cutoff Frequency

The upper Frequency boundary of a defined Passband or Resonant response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fH

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Half-Power Frequency

A Frequency at which output Power equals half its reference maximum for the specified response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fL or fH

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Half-Power Point

A response point corresponding to half the reference Power and approximately 0.707 of reference amplitude under the same Resistive condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: −3 dB Point

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB) or Dimensionless Ratio

\## Decibel

A logarithmic unit used to express Power or amplitude ratios.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: dB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB)

\## Selectivity

The ability of a Circuit or Filter to distinguish desired Frequencies from nearby unwanted Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Frequency Response

The variation of a Circuit's magnitude and phase response with Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H(jω)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Usually Dimensionless Gain and Degree or Radian

\## Response Curve

A graph of a Circuit output, gain, Current, Impedance or other quantity as a function of Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Plotted Quantity

\## Filter

A Frequency-selective Circuit that passes desired Frequencies and attenuates unwanted Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H(jω)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Passive Filter

A Filter formed using passive elements such as Resistors, Inductors and Capacitors without an active gain device.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RLC or RC Filter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Active Filter

A Filter using an active device, commonly an operational amplifier, together with passive components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Active RC Filter

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Passband

The Frequency range transmitted by a Filter with acceptable attenuation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Stopband

The Frequency range intentionally attenuated by a Filter.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Transition Band

The Frequency region between a Passband and Stopband where Filter response changes significantly.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Cutoff Frequency

The Frequency marking a specified boundary of Filter response, commonly the −3 dB point for a simple first-order design.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fc

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Center Frequency

The central Frequency of a standard Band Pass, Band Stop or Resonant response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f₀ = √(fLfH)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Filter Order

A number associated with the count of independent energy-storage sections and the asymptotic attenuation slope of a Filter.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: n

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless Count

\## First-Order Filter

A Filter with one independent energy-storage section and an asymptotic slope of approximately 20 dB per decade.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: n = 1

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Attenuation

The reduction of signal magnitude or Power as it passes through a Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A or α as Context Requires

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB) or Dimensionless Ratio

\## Gain

The ratio of output quantity to input quantity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A or H

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or Decibel (dB)

\## Transfer Function

The Complex ratio of output to input as a function of Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H(jω) = Output/Input

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Input and Output Quantities

\## Low Pass Filter

A Filter that passes lower Frequencies and attenuates higher Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LPF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## RC Low Pass Filter

A first-order passive Low Pass Filter using a Series resistor and shunt Capacitor, with output normally taken across the Capacitor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fc = 1/(2πRC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz) for fc

\## High Pass Filter

A Filter that passes higher Frequencies and attenuates lower Frequencies.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HPF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## RC High Pass Filter

A first-order passive High Pass Filter using a Series Capacitor and resistor, with output normally taken across the resistor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fc = 1/(2πRC)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz) for fc

\## Band Pass Filter

A Filter that passes a selected middle Frequency band and attenuates Frequencies below and above it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BPF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Band Stop Filter

A Filter that attenuates a selected middle Frequency band and passes Frequencies below and above it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BSF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Band Reject Filter

Another name for a Band Stop Filter.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BRF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Notch Filter

A narrow Band Stop Filter designed to strongly reject a small Frequency region.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Notch

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Notch Frequency

The Frequency at which a Notch Filter produces maximum rejection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fn

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Notch Depth

The amount of attenuation produced at the Notch Frequency relative to a stated reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Anotch

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB)

\## Twin-T Network

An RC network formed from complementary T sections and commonly used to produce a Notch response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Twin-T

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Source Impedance

The equivalent Impedance of the signal or power source seen by the connected Filter.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Zs

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Load Impedance

The Impedance connected to the output of a Filter or Resonant network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ZL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Loading Effect

The change in gain, cutoff, Q or Bandwidth caused when a source or load interacts with a Filter or Resonant Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Buffer

An active stage used to reduce interaction between connected Circuit sections by providing high input and low output Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Voltage Follower or Buffer Symbol

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Component Tolerance

The permitted variation of a component's actual value from its nominal value.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ±%

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\## Parasitic Element

An unintended Resistance, Capacitance or Inductance that affects practical Frequency response.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Rp, Cp or Lp

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ω, F or H as Applicable

\## Frequency Sweep

A test in which input Frequency is varied across a range while Circuit response is measured.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: fmin to fmax

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Harmonic Filter

A Filter designed to attenuate one or more harmonic Frequencies in a power or signal system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Resonant Converter

A power-electronic converter that uses controlled Resonant Circuit behavior for energy transfer and switching performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LLC, Series or Parallel Resonant Topology

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable
