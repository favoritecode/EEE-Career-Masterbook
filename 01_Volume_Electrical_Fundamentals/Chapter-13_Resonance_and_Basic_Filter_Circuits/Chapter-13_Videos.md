\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review Inductive and Capacitive Reactance.

3\. Understand the condition X_L = X_C.

4\. Compare Series and Parallel Resonance.

5\. Practice Resonant Frequency calculations.

6\. Study Quality Factor and Circuit loss.

7\. Identify half-power frequencies on a response graph.

8\. Practice Bandwidth and Selectivity calculations.

9\. Learn Passband, Stopband and Cutoff Frequency.

10\. Compare Low Pass and High Pass Filters.

11\. Compare Band Pass and Band Stop Filters.

12\. Perform a frequency sweep in simulation or a safe laboratory setup.

13\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

14\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-14 Three-Phase AC Systems.
