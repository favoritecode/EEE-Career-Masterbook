\# Reference Notes

\- Frequency is measured in Hertz (Hz), and Time Period is measured in second (s).

\- Frequency and Time Period are reciprocal quantities: f = 1/T and T = 1/f.

\- One complete Cycle equals 360 electrical degrees or 2π radians.

\- For a pure sine wave, RMS Value equals Peak Value divided by √2.

\- The full-cycle algebraic Average Value of a symmetrical sine wave is zero, while its positive half-cycle Average Value is 2/π times its Peak Value.

\- Sine-wave RMS, Average, Crest and Form Factors must not be applied automatically to non-sinusoidal waveforms.

\- AC instrument accuracy depends on waveform, Frequency, bandwidth, Crest Factor, range and measurement method.

\- Actual AC work must follow suitable isolation, grounding, instrument-rating and electrical-safety procedures.
