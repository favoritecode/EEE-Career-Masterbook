\# Chapter 10

\# Glossary

\## Alternating Current

An Electric Current that changes magnitude with time and reverses direction periodically.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: AC or i(t)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Alternating Voltage

A Voltage whose magnitude varies with time and whose polarity reverses periodically.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: v(t)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Direct Current

Electric Current that normally flows in one direction; ideal steady DC has constant magnitude.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DC or I

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Periodic Waveform

A waveform that repeats the same pattern after a fixed interval of time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: x(t) = x(t + T)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## AC Waveform

A graph or mathematical representation showing how an alternating electrical quantity changes with time or electrical angle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Sine Wave

A smooth periodic waveform represented by a sine function and commonly used as the ideal AC power waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: x(t) = Xm sin(ωt + φ)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Square Wave

A periodic waveform that switches between two levels with comparatively rapid transitions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Triangular Wave

A periodic waveform whose value rises and falls approximately linearly, forming a triangular shape.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Sawtooth Wave

A periodic waveform with a linear or nearly linear change in one direction followed by a rapid return.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Cycle

One complete repetition of a periodic waveform, equal to 360 electrical degrees or 2π radians.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Cycle

\## Alternation

One half of a complete AC Cycle, corresponding to either the positive or negative half-cycle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Positive Half-Cycle

The half of a waveform for which Instantaneous Values are positive relative to the selected reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Negative Half-Cycle

The half of a waveform for which Instantaneous Values are negative relative to the selected reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Zero Crossing

A point at which a waveform passes through zero and changes sign.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: x(t) = 0

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Instantaneous Value

The value of a time-varying electrical quantity at a particular instant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: v(t), i(t) or x(t)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Frequency

The number of complete Cycles of a periodic waveform occurring per second.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Hertz

The SI unit of Frequency, equal to one Cycle per second.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Hz

\*\*Unit\*\*: s⁻¹

\## Angular Frequency

The angular rate of a sinusoidal waveform, equal to 2π times its Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ω

\*\*Unit\*\*: Radian per Second (rad/s)

\## Time Period

The time required for a periodic waveform to complete one full Cycle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: T

\*\*Unit\*\*: Second (s)

\## Electrical Angle

An angular measure of position within an AC Cycle, where one complete Cycle equals 360° or 2π radians.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Amplitude

The maximum magnitude reached by a waveform measured from its zero reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xm

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the represented quantity

\## Peak Value

The maximum positive or negative magnitude of an alternating quantity from the zero reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vp, Vm, Ip or Im

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## Peak-to-Peak Value

The total difference between the Positive Peak and Negative Peak of a waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vpp or Ipp

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## RMS Value

The square root of the mean of the squares of Instantaneous Values over one complete Cycle, representing equivalent resistive heating effect.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vrms or Irms

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## Root Mean Square

The mathematical process of squaring values, finding their mean and taking the square root.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the original quantity

\## True-RMS Meter

An instrument that determines RMS Value from the actual waveform within its specified bandwidth, Crest Factor and accuracy limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: True RMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the measured quantity

\## Average Value

The algebraic mean of a waveform's Instantaneous Values over a stated interval.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vavg, Iavg or Xavg

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the original quantity

\## Average Rectified Value

The mean of the absolute or rectified Instantaneous Values over a complete Cycle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Vavg(rect) or Iavg(rect)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) or Ampere (A)

\## Rectification

The process of converting an alternating waveform into a unidirectional waveform with a non-zero DC component.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Crest Factor

The ratio of a waveform's Peak Value to its RMS Value.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CF = Peak/RMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Form Factor

The ratio of RMS Value to Average Rectified Value.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FF = RMS/Average Rectified Value

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Phase

The angular position of a periodic waveform relative to a selected reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Phase Angle

The angle specifying a waveform's Phase relative to a reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: φ or θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Phase Difference

The angular or equivalent time displacement between two waveforms of the same Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Δφ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## In Phase

The condition in which same-Frequency waveforms reach corresponding points at the same time and have zero Phase Difference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Δφ = 0°

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°)

\## Leading

The condition in which one waveform reaches a corresponding point earlier than another same-Frequency waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: +φ by a common convention

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Lagging

The condition in which one waveform reaches a corresponding point later than another same-Frequency waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: −φ by a common convention

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Phasor

A rotating-vector mathematical representation of the magnitude and Phase of a sinusoidal quantity at one Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V̅, Ī or X̅

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the represented quantity

\## Waveform Distortion

Deviation of an actual waveform from its intended or ideal shape.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Harmonic

A sinusoidal component whose Frequency is an integer multiple of a waveform's Fundamental Frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: h or n

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Fundamental Frequency

The lowest Frequency component in a periodic waveform, with harmonics occurring at integer multiples of it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f₁

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\## Oscilloscope

An electronic instrument that displays Voltage as a function of time and supports waveform, Amplitude, Period and Phase measurements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CRO or DSO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Single-Phase AC

An AC supply system based on one alternating Voltage waveform or one phase set.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 1φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Three-Phase AC

An AC system containing three sinusoidal phase quantities of the same Frequency, normally displaced by 120 electrical degrees.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: 3φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Synchronous Speed

The rotational speed of an AC machine's rotating Magnetic Field, determined by supply Frequency and pole count.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ns

\*\*Unit\*\*: Revolution per Minute (r/min or rpm)

\## Inductive Reactance

The opposition an ideal inductor presents to sinusoidal AC, equal to 2πfL.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Capacitive Reactance

The opposition an ideal capacitor presents to sinusoidal AC, equal to 1/(2πfC).

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)
