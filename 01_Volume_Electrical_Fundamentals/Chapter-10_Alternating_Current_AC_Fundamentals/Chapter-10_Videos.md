\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Watch a beginner animation comparing AC and DC.

3\. Learn the parts of a Sine Wave and one complete Cycle.

4\. Practice Frequency and Time Period conversions.

5\. Identify Peak and Peak-to-Peak Values on waveform graphs.

6\. Practice converting sine-wave Peak and RMS Values.

7\. Compare full-cycle and half-cycle Average Values.

8\. Observe practical oscilloscope and True-RMS meter demonstrations.

9\. Practice Phase Difference calculations using angle and time.

10\. Review practical AC generation, transformation and applications.

11\. Solve the MCQs.

12\. Practice the Viva questions.

13\. Review the chapter summary and glossary.
