\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

Which statement correctly defines Alternating Current?

A. Current that always remains zero
B. Current that changes magnitude and reverses direction periodically
C. Current that flows only in one direction with constant magnitude
D. Current available only from a battery

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Current that changes magnitude and reverses direction periodically\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

AC varies with time and periodically reverses direction relative to the selected reference.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* AC normally has changing non-zero Instantaneous Values.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* This describes ideal steady DC.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Batteries normally provide DC.

\*\*Remember\*\*

AC → Varying Magnitude and Periodic Reversal
