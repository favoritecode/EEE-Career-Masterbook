\# Reference Notes

\- Magnetic Flux is measured in Weber (Wb), while Magnetic Flux Density is measured in Tesla (T).

\- One Tesla equals one Weber per square metre.

\- The general uniform-field Flux relationship is Φ = BA cos θ, where θ is measured between the field and the surface normal.

\- Electromagnetic Induction requires changing Flux Linkage; a steady Flux does not produce a continuous induced EMF.

\- Faraday's Law determines induced EMF from the rate of change of Flux Linkage, while Lenz's Law determines the opposing polarity or direction.

\- Ferromagnetic material properties are nonlinear and depend on operating point, temperature and magnetic history.

\- Transformer, motor, generator, relay and solenoid behavior must be evaluated using actual equipment ratings and manufacturer data.

\- Electromagnetic testing must follow suitable isolation, instrument-rating and electrical-safety procedures.
