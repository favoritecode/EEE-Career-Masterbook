\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

Which statement describes the interaction between magnetic poles?

A. Like poles attract and unlike poles repel
B. Like poles repel and unlike poles attract
C. All magnetic poles attract one another
D. Magnetic poles do not exert force

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Like poles repel and unlike poles attract\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A North Pole repels another North Pole, and a South Pole repels another South Pole. A North Pole and a South Pole attract each other.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* This reverses the law of magnetic poles.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Like poles repel rather than attract.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Magnetic poles can exert attractive or repulsive force.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Like Poles → Repel; Unlike Poles → Attract
