\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Watch a beginner animation on magnets and Magnetic Field Lines.

3\. Compare Diamagnetic, Paramagnetic, Ferromagnetic and Ferrimagnetic Materials.

4\. Learn the symbols and units of Magnetic Flux, Flux Density, Field Strength and Permeability.

5\. Observe how Electric Current produces a Magnetic Field.

6\. Watch practical Electromagnetic Induction demonstrations.

7\. Practice Faraday's Law numerical problems.

8\. Use Lenz's Law and the Right-Hand Grip Rule to determine direction.

9\. Compare the electromagnetic principles of motors, generators and transformers.

10\. Solve the MCQs.

11\. Practice the Viva questions.

12\. Review the chapter summary and glossary.
