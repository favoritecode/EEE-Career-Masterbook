\### MCQ-001



\*\*Question\*\*



What does Voltage represent?



A. Electric charge per unit time

B. Electrical energy transferred per unit charge

C. Opposition to electric current

D. Electrical power per unit resistance



\*\*Answer\*\*



\*\*B. Electrical energy transferred per unit charge\*\*



\*\*Explanation\*\*



Voltage describes the electrical energy transferred for each Coulomb of charge between two points. It can be expressed as V = W/Q.



\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*



❌ \*\*A.\*\* Charge per unit time defines Electric Current.

❌ \*\*C.\*\* Opposition to current defines Resistance.

❌ \*\*D.\*\* This is not the definition of Voltage.



\*\*Remember\*\*



Voltage = Energy per Charge

❌ \*\*C.\*\* Resistance equals V/I, not VI.

❌ \*\*D.\*\* Current equals V/R, not VR.
