\# Reference Notes



\- Use the SI unit Volt (V) for Voltage, EMF and Potential Difference.

\- Use the SI unit Ohm (Ω) for Electrical Resistance.

\- Ohm's Law applies when temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

\- Manufacturer data should be consulted for practical component ratings and temperature limits.
