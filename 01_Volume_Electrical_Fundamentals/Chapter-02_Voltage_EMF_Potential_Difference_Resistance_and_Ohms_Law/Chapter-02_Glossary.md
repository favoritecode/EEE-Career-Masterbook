\# Chapter 02



\# Glossary



\## Voltage



Electrical energy transferred per unit charge between two points.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: V



\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)



\## Electromotive Force (EMF)



Electrical energy supplied by a source per unit charge.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: E or ε



\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)



\## Potential Difference



Electrical energy converted or transferred per unit charge between two points in a circuit.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: V



\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)



\## Volt



The SI unit of Voltage, EMF and Potential Difference. One Volt equals one Joule of energy per Coulomb of charge.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: V



\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule per Coulomb (J/C)



\## Resistance



The property of a material or component that opposes the flow of electric current.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: R



\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)



\## Ohm



The SI unit of Electrical Resistance. One Ohm is the resistance that allows one Ampere to flow when one Volt is applied.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ω



\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt per Ampere (V/A)



\## Resistor



A circuit component designed to provide a specified amount of electrical resistance.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: R



\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)



\## Resistivity



A material property that indicates how strongly the material opposes electric current.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: ρ



\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm-metre (Ω·m)



\## Internal Resistance



The resistance inside a practical electrical source that causes internal voltage drop when current flows.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: r



\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)



\## Temperature Coefficient of Resistance



The fractional change in resistance per degree change in temperature at a specified reference temperature.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: α



\*\*Unit\*\*: Per Degree Celsius (°C⁻¹) or Per Kelvin (K⁻¹)



\## Ohm's Law



The relationship stating that current is directly proportional to potential difference for a conductor when temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.



\*\*Formula\*\*: V = IR



\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable



\## Ohmic Conductor



A conductor that obeys Ohm's Law and has a linear V–I characteristic under constant physical conditions.



\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable



\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable
