\# Basic Level

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What does Kirchhoff's Current Law state?

A. The algebraic sum of Currents at a Node is zero
B. The algebraic sum of Resistances at a Node is zero
C. Current is always equal in Parallel branches
D. Voltage is always zero across a load

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. The algebraic sum of Currents at a Node is zero\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

KCL states that total Current entering a Node equals total Current leaving it. Therefore, the algebraic sum of Node Currents is zero.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*B.\*\* KCL concerns Current, not the sum of Resistances.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Parallel branch Currents depend on branch Resistance.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* A load normally has a Voltage drop while operating.

\*\*Remember\*\*

KCL: ΣI = 0

B. The algebraic sum of Voltages around a closed Loop is zero

❌ \*\*A.\*\* Branch Currents may differ.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Circuit Resistance is not generally zero.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Different Nodes can have different electrical potentials.

KVL: ΣV = 0

\### MCQ-011

\*\*Question\*\*

Currents of 5 A and 3 A enter a Node. If 6 A leaves, what other Current must leave?

A. 1 A
B. 2 A
C. 8 A
D. 14 A

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. 2 A\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Using KCL, total entering Current is 5 + 3 = 8 A. Therefore, the remaining leaving Current is 8 − 6 = 2 A.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* This does not balance the Node Currents.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* This is total entering Current, not the unknown branch Current.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* All Currents must not be added without considering direction.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Entering Current = Leaving Current

\### MCQ-017

\*\*Question\*\*

A Node V is connected to 10 V through 5 Ω and to Ground through 10 Ω. What is V?

A. 3.33 V
B. 5 V
C. 6.67 V
D. 10 V

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*C. 6.67 V\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

KCL gives (V − 10)/5 + V/10 = 0. Multiplying by 10 gives 2V − 20 + V = 0, so V = 20/3 = 6.67 V.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* This is the Voltage difference between the 10 V Node and V.
❌ \*\*B.\*\* Unequal resistors do not divide the Voltage equally.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Current through the 5 Ω resistor would then be zero while the 10 Ω branch draws Current.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Write Every Branch Current in Terms of Node Voltage
