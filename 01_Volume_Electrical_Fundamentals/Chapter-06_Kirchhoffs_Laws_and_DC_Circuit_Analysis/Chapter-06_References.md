\# Reference Notes

\- Apply a consistent reference direction to every Branch Current before writing KCL equations.

\- Apply a consistent traversal direction and Voltage polarity convention when writing KVL equations.

\- A negative calculated Current or Voltage indicates that the actual direction or polarity is opposite to the assumed reference.

\- Independent KCL or KVL equations must be selected carefully; redundant equations do not provide new information.

\- Nodal Analysis is often convenient when a circuit has fewer essential Nodes than independent Meshes.

\- Mesh Analysis applies directly to planar circuits and is often convenient when a circuit has fewer Meshes than essential Nodes.

\- Verify a completed solution using KCL, KVL, Ohm's Law and Power balance whenever practical.

\- Actual circuit testing must follow suitable isolation, instrument-rating and electrical-safety procedures.
