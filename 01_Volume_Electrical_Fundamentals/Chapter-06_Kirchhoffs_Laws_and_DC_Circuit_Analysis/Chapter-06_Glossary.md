\# Chapter 06

\# Glossary

\## Kirchhoff's Current Law

The law stating that the algebraic sum of all Currents at an electrical Node is zero.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: KCL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Kirchhoff's Voltage Law

The law stating that the algebraic sum of all Voltage rises and drops around a closed Loop is zero.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: KVL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Current Balance

The condition in which total Current entering a Node equals total Current leaving it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΣI = 0

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Voltage Balance

The condition in which the algebraic sum of Voltages around a closed Loop is zero.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΣV = 0

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Node

A point or continuous ideal conducting region where two or more circuit terminals are electrically connected and share one potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Simple Node

A Node connecting only two circuit elements, commonly forming part of a Series path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Essential Node

A Node at which three or more circuit elements or Branches are connected.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Reference Node

The Node selected as the zero-Volt reference from which other Node Voltages are measured.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ground or 0 V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Ground

The common name for the selected Reference Node in circuit analysis; it is not necessarily a physical earth connection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GND

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Node Voltage

The Potential Difference between a selected Node and the Reference Node.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V₁, V₂, V₃, ...

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Branch

A path between two Nodes containing one or more circuit elements and carrying one branch Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Branch Current

The Current flowing through an individual Branch between two Nodes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I₁, I₂, I₃, ...

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Loop

A closed path that starts and ends at the same Node without passing through a Node more than once along the selected path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Mesh

A Loop that contains no other Loop inside it and forms a smallest independent closed path in a planar circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Planar Circuit

A circuit that can be drawn on a plane without Branches crossing except at electrical Nodes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Independent Loop

A Loop whose KVL equation provides information not obtainable by combining the equations of other selected Loops.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: L

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless Count

\## Mesh Current

An assumed Current circulating around a Mesh for the purpose of Mesh-Current Analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I₁, I₂, I₃, ...

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## Shared Branch

A Branch that belongs to two adjacent Meshes and carries the algebraic combination of their Mesh Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Node-Voltage Analysis

A systematic circuit-analysis method that uses KCL and Ohm's Law to determine unknown Node Voltages.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Nodal Analysis

Another name for Node-Voltage Analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Mesh-Current Analysis

A systematic method that assigns Mesh Currents and uses KVL and Ohm's Law to determine unknown circuit Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Supernode

A combined analysis region formed when an ideal Voltage source connects two non-reference Nodes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Supermesh

A combined Mesh path formed when an ideal Current source lies in a Branch shared by two Meshes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Constraint Equation

An additional equation that expresses a known relationship imposed by a Voltage or Current source during Supernode or Supermesh Analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Constraint

\## Algebraic Sum

A sum in which the sign of each quantity is included according to its selected direction or polarity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Σ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Quantity

\## Sign Convention

A consistent rule used to assign positive and negative signs to Currents, Voltage rises and Voltage drops.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Assumed Direction

A reference direction selected for an unknown Current before solving a circuit equation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Arrow Direction

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Voltage Rise

An increase in electric potential encountered while traversing a circuit element from lower to higher potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: +V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Voltage Drop

A decrease in electric potential encountered while traversing a circuit element from higher to lower potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: −V

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Passive Sign Convention

A polarity convention in which Current enters the terminal marked positive for an element absorbing Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PSC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Simultaneous Equations

Two or more equations containing common unknown quantities that must be satisfied at the same time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Power Balance

The condition in which total Power supplied by sources equals total Power absorbed by loads and other circuit elements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΣP = 0

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Lumped Circuit

An idealized circuit model in which electrical properties are represented by discrete elements and propagation effects are neglected for the analysis conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Circuit Topology

The connection structure of Nodes, Branches and circuit elements, independent of their physical drawing shape or position.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Power Supplied

Electrical Power delivered by a source to the rest of a circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P supplied

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Power Absorbed

Electrical Power received or converted by a load or circuit element.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P absorbed

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)
