\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Watch a beginner animation on charge and energy conservation.

3\. Learn the definitions of Node, Branch, Loop and Mesh.

4\. Practice assigning Current directions and Voltage polarities.

5\. Observe practical KCL and KVL verification demonstrations.

6\. Solve KCL and KVL numerical problems step by step.

7\. Check each solution using Ohm's Law and Power balance.

8\. Solve the MCQs.

9\. Practice the Viva questions.

10\. Review the chapter summary.
