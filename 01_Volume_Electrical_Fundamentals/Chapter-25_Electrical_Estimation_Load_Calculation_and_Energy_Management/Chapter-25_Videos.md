\## Electrical Estimation, Load Calculation and Energy Management

\---

\## About This Section

The following educational resources are recommended for strengthening this chapter. Select videos with transparent worksheets, stated assumptions, load-profile graphs, demand and diversity timelines, Power-Factor calculations, Cable and protection tables, annotated electricity bills, Energy-audit measurements and lifecycle-cost examples.

Practical measurements, panel inspection and installation surveys must be demonstrated by competent persons under approved safety procedures. Tariff examples should display their source and effective date; an old or illustrative rate must not be presented as a current official tariff.

\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Define the estimate scope and required outputs.

2\. Classify all electrical loads.

3\. Prepare the Connected-Load inventory.

4\. Estimate Maximum Demand with approved data.

5\. Practice Demand and Diversity Factors separately.

6\. Calculate Load Factor from Energy and peak demand.

7\. Review Power Factor and Current reduction.

8\. Complete a residential load worksheet.

9\. Complete a commercial load worksheet.

10\. Follow the full Cable-selection sequence.

11\. Coordinate protective devices with load and fault duty.

12\. Calculate kWh for several load profiles.

13\. Identify bill components and verify tariff date.

14\. Learn baseline, EnPI, SEU and M&V concepts.

15\. Prioritize safe Energy-conservation measures.

16\. Compare efficient equipment by actual duty and TCO.

17\. Perform quantity take-off and BOQ review.

18\. Audit units, diversity, efficiency and assumptions.

19\. Keep all safety and verification items in the estimate.

20\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

21\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-26 Semiconductor Fundamentals, Diodes and Rectifiers.
