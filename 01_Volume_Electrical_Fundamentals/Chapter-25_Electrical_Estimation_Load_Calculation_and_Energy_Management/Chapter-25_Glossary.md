\# Chapter 25

\# Glossary

\## Electrical Estimation, Load Calculation and Energy Management

\---

\## Electrical Estimation

Systematic determination of electrical loads, ratings, quantities, labour and cost for a defined project.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Estimate

A documented calculation of expected project scope, quantities, resources and cost.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Scope

The defined work, systems, boundaries and deliverables included in a project.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Exclusion

A work item explicitly stated as not included in an estimate or contract boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Assumption

A stated condition used when verified project data is not yet available.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Material Take-Off

Measurement and listing of material quantities from drawings, schedules and site data.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Item, metre (m), set, or other quantity

\---

\## Bill of Quantities

A structured list of work items and quantities for pricing, procurement and control.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BOQ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Item-specific quantity

\---

\## Unit Rate

Cost assigned to one unit of material, labour or work.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per unit

\---

\## Direct Cost

Cost directly attributable to materials, labour, equipment or a specific work item.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Indirect Cost

Project cost not assigned to one installed item, such as supervision or temporary facilities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Contingency

A controlled allowance for defined estimating uncertainty and risk.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency or percent (%)

\---

\## Load Schedule

A tabulated record of electrical loads, ratings, demand, phase and circuit information.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW, kVA, A

\---

\## Cable Schedule

A record of Cable origin, destination, type, size, length and installation information.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: mm² and metre (m)

\---

\## Distribution Board Schedule

A table of outgoing circuits, loads, protection, Cables and phase allocation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DB schedule

\*\*Unit\*\*: A, kW or kVA

\---

\## Single-Line Diagram

A simplified drawing representing a multi-conductor Power system using single lines and symbols.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SLD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Electrical Load

Equipment or a system that consumes, converts, stores or sometimes returns electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Resistive Load

A load whose ideal Current is in phase with Voltage and primarily converts Energy to Heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R load

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Inductive Load

A load that stores magnetic Energy and normally requires Reactive Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: L load

\*\*Unit\*\*: var or volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Nonlinear Load

A load drawing Current that is not proportional to the applied Voltage waveform.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W), VA, THD (%)

\---

\## Continuous Load

A load expected to operate for a duration classified as continuous by the applicable code.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or watt (W)

\---

\## Intermittent Load

A load operating in repeated or occasional periods with off-time between operations.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Duty cycle in percent (%)

\---

\## Critical Load

A load requiring enhanced continuity because interruption has serious safety or operational effects.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Connected Load

Sum of installed electrical input ratings within a defined boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pc

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Installed Capacity

Total rated capacity of installed equipment within a defined system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW, kVA or MW

\---

\## Maximum Demand

Highest average load measured or expected over a specified demand interval and period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MD

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Demand Interval

Time interval over which average demand is calculated for billing or analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: minute (min)

\---

\## Coincident Demand

Demand of a group at the same instant or demand interval.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Noncoincident Demand

Sum or consideration of individual demands that occur at different times.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Demand Factor

Ratio of Maximum Demand to Connected Load for the same boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Diversity Factor

Ratio of the sum of individual Maximum Demands to the coincident group Maximum Demand.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Load Factor

Ratio of Average Load to Maximum Demand over the same period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Average Load

Energy consumed during a period divided by the duration of that period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pavg

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Utilization Factor

Ratio comparing the maximum load or output used with the rated capacity under a stated definition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ku

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Coincidence Factor

Ratio of coincident group demand to the sum of individual Maximum Demands; reciprocal of Diversity Factor under matching definitions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Future Load

Approved or expected additional load considered in capacity planning.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Spare Capacity

Unused rated capacity available for additional load under defined conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW, kVA or percent (%)

\---

\## Active Power

Average Power converted into useful work, Heat or other Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or kilowatt (kW)

\---

\## Reactive Power

Power associated with periodic Energy exchange in AC electric and magnetic fields.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: var or kilovar (kvar)

\---

\## Apparent Power

Product of RMS Voltage and Current without the phase-angle multiplier.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere (VA) or kVA

\---

\## Power Factor

Ratio of active Power to apparent Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Displacement Power Factor

Cosine of the phase angle between fundamental Voltage and Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: cos φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## True Power Factor

Ratio of total active Power to total apparent Power, including distortion effects.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Power-Factor Correction

Controlled supply of Reactive Power to reduce upstream Reactive Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PFC

\*\*Unit\*\*: kvar

\---

\## Capacitor Bank

An assembly of Capacitors used for Reactive-Power compensation or Voltage support.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilovar (kvar)

\---

\## Load Current

Current drawn by a load during stated operating conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Design Current

Current used as the basis for circuit design and equipment selection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ib

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Current-Carrying Capacity

Permitted continuous Cable Current under stated installation conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iz

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Protective-Device Rating

Nominal Current rating assigned to a protective device.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: In

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Correction Factor

Multiplier applied to tabulated Cable capacity for actual installation conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Derating

Reduction of permitted equipment or Cable loading because of adverse conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Factor or percent (%)

\---

\## Voltage Drop

Reduction in Voltage along a circuit caused by Current and Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V) or percent (%)

\---

\## Prospective Fault Current

Current expected at a point if a specified fault occurs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PFC

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Breaking Capacity

Maximum fault Current a protective device can interrupt safely under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Icn/Icu

\*\*Unit\*\*: kiloampere (kA)

\---

\## Selectivity

Coordination in which the nearest suitable protective device operates while upstream supply remains available where practical.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current range in A or kA

\---

\## Trip Curve

Relationship between protective-device operating time and Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time versus Current

\---

\## Motor Starting Current

Current drawn by a Motor during acceleration from rest.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ist

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Inrush Current

Short-duration input Current when equipment is energized.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iinrush

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Short-Circuit Withstand

Ability of equipment or a conductor to withstand fault Current for a specified time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I²t

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere-squared second (A²s)

\---

\## Electrical Energy

Active Power integrated over time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt-hour (Wh) or kilowatt-hour (kWh)

\---

\## Kilowatt-Hour

Energy used by one kilowatt operating for one hour.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: kWh

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilowatt-hour (kWh)

\---

\## Operating Hours

Duration for which a load operates during a stated period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: t

\*\*Unit\*\*: hour (h)

\---

\## Duty Cycle

Fraction of time equipment operates in an active state during a cycle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: D

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%)

\---

\## Load Profile

Time-ordered record or estimate of load or demand.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW versus time

\---

\## Load Curve

Graph of load against chronological time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or MW versus time

\---

\## Energy Meter

Instrument that measures electrical Energy consumption or exchange.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Meter

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh or kvarh

\---

\## Submetering

Measurement of Energy or demand below the main service boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh, kW or kVA

\---

\## Meter Multiplier

Factor applied to a meter register when instrument-transformer or scaling ratios are used.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Electricity Tariff

Official schedule of rates and conditions used to calculate electricity charges.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per kWh, kW or kVA

\---

\## Energy Charge

Billing component based on metered active Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Demand Charge

Billing component based on billed Maximum Demand.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per kW or kVA

\---

\## Fixed Charge

Billing amount independent of measured Energy within stated tariff rules.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per billing period

\---

\## Time-of-Use Tariff

Tariff applying different rates during defined time periods.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TOU

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency per kWh

\---

\## Power-Factor Adjustment

Billing incentive or penalty linked to specified Power-Factor performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency or percent (%)

\---

\## Billing Period

Time interval covered by an electricity invoice.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: day or month

\---

\## Energy Management

Organized measurement, analysis, control and continual improvement of Energy performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EnM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Energy Policy

Formal statement of an organization's Energy-performance commitments and direction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Energy Baseline

Quantitative reference used to compare Energy performance over time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EnB

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh, GJ, or normalized value

\---

\## Energy Performance Indicator

Quantitative measure of Energy performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EnPI

\*\*Unit\*\*: Application-specific

\---

\## Significant Energy Use

Energy use with substantial consumption or improvement potential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SEU

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh, GJ or percent (%)

\---

\## Energy Audit

Systematic inspection and analysis of Energy use and improvement opportunities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Energy and currency metrics

\---

\## Measurement and Verification

Process of quantifying Energy savings using measurements and an agreed methodology.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: M&V

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh or currency

\---

\## Energy Conservation

Reduction of avoidable Energy use while maintaining required service and safety.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh or percent (%)

\---

\## Energy Efficiency

Ratio or relationship between useful output and Energy input.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: η

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Energy Saving

Reduction in Energy use relative to an adjusted baseline.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh, GJ or percent (%)

\---

\## Demand Management

Control or shifting of load to reduce peak demand or improve system operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DSM

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Peak Shaving

Reduction of short-duration peak demand.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kW or kVA

\---

\## Load Shifting

Movement of Energy use from one time period to another.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: kWh shifted

\---

\## Variable Frequency Drive

Power-electronic equipment controlling AC Motor frequency and Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VFD

\*\*Unit\*\*: hertz (Hz), volt (V), ampere (A)

\---

\## High-Efficiency Motor

A Motor meeting a defined efficiency class under an applicable test standard.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HEM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Efficiency in percent (%)

\---

\## LED Efficacy

Visible light output per electrical input Power for an LED product.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: lumen per watt (lm/W)

\---

\## Standby Loss

Power consumed while equipment is ready but not delivering its principal service.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pstandby

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Part-Load Efficiency

Efficiency when equipment operates below rated full output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ηPL

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%)

\---

\## Total Cost of Ownership

Combined purchase, installation, Energy, maintenance, downtime and residual cost over life.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TCO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Simple Payback Period

Initial investment divided by annual monetary saving.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SPP

\*\*Unit\*\*: year

\---

\## Lifecycle Cost

Present or undiscounted total cost over an asset's defined life, according to stated method.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LCC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Net Present Value

Present value of future cash flows minus investment under a stated discount rate.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NPV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Currency

\---

\## Return on Investment

Ratio relating net benefit to investment under a stated definition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ROI

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%)

\---

\## Energy Management System

Organizational framework for policy, objectives, data, action and continual Energy improvement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EnMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## ISO 50001

International standard specifying requirements for an Energy Management System.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ISO 50001

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Power Usage Effectiveness

Ratio of total data-center Energy to IT-equipment Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PUE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Lifecycle Assessment

Assessment of environmental impacts across defined stages of a product or system life.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LCA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Impact-specific units

\---

\## As-Built Drawing

Drawing revised to show the installation as actually constructed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Drawing scale

\---

\## Revision Control

Managed identification, approval and history of document changes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Revision number

\---

\## Quantity Survey

Measurement and checking of work and material quantities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Item-specific quantity

\---

\## Commissioning

Controlled verification that an installed system meets specified design and functional requirements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Test-specific units
