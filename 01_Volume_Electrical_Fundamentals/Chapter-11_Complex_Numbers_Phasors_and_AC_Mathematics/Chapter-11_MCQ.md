\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

Which symbol is normally used for the Imaginary Unit in Electrical Engineering?

A. a
B. e
C. i only
D. j

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*D. j\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Electrical Engineering normally uses j = √−1 because i is commonly used to represent instantaneous Current.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A is commonly a Real-number coefficient.
\- B is the base of natural logarithms.
\- C is common in mathematics but is avoided in many electrical calculations to prevent confusion with Current.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Electrical Engineering: j = √−1
