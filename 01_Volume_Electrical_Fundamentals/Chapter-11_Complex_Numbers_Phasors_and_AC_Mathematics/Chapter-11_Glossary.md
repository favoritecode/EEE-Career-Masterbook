\# Chapter 11

\# Glossary

\## Complex Number

A number containing a Real Part and an Imaginary Part, commonly written as a + jb.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z = a + jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Real Number

A number located on the Real Axis and having no Imaginary component.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: a or Re(z)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Imaginary Number

A Real coefficient multiplied by the Imaginary Unit j.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Imaginary Unit

The quantity defined as the square root of negative one and written as j in Electrical Engineering.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: j = √−1

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Real Part

The horizontal component a of a Complex Number z = a + jb.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Re(z) = a

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Imaginary Part

The Real coefficient b of the Imaginary term in z = a + jb.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Im(z) = b

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Complex Plane

A two-dimensional plane used to plot Complex Numbers with a horizontal Real Axis and vertical Imaginary Axis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Real Axis

The horizontal axis of the Complex Plane on which Real components are plotted.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Re Axis

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Imaginary Axis

The vertical axis of the Complex Plane on which Imaginary components are plotted.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Im Axis or j Axis

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Quadrant

One of the four regions of the Complex Plane determined by the signs of Real and Imaginary components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I, II, III or IV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Complex Conjugate

A Complex Number formed by keeping the Real Part unchanged and reversing the sign of the Imaginary Part.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z\* = a − jb for z = a + jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Rectangular Form

A Complex Number form that displays separate Real and Imaginary components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z = a + jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Cartesian Form

Another name for Rectangular Form of a Complex Number.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z = a + jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Polar Form

A Complex Number form that displays magnitude and phase angle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z = r∠θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on r; Angle in Degree or Radian

\## Exponential Form

A Complex Number form using a magnitude multiplied by a Complex Exponential.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: z = reʲθ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on r; Angle in Radian Mathematically

\## Magnitude

The non-negative length of a Complex Number from the origin of the Complex Plane.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: r = |z| = √(a² + b²)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Represented Quantity

\## Argument

The angle of a Complex Number measured from the positive Real Axis under a selected convention.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: arg(z) = θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Phase Angle

The angular position of a Complex or Phasor quantity relative to a selected reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: θ or φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Reference Angle

The acute angle between a Complex Number direction and the nearest Real Axis, used before quadrant correction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: θref

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Principal Angle

The representative angle of a Complex Number selected within a specified standard range, commonly −180° to +180°.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Arg(z)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Degree

An angular unit in which one complete revolution equals 360 degrees.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: °

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°)

\## Radian

The SI coherent unit of plane angle; one complete revolution equals 2π radians.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: rad

\*\*Unit\*\*: Radian (rad)

\## Rectangular-to-Polar Conversion

The process of calculating magnitude and angle from Real and Imaginary components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: a + jb → r∠θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Quantity

\## Polar-to-Rectangular Conversion

The process of calculating Real and Imaginary components using a = r cos θ and b = r sin θ.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: r∠θ → a + jb

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Quantity

\## Inverse Tangent

The inverse trigonometric function used with component ratios to determine a reference angle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: tan⁻¹ or arctan

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## atan2 Function

A two-argument inverse tangent function that uses both component signs to determine the correct angle quadrant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: atan2(b, a)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Complex Addition

The operation of adding Real Parts together and Imaginary Parts together.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: (a + jb) + (c + jd)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Added Quantities

\## Complex Subtraction

The operation of subtracting corresponding Real and Imaginary components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: (a + jb) − (c + jd)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Subtracted Quantities

\## Complex Multiplication

The operation that multiplies magnitudes and adds angles when performed in Polar Form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: r₁r₂∠(θ₁ + θ₂)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Product of the Operand Units

\## Complex Division

The operation that divides magnitudes and subtracts angles when performed in Polar Form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: (r₁/r₂)∠(θ₁ − θ₂)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ratio of the Operand Units

\## Component Addition

Addition performed separately along the Real and Imaginary axes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΣRe + jΣIm

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Components

\## Phasor

A Complex Number representing the magnitude and phase angle of a sinusoidal quantity at a specified common frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V, I or X in bold or marked Phasor notation

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Represented Quantity

\## Reference Phasor

The Phasor selected as the angular reference, commonly assigned an angle of 0°.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xref = X∠0°

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Quantity

\## Voltage Phasor

A Phasor representing the magnitude and phase of a sinusoidal Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V = V∠φV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\## Current Phasor

A Phasor representing the magnitude and phase of a sinusoidal Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I = I∠φI

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\## RMS Phasor

A Phasor whose magnitude is the Root Mean Square value of the sinusoidal quantity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xrms∠φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Represented Quantity

\## Peak Phasor

A Phasor whose magnitude is the Peak value of the sinusoidal quantity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Xm∠φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Represented Quantity

\## Cosine Reference

A Phasor convention in which sinusoidal waveforms are expressed as cosine functions before their phase angles are recorded.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: x(t) = Xm cos(ωt + φ)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Sine Reference

A Phasor convention in which sinusoidal waveforms are expressed as sine functions before their phase angles are recorded.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: x(t) = Xm sin(ωt + φ)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Common Time Factor

The sinusoidal or Complex Exponential time dependence shared by Phasors at one frequency and omitted from constant Phasor notation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: eʲωt

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Angular Frequency

The rate of sinusoidal phase change with time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ω = 2πf

\*\*Unit\*\*: Radian per Second (rad/s)

\## Phase Difference

The angular separation between two sinusoidal quantities of the same frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Δφ = φ₁ − φ₂

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Leading Quantity

A sinusoidal quantity whose phase angle is more positive than another quantity relative to the same reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Positive Relative Phase

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Lagging Quantity

A sinusoidal quantity whose phase angle is more negative than another quantity relative to the same reference.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Negative Relative Phase

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)

\## Phasor Addition

The component-wise addition of same-frequency sinusoidal Phasors after conversion to Rectangular Form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X = X₁ + X₂

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Added Phasors

\## Phasor Subtraction

The component-wise subtraction of same-frequency sinusoidal Phasors in Rectangular Form.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X = X₁ − X₂

\*\*Unit\*\*: Same as the Phasors

\## Phasor Diagram

A Complex Plane drawing showing the relative magnitudes and phase angles of Phasor quantities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Depends on the Plotted Quantities

\## Sinusoidal Steady State

The condition in which Circuit Voltages and Currents are sinusoidal at the excitation frequency after switching transients have decayed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not Applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Euler's Formula

The identity connecting Complex Exponential Form with cosine and sine components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: eʲθ = cos θ + j sin θ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Complex Exponential

An exponential expression with an Imaginary exponent that represents rotation or phase in the Complex Plane.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: eʲθ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\## Complex Impedance

The Complex opposition to sinusoidal Current, containing Resistance and net Reactance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z = R + jX

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Resistance Component

The Real Part of Complex Impedance associated with active energy conversion or dissipation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R = Re(Z)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Reactance Component

The Imaginary Part of Complex Impedance associated with Inductive or Capacitive energy exchange.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: X = Im(Z)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Inductive Reactance Sign

The positive Imaginary sign used for Inductive Reactance in standard Complex Impedance notation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: +jXL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Capacitive Reactance Sign

The negative Imaginary sign used for Capacitive Reactance in standard Complex Impedance notation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: −jXC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\## Admittance

The reciprocal of Impedance, representing how readily sinusoidal Current flows.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Y = 1/Z

\*\*Unit\*\*: Siemens (S)

\## Phasor Ohm's Law

The Complex relationship among sinusoidal steady-state Voltage, Current and Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V = IZ

\*\*Unit\*\*: V, A and Ω as Applicable

\## Complex Power

A Complex quantity combining Active and Reactive Power when calculated with consistent RMS Phasors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S = VI\* = P + jQ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt-Ampere (VA)

\## Active Power

The Real Part of Complex Power associated with net energy conversion or useful work.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P = Re(S)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\## Reactive Power

The Imaginary Part of Complex Power associated with periodic energy exchange in reactive elements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q = Im(S)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt-Ampere Reactive (var)

\## Magnitude Convention

The declared use of RMS or Peak values for the magnitudes of all related Phasors in a calculation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RMS or Peak

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not Applicable

\## Angle Convention

The declared reference direction, sign, trigonometric basis and unit used for phase angles.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: θ or φ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Degree (°) or Radian (rad)
