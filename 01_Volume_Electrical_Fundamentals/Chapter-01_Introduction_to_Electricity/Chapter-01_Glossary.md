\# Chapter 01



\# Glossary



\---



\## Electricity



A form of energy associated with the presence or movement of electric charge.



\---



\## Atom



The smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties.



\---



\## Electron



A negatively charged subatomic particle responsible for electric current in conductors.



\---



\## Proton



A positively charged subatomic particle located in the nucleus.



\---



\## Neutron



An electrically neutral subatomic particle located in the nucleus.



\---



\## Electric Charge



A fundamental property of matter responsible for electrical attraction and repulsion.



\---



\## Conductor



A material that allows electric current to flow easily.



\---



\## Insulator



A material that resists the flow of electric current.



\---



\## Semiconductor



A material whose conductivity lies between a conductor and an insulator.



\---



\## Static Electricity



The accumulation of electric charge at rest.



\---



\## Electric Current



The rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.
