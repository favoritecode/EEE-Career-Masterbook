\# Basic Viva



\### Viva-001



\*\*Question\*\*



What is Electricity?



\*\*Answer\*\*



Electricity is a physical phenomenon associated with the presence or movement of electric charge. In electrical circuits, the controlled movement of electrons produces electric current, which is used to transfer electrical energy.



\*\*Key Points\*\*



\- Electricity is related to electric charge.

\- Electricity is the foundation of Electrical Engineering.

\- Electrical energy is delivered through electric current.



\*\*Remember\*\*



Electricity ≠ Electric Current



Electric Current = Flow of Electric Charge

\### Viva-002



\*\*Question\*\*



What is an Atom?

\### Viva-008



\*\*Question\*\*



What is a Semiconductor?

\### Viva-010



\*\*Question\*\*



What is Static Electricity?



\*\*Answer\*\*



Static electricity is the accumulation of electric charge on the surface of a material. It usually occurs due to friction and remains at rest until it is discharged.



\*\*Key Points\*\*



\- Charge remains at rest.

\- Usually produced by friction.

\- Can cause a small electric spark during discharge.



\*\*Remember\*\*



Static Electricity = Charge at Rest



Current Electricity = Charge in Motion

\### Viva-015



\*\*Question\*\*

\- Static electricity is usually caused by friction.



\*\*Remember\*\*

\### Viva-016



\*\*Question\*\*



Why is Silicon preferred over Copper for manufacturing electronic devices?



\*\*Answer\*\*



Silicon is preferred because its electrical conductivity can be controlled, which is essential for electronic components such as diodes, transistors and integrated circuits. Copper has very high conductivity but cannot perform switching or signal control functions.



\*\*Key Points\*\*



\- Silicon → Controllable conductivity.

\- Copper → High conductivity.

\- Silicon is used in electronics, while Copper is mainly used for electrical wiring.



\*\*Remember\*\*



Copper → Electrical Wiring



Silicon → Electronic Devices

\### Viva-019



\*\*Question\*\*



What is the difference between an Atom and an Electron?



\*\*Answer\*\*



An Atom is the smallest unit of an element and consists of protons, neutrons and electrons. An Electron is a negatively charged subatomic particle that moves around the nucleus and is mainly responsible for electric current.



\*\*Key Points\*\*



\- Atom contains Electron.

\- Electron is a part of an Atom.

\- Electron carries a negative charge.



\*\*Remember\*\*



Atom ≠ Electron



Electron is one part of an Atom.
