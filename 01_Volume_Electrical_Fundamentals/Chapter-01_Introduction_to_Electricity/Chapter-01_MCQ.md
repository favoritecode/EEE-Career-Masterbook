\### MCQ-001



\*\*Question\*\*



Which statement best describes Electricity?



A. Electricity is a type of metal.



B. Electricity is the presence or movement of electric charge.



C. Electricity is a chemical substance.



D. Electricity is a magnetic material.



\*\*Answer\*\*



\*\*B. Electricity is the presence or movement of electric charge.\*\*



\*\*Explanation\*\*



Electricity is a physical phenomenon associated with the presence or movement of electric charge. In electrical circuits, the controlled movement of electrons produces electric current, which is used to operate electrical and electronic devices.



\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*



❌ \*\*A.\*\* Electricity is not a metal; metals such as copper and aluminium are conductors of electricity.



❌ \*\*C.\*\* Electricity is not a chemical substance. Although chemical reactions inside a battery can generate electricity, electricity itself is not a substance.



❌ \*\*D.\*\* Magnetism and electricity are related phenomena, but electricity is not a magnetic material.



\*\*Remember\*\*



\- Electricity → Related to Electric Charge

\- Electric Current → Flow of Electric Charge
