\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main purpose of Network Theorems?

A. To increase every source Voltage
B. To simplify and analyze electrical networks
C. To remove all Circuit loads
D. To convert Resistance into Energy

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. To simplify and analyze electrical networks\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Network Theorems provide systematic methods for separating source effects or replacing parts of a linear network with simpler equivalents.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

❌ \*\*A.\*\* A theorem does not increase source Voltage.
❌ \*\*C.\*\* Loads may be temporarily removed only for selected calculations.
❌ \*\*D.\*\* Resistance is not converted into Energy.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Network Theorems Reduce Calculation Complexity
