\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review linearity, Ohm's Law, KCL and KVL.

3\. Watch an overview of Network Theorems and equivalent circuits.

4\. Practice correct independent-source deactivation.

5\. Solve one Superposition example source by source.

6\. Find Thevenin and Norton equivalents at the same load terminals.

7\. Verify both equivalents using a selected load.

8\. Practice Maximum Power Transfer and Source Transformation problems.

9\. Solve Delta–Star and Star–Delta conversion examples.

10\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

11\. Review the Glossary and chapter summary.
