\# Chapter 21

\# Glossary

\## Generation of Electrical Power

\---

\## Electrical Power Generation

The controlled conversion of primary Energy into electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Generation

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in watt (W); Energy in watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Primary Energy

Energy available directly from a natural or extracted source before conversion into an Energy carrier.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J) or source-specific unit

\---

\## Secondary Energy

An Energy carrier produced by converting Primary Energy, such as electricity or Hydrogen.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J), watt-hour (Wh) or source-specific unit

\---

\## Prime Mover

A machine that supplies mechanical shaft Power to a Generator.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W)

\---

\## Alternator

A Synchronous Generator that converts mechanical shaft Power into AC electrical Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G or alternator symbol

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt-ampere (VA) or watt (W)

\---

\## Generating Station

A complete facility containing generating units, auxiliaries, controls, protection and Grid connection equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GS where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: Capacity in watt (W)

\---

\## Distributed Generation

Generation connected close to consumers or within a distribution network rather than only at a large central station.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DG

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Captive Generation

Generation installed primarily to supply the owner's own facility or industrial load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Captive Power

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Renewable Energy

Energy obtained from resources naturally replenished on a useful human time scale.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RE

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J) or watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Non-Renewable Energy

Energy obtained from finite resources depleted by use, such as fossil or nuclear fuel.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J) or source-specific unit

\---

\## Dispatchable Generation

Generation whose output can be scheduled or adjusted on command within technical limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Variable Renewable Energy

Renewable generation whose available output varies with weather or natural resource conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VRE

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Thermal Power Plant

A plant that converts Heat Energy into mechanical and then electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: TPP where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: Capacity in watt (W)

\---

\## Boiler

Equipment that transfers Heat to water to produce steam at required pressure and temperature.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Steam rating in kg/s or t/h; pressure in pascal (Pa)

\---

\## Steam Turbine

A prime mover that converts steam Energy into rotating mechanical Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ST

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Condenser

A Heat Exchanger that converts exhaust steam into water by rejecting Heat to a cooling medium.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Heat duty in watt (W)

\---

\## Hydroelectric Power Plant

A plant that converts the potential or kinetic Energy of water into electricity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HPP or Hydro

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Hydraulic Head

The Energy per unit weight of water represented by an equivalent vertical height.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m)

\---

\## Flow Rate

The volume of fluid passing a section per unit time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: cubic metre per second (m³/s)

\---

\## Penstock

A pressure conduit that carries water from an intake or reservoir toward a Hydro Turbine.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Surge Tank

A hydraulic chamber that reduces pressure transients and provides temporary water storage in a Hydro system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Nuclear Fission

The splitting of a heavy atomic nucleus with release of Energy and neutrons.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Fission

\*\*Unit\*\*: Energy in joule (J)

\---

\## Nuclear Reactor

A controlled system in which nuclear fission produces Heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Reactor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Thermal rating in watt thermal (Wt)

\---

\## Control Rod

A neutron-absorbing component used to control or shut down a nuclear chain reaction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Diesel Power Plant

A plant in which a Compression-Ignition Engine drives an Alternator.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DG Set where applicable

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere (VA) or watt (W)

\---

\## Gas Turbine

A prime mover in which compressed, heated gas expands through Turbine stages to produce shaft Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GT

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Open-Cycle Gas Turbine

A Gas-Turbine arrangement that discharges exhaust after expansion without a steam Heat-Recovery cycle.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OCGT

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Combined-Cycle Power Plant

A plant that combines a Gas-Turbine cycle with a steam cycle using recovered exhaust Heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CCPP or CCGT

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Heat-Recovery Steam Generator

A Heat Exchanger that uses Gas-Turbine exhaust to produce steam for a Combined-Cycle Plant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HRSG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Thermal duty in watt (W); steam flow in kg/s

\---

\## Solar Photovoltaic Cell

A semiconductor device that converts light directly into DC electricity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PV Cell

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt peak (Wp)

\---

\## Solar Array

An electrically connected group of Solar PV modules.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PV Array

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt peak (Wp)

\---

\## Solar Inverter

A Power Electronic converter that converts Solar DC output into controlled AC output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: INV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere (VA) or watt (W)

\---

\## Wind Turbine

A machine that captures part of the kinetic Energy of wind and converts it into mechanical and electrical Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: WT

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Swept Area

The circular area traced by Wind-Turbine blades as the Rotor turns.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A

\*\*Unit\*\*: square metre (m²)

\---

\## Biomass

Organic material used as an Energy feedstock through combustion, gasification or biological conversion.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: kilogram (kg) or Energy-content unit

\---

\## Biogas

A combustible gas mixture produced by anaerobic digestion of organic material.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: cubic metre (m³) or Energy-content unit

\---

\## Tidal Energy

Energy obtained from periodic sea-level difference or tidal-water movement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J) or watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Geothermal Energy

Thermal Energy obtained from within the Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: joule (J) or watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Gross Generation

The total electrical output measured at Generator terminals before subtracting Plant Auxiliaries.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pgross

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Net Generation

The electrical output remaining after Plant Auxiliary consumption is subtracted from Gross Generation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pnet

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W) or watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Auxiliary Power

Power consumed by pumps, fans, conveyors, controls and other equipment needed to operate a Power Plant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Paux

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Thermal Efficiency

The ratio of useful output Energy to thermal Energy input over the same boundary and period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ηth

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Base Load

The relatively continuous minimum demand on a Power System during a stated period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pbase where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Peak Load

The highest or elevated system demand occurring for a limited period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ppeak

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Intermediate Load

The demand between Base and Peak levels that varies through the normal operating period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Operating Reserve

Generation or responsive capacity available to cover unexpected imbalance, forecast error or equipment loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Reserve

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Load Curve

A chronological graph of Power demand against time.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P(t)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in watt (W); time in hour (h) or second (s)

\---

\## Load Duration Curve

A graph of load values arranged from highest to lowest against cumulative duration.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LDC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in watt (W); duration in hour (h)

\---

\## Maximum Demand

The highest demand measured or averaged according to a specified interval during a stated period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pmax or MD

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Average Load

The Energy used during a period divided by the duration of that period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pavg

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Load Factor

The ratio of Average Load to Maximum Demand during the same period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Capacity Factor

The ratio of actual generated Energy to rated capacity multiplied by total hours in the period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Plant Factor

A plant-utilization ratio whose exact definition must be stated because terminology varies among sources.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PF or Plant Factor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Plant Use Factor

The ratio of actual generated Energy to rated capacity multiplied by the hours the plant actually operated.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PUF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or percent (%)

\---

\## Installed Capacity

The sum of nameplate ratings of installed generating units within a defined system boundary.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pinstalled

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Available Capacity

The generating capacity capable of service under current equipment, fuel, resource and operating conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pavailable

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Derated Capacity

The reduced achievable rating of equipment caused by condition, environment, fuel, resource or operating limitation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pderated

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Ramp Rate

The rate at which a generating unit can increase or decrease active-Power output.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: dP/dt

\*\*Unit\*\*: megawatt per minute (MW/min) or applicable Power/time unit

\---

\## Economic Dispatch

The allocation of required Generation among available units at acceptable cost while respecting system constraints.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ED

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cogeneration

The combined production of electricity and useful thermal Energy from the same fuel input.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CHP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Electrical and thermal Power in watt (W)

\---

\## Black Start

The capability to start a generating resource without relying on the external Grid supply and assist system restoration.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Black-Start Capability

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Independent Power Producer

A non-utility or private generating entity that sells electrical Power under applicable commercial and regulatory arrangements.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IPP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Single Buyer

An entity that purchases electricity from generators and sells it onward within a defined market structure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Power Purchase Agreement

A contract defining the commercial and technical terms for purchasing electricity from a generating project.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PPA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Flue-Gas Desulfurization

A process used to remove sulfur compounds from combustion flue gas.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FGD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Removal efficiency in percent (%) where stated

\---

\## Thermal Pollution

An undesirable change in environmental water temperature caused by Heat discharge.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔT where quantified

\*\*Unit\*\*: kelvin (K) or degree Celsius (°C) difference

\---

\## Permit to Work

A formal authorization defining equipment, hazards, isolations, precautions and responsibility for a controlled task.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PTW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled procedure that isolates hazardous Energy and prevents unauthorized re-energization.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## End of Chapter 21 Glossary
