\# Practical Learning Suggestions

Students are encouraged to watch demonstrations on the following topics:

\- Tracing a Thermal-Plant steam and water cycle
\- Identifying Hydro-Plant intake, Penstock, Turbine and Tailrace
\- Comparing Nuclear and fossil steam-cycle Heat sources
\- Demonstrating Diesel Generator starting and loading
\- Comparing Open-Cycle and Combined-Cycle Gas Turbines
\- Identifying Solar modules, Combiner Boxes, inverters and Transformers
\- Demonstrating Wind-Turbine blade pitch and Generator control
\- Comparing Biomass combustion, gasification and Biogas routes
\- Drawing Energy-conversion block diagrams
\- Converting a daily Load Curve into a Load Duration Curve
\- Calculating Energy from area under a Load Curve
\- Solving Load-Factor and Capacity-Factor problems
\- Comparing Base, Intermediate and Peak scheduling
\- Reading dated BPDB generation statistics
\- Comparing installed, derated and available capacity
\- Demonstrating stack-emission and cooling-water monitoring
\- Applying Permit-to-Work and multi-Energy Lockout/Tagout
\- Reviewing Captive Generation and Cogeneration case studies

\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Learn the complete Energy-conversion chain.

2\. Classify renewable, non-renewable, dispatchable and variable sources.

3\. Study the major components of each conventional Power Plant.

4\. Compare Solar, Wind, Biomass, Tidal and Geothermal systems.

5\. Draw the Energy path of every plant from memory.

6\. Distinguish Gross, Auxiliary and Net Generation.

7\. Learn Base, Intermediate and Peak Load duties.

8\. Practice Load-Curve and Load-Duration-Curve construction.

9\. Solve Load-Factor, Capacity-Factor and Plant-Use-Factor problems.

10\. Compare plants using lifecycle technical and economic criteria.

11\. Verify Bangladesh sector data from dated official sources.

12\. Study environmental mitigation for each generation type.

13\. Practice electrical and process isolation steps.

14\. Relate generation reliability to industrial applications.

15\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

16\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-22 Transmission and Distribution.
