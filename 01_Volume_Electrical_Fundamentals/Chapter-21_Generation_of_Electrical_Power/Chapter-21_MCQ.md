\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main purpose of an electrical generating station?

A. To convert primary Energy into electrical Energy
B. To consume all electrical Energy locally
C. To increase load without supplying Power
D. To store only mechanical Energy

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. To convert primary Energy into electrical Energy\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A generating station converts chemical, nuclear, hydraulic, solar, wind or another form of primary Energy into controlled electrical output.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B describes consumption, not generation.
\- C contradicts the supply function of a plant.
\- D ignores the required electrical output.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Generation is an Energy-conversion process.

\### MCQ-002

\*\*Question\*\*

Which machine normally converts mechanical shaft Power into AC electrical Power in a large Power Plant?

A. Transformer
B. Synchronous Generator
C. Rectifier
D. Circuit Breaker

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Synchronous Generator\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A prime mover drives a Synchronous Generator, which produces AC electricity by Electromagnetic Induction.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A changes AC Voltage but does not generate Energy.
\- C converts AC to DC.
\- D switches or interrupts a circuit.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Prime mover → Generator → electrical Power.

C. Auxiliary Power minus Gross Power

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A incorrectly adds internal consumption.

\- D is dimensionally incorrect.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Pnet = Pgross − Pauxiliary.

What does the area under a Load Curve represent?

B. Frequency

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\### MCQ-015

\*\*Question\*\*

A 50 MW plant produces 18,000 MWh in 30 days. What is its Capacity Factor?

A. 25%
B. 50%
C. 72%
D. 100%

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. 50%\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Maximum possible Energy = 50 × 30 × 24 = 36,000 MWh. Capacity Factor = 18,000/36,000 = 0.50.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A results from an incorrect denominator.
\- C confuses the 720 period hours with a percentage.
\- D would require 36,000 MWh.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Capacity Factor = actual Energy ÷ rated maximum possible Energy.
