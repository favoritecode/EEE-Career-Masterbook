\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What are the two principal functions of a Transistor?

A. Switching and amplification
B. Mechanical rotation and heating
C. Earthing and insulation
D. Only AC generation

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Switching and amplification\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A small Transistor input can control a larger electrical output for electronic switching or signal amplification.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B describes electromechanical or thermal functions, not basic Transistor action.
\- C describes installation-safety functions.
\- D is not the sole or primary function of a Transistor.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Transistor = electronic switch or amplifier.

\### MCQ-003

\*\*Question\*\*

Which BJT Current relation is correct?

A. I_E = I_C + I_B
B. I_C = I_E + I_B
C. I_B = I_C + I_E
D. I_E = I_C − I_B

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. I_E = I_C + I_B\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Kirchhoff's Current Law at the Transistor gives Emitter Current equal to the sum of Collector and Base Current magnitudes.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B incorrectly makes Collector Current larger than Emitter Current.
\- C incorrectly defines Base Current as the sum.
\- D uses subtraction in the wrong relation.

\*\*Remember\*\*

The Emitter carries the total of Base and Collector Currents.

Why is the BJT Base made very thin and lightly doped?

Thin Base → less recombination → more carriers reach Collector.

\### MCQ-006

\*\*Question\*\*

Which BJT operating region is normally used for linear amplification?

A. Active region
B. Cutoff region
C. Saturation region
D. Reverse-breakdown region

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Active region\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

In the active region, the Emitter-Base junction is forward biased and Collector-Base junction is reverse biased, supporting controlled amplification.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is the approximate OFF state.
\- C is the approximate ON switching state.
\- D is generally unsafe and not normal linear operation.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Active = amplify; cutoff and saturation = switch.

\### MCQ-007

\*\*Question\*\*

In BJT saturation, Collector Current is mainly limited by what?

A. The external Load and supply circuit
B. Only the nominal value of β
C. Infinite input Resistance
D. The Op-Amp Slew Rate

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. The external Load and supply circuit\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Once saturated, the BJT no longer follows the active-region approximation I_C = βI_B; the Load circuit mainly sets Current.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is not a reliable saturation relation.
\- C is unrelated and false for a BJT Base.
\- D is an Op-Amp dynamic parameter.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Do not use βI_B blindly in saturation.

\### MCQ-030

\*\*Question\*\*

Why is a Voltage Follower useful between a high-impedance sensor and a lower-impedance Load?

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*
