\# Chapter 27

\# Glossary

\## Transistors, Amplifiers and Operational Amplifiers

\---

\## Transistor

A three-terminal Semiconductor device used mainly for amplification and switching.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Bipolar Junction Transistor

A two-junction Transistor in which electrons and Holes both participate in conduction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BJT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## NPN Transistor

A BJT formed from N-Type Emitter, P-Type Base and N-Type Collector regions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NPN

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## PNP Transistor

A BJT formed from P-Type Emitter, N-Type Base and P-Type Collector regions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PNP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Emitter

The BJT region and terminal designed to inject majority carriers into the Base.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Base

The thin BJT control region located between Emitter and Collector.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Collector

The BJT region and terminal that collects carriers crossing the Base and commonly handles significant Voltage and heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Emitter Current

The total BJT terminal Current whose magnitude equals the sum of Collector and Base Currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I_E

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Base Current

The Current entering or leaving the BJT Base terminal according to device polarity and operating condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I_B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Collector Current

The Current at the BJT Collector terminal, controlled by junction conditions and the external circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I_C

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Common-Emitter Current Gain

The ratio of DC Collector Current to Base Current under specified active-region conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: β or h_FE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Cutoff Region

The approximate BJT OFF region in which normal Base and Collector Currents are very small except for leakage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Active Region

The BJT region used for approximately linear amplification, with forward-biased Emitter-Base and reverse-biased Collector-Base junctions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Saturation Region

The approximate BJT ON region in which both junctions are forward biased and the external circuit mainly limits Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Collector-Emitter Saturation Voltage

The small Collector-to-Emitter Voltage across a saturated BJT under specified Current and drive conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_CE(sat)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Biasing

The establishment of DC terminal Voltages and Currents required for a selected Transistor operating condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Quiescent Point

The no-signal DC operating point of an amplifier stage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Q-Point

\*\*Unit\*\*: Specified by Volt (V) and Ampere (A)

\---

\## Thermal Runaway

A self-reinforcing temperature and Current increase that can overheat or destroy a Semiconductor device.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Common Base

A BJT configuration in which the Base is common to input and output signal paths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Common Emitter

A BJT configuration in which the Emitter is common and the output Voltage is normally phase-inverted.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Common Collector

A BJT configuration in which the Collector is common and output is taken from the Emitter.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Emitter Follower

The Common-Collector BJT circuit whose Emitter Voltage follows its Base-input Voltage with Gain near unity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Input Characteristic

A graph relating input Current and input Voltage while a specified output condition is controlled.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Axis-dependent

\---

\## Output Characteristic

A family of curves relating output Current and output Voltage for selected input levels.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Axis-dependent

\---

\## Transfer Characteristic

A relationship between an output quantity and the input quantity controlling it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Axis-dependent

\---

\## DC Load Line

A line showing the DC Current-Voltage combinations permitted by the supply and Load circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) and Ampere (A) axes

\---

\## Field Effect Transistor

A voltage-controlled Semiconductor device whose Gate Electric Field controls a Source-to-Drain channel.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: FET

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Gate

The FET control terminal whose Voltage influences channel conduction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Source

The FET terminal from which majority carriers enter the channel under the selected operating convention.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Drain

The FET terminal through which channel carriers leave under the selected operating convention.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: D

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Gate-to-Source Voltage

The Voltage between the FET Gate and Source terminals that controls channel condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_GS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Drain Current

The FET Current flowing through the Drain terminal and channel under specified bias.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I_D

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Junction Field Effect Transistor

A FET whose reverse-biased PN-Junction Gate controls channel width.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: JFET

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor

A FET having an electrically insulated Gate separated from the Semiconductor channel region by a dielectric.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MOSFET

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Enhancement MOSFET

A MOSFET that normally requires sufficient Gate-to-Source Voltage to create or enhance a conducting channel.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E-MOSFET

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Threshold Voltage

The Gate-to-Source Voltage associated with the beginning of a specified MOSFET conduction condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_GS(th)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Drain-to-Source On Resistance

The effective MOSFET Drain-to-Source Resistance in the ON state at stated Gate drive, Current and temperature conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R_DS(on)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Amplifier

A circuit that uses supply energy to produce an output signal with increased Voltage, Current or Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Voltage Gain

The ratio of output Voltage to input Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_v

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Current Gain

The ratio of output Current to input Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_i

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Power Gain

The ratio of output signal Power to input signal Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_p

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Decibel

A logarithmic unit used to express Power ratios or related Voltage and Current ratios under stated impedance conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: dB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB)

\---

\## Frequency Response

The variation of circuit Gain and phase with signal frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H(f) or A(f)

\*\*Unit\*\*: Gain and phase versus hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Lower Cutoff Frequency

The lower frequency boundary of a specified amplifier passband.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f_L

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Upper Cutoff Frequency

The upper frequency boundary of a specified amplifier passband.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: f_H

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Bandwidth

The frequency interval over which a circuit meets stated performance limits; for a basic band-limited amplifier, BW = f_H − f_L.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Clipping

Waveform distortion caused when an amplifier output cannot follow the demanded signal beyond its available range.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Operational Amplifier

A high-Gain differential Voltage amplifier normally used with feedback.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Op-Amp or A

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Open-Loop Gain

The Op-Amp differential Voltage Gain without an external feedback network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_OL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Negative Feedback

The return of an output-derived signal in a direction that opposes the input difference and controls closed-loop behavior.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Closed-Loop Gain

The overall Gain of an amplifier while its external feedback network is connected.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_CL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Inverting Input

The Op-Amp input for which a positive differential change tends to produce a negative output change.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_−

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Non-Inverting Input

The Op-Amp input for which a positive differential change tends to produce a positive output change.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_+

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Differential Input Voltage

The Voltage difference between Non-Inverting and Inverting Op-Amp inputs.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_d = V_+ − V_−

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Input Offset Voltage

The small differential DC input Voltage needed to produce a specified Op-Amp output condition, commonly zero output in an idealized test.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_OS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Input Bias Current

The DC Current required at practical Op-Amp input terminals, commonly specified as an average according to datasheet convention.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I_B

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere (A)

\---

\## Common-Mode Rejection Ratio

The ability of a differential amplifier to reject a signal common to both inputs relative to differential amplification.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CMRR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or decibel (dB)

\---

\## Power-Supply Rejection Ratio

A measure of how well an amplifier rejects output or offset changes caused by supply-Voltage variation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PSRR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Decibel (dB) or datasheet-specified form

\---

\## Slew Rate

The maximum rate at which an Op-Amp output Voltage can change under specified conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt per microsecond (V/μs)

\---

\## Gain-Bandwidth Product

A frequency-related Op-Amp parameter approximately equal to closed-loop Noise Gain multiplied by Bandwidth for suitable single-pole conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: GBW or GBP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Inverting Amplifier

An Op-Amp configuration with ideal Gain equal to negative feedback Resistance divided by input Resistance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_v = −R_f/R_in

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Non-Inverting Amplifier

An Op-Amp configuration with input at the Non-Inverting terminal and ideal Gain equal to one plus the feedback-resistor ratio.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_v = 1 + R_f/R_g

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Voltage Follower

A unity-Voltage-Gain Non-Inverting Op-Amp configuration used for buffering and impedance isolation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A_v = 1

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Virtual Ground

A feedback-controlled node held approximately at ground potential without a direct physical ground connection under valid linear ideal conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Buffer

A circuit that isolates stages by providing high input Resistance and low output Resistance, commonly with near-unity Voltage Gain.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---
