\## Semiconductor Fundamentals, Diodes and Rectifiers

\---

\## About This Chapter

This chapter was prepared using established Semiconductor-device and electronic-circuit textbooks together with IEC standards and university learning resources. These references support deeper study of Energy Bands, Intrinsic and Extrinsic Semiconductors, PN Junction behavior, Diode characteristics, special-purpose Diodes, Rectifiers, Ripple, filtering and basic Voltage regulation.

Ideal Rectifier equations are teaching models. Practical design must also use the selected component datasheet and account for forward Voltage, leakage, Peak Inverse Voltage, surge Current, reverse recovery, junction temperature, transformer regulation, Load variation, capacitor tolerance and safety requirements.

\# Reference Use Guide

\- Use Boylestad and Nashelsky or Floyd for a beginner-friendly first study of Diodes and Rectifiers.
\- Use Sedra and Smith or Jaeger and Blalock for engineering circuit models and deeper analysis.
\- Use Neamen, Kasap, or Streetman and Banerjee for Energy Bands, carrier transport and PN Junction physics.
\- Use Mohan or Rashid when studying practical power Rectifiers and device ratings.
\- Use the applicable IEC 60747 publication and the current manufacturer datasheet when selecting or testing a real Diode.
\- Confirm that the standard edition, national adoption and safety requirements are current before professional use.
