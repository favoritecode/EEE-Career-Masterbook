\## Electrical Wiring, Cables and Installation

\---

\## About This Chapter

This chapter was prepared using established Electrical Installation and Power-Cable textbooks, international Low-Voltage installation and Cable standards, industrial Power-System guidance and official Bangladesh regulatory resources. The references support deeper study of wiring systems, Cable construction and sizing, conductor identification, joints, accessories, domestic and industrial installation, inspection, testing and troubleshooting.

Electrical rules, colour identification, permitted wiring methods and approval procedures vary by jurisdiction. Practical work must follow the current locally adopted code, authority requirements, manufacturer instructions and project specification.

\# Reference Notes

\- A wiring system includes Conductors, containment, accessories, protection and documentation.

\- Wiring method selection depends on environment, mechanical risk, fire performance and maintainability.

\- Conductor ampacity must be corrected for installation conditions.

\- Cable size must satisfy thermal, Voltage-drop, fault and protective-device requirements.

\- Armour provides mechanical protection; shielding controls electromagnetic interference.

\- Common IEC-based colours may differ from local or legacy identification.

\- Colour does not prove that a circuit is de-energized.

\- Cable joints require compatible connectors, specified tools, torque and environmental sealing.

\- Continuity and Insulation-Resistance tests assess different properties.

\- Sensitive equipment may require disconnection before insulation testing.

\- Never bypass a protective device to hide a wiring fault.

\- IEC 60364 provides an international framework, but local law and adopted rules control practical work.

\- Chapter-24 continues with Earthing, Protection, Switchgear and Circuit Breakers.
