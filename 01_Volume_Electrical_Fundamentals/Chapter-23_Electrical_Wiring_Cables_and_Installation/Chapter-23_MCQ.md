\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main purpose of an Electrical Wiring system?

A. Safely distribute electricity to loads
B. Generate mechanical Energy only
C. Increase supply frequency
D. Measure room temperature

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Safely distribute electricity to loads\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Wiring carries electrical Energy through selected Conductors, accessories and protection from a supply point to utilization equipment.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is a machine function.
\- C is not controlled by building wiring.
\- D is unrelated to Power distribution.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Safe wiring requires ampacity, protection, insulation and acceptable Voltage drop.

\### MCQ-002

\*\*Question\*\*

Which conductor normally carries fault Current but not normal load Current?

A. Protective Conductor
B. Line Conductor
C. Neutral Conductor in every circuit
D. Signal Conductor

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B normally carries load Current.
\- C may carry normal load Current.
\- D serves control or communication duty.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Protective Conductor = safety path, not a normal load path.

\### MCQ-011

\*\*Question\*\*

For a conductor of fixed material and length, increasing cross-sectional area causes Resistance to:

A. Decrease
B. Increase
C. Become infinite
D. Depend only on colour

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Decrease\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

From R = ρL/A, Resistance is inversely proportional to cross-sectional area.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B reverses the relation.
\- C has no basis for a larger conductor.
\- D is unrelated to electrical Resistance.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Larger area → lower R and lower Voltage drop.
