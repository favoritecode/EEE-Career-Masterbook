\# Chapter 23

\# Glossary

\## Electrical Wiring, Cables and Installation

\---

\## Electrical Wiring

A system of Conductors, Cables, containment, accessories and protection that distributes electricity to loads.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Wiring

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Wiring System

A defined method of routing, supporting and protecting Conductors or Cables.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Final Circuit

A circuit connected directly to utilization equipment or Socket-Outlets after the final protective device.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Line Conductor

A conductor energized relative to Neutral or Earth during normal operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: L

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Neutral Conductor

A conductor connected to the system Neutral point and able to carry operating Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Protective Conductor

A conductor provided for electrical safety and fault-current return.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Cleat Wiring

A wiring method in which insulated Conductors are supported on insulating cleats.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Batten Wiring

A wiring method in which sheathed Cable is clipped to a supporting batten.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Casing-and-Capping Wiring

A wiring method using a covered channel to contain insulated Conductors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Conduit

A tube or closed channel used to route and protect insulated Conductors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Diameter in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Surface Conduit

Conduit installed visibly on a building or equipment surface.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in metre (m)

\---

\## Concealed Conduit

Conduit embedded within a wall, floor or ceiling.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in metre (m)

\---

\## Cable Tray

An open structural system that supports groups of Cables.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Width in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Trunking

An enclosed wiring containment with a removable cover.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Size in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Electrical Cable

One or more insulated Conductors with protective coverings for transmitting Power or signals.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage in volt (V); area in mm²

\---

\## Single-Core Cable

A Cable containing one insulated Current-carrying conductor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Multi-Core Cable

A Cable containing two or more separately insulated Conductors within one overall covering.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Number of cores; area in mm²

\---

\## Flexible Cord

A flexible, fine-stranded insulated Cable used for portable or movable equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Armoured Cable

A Cable containing a protective metallic armour layer against specified mechanical stress.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SWA/STA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Shielded Cable

A Cable with a conductive screen that limits electromagnetic coupling.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Shield coverage in percent (%)

\---

\## Fire-Resistant Cable

A Cable designed to maintain circuit integrity for specified conditions during a standard fire test.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Time in minute (min)

\---

\## Conductor

A material or component intended to carry electric Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Copper Conductor

A conductor made mainly of copper and valued for high conductivity and reliable connections.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cu

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Aluminium Conductor

A lightweight conductor made mainly of aluminium and requiring compatible connections.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Al

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Solid Conductor

A conductor formed from one solid wire.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Stranded Conductor

A conductor formed from multiple wires combined to improve flexibility and handling.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Insulation

A dielectric material that electrically separates a conductor from other Conductors and Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Insulation Resistance in megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Polyvinyl Chloride

A thermoplastic insulation and sheath material widely used in general wiring.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PVC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cross-Linked Polyethylene

A thermoset Cable insulation with strong electrical and thermal performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XLPE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Ethylene Propylene Rubber

A flexible thermoset insulation material used in particular Cable applications.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EPR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cable Screen

A conductive layer used to control electric stress or electromagnetic fields.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A), where applicable

\---

\## Bedding

An inner Cable layer that cushions and protects components beneath armour.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Thickness in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Armour

A metallic Cable layer that provides specified mechanical protection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²), where applicable

\---

\## Outer Sheath

The external Cable covering that resists moisture, abrasion and environmental exposure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Thickness in millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Cable Size

The nominal conductor cross-sectional area used to identify a Cable conductor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S

\*\*Unit\*\*: square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## Design Current

The Current expected to flow in a circuit under its intended design load.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ib

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Current-Carrying Capacity

The maximum continuous Current permitted under stated installation conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iz

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Ampacity

Another term for the permitted Current-Carrying Capacity of a conductor or Cable.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iz

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Wire Gauge

A standardized designation that identifies wire diameter or size.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: AWG or gauge number

\*\*Unit\*\*: Gauge number or square millimetre (mm²)

\---

\## American Wire Gauge

A wire-size system in which a smaller gauge number generally indicates a larger conductor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: AWG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Gauge number

\---

\## Correction Factor

A multiplier applied to a tabulated Cable rating for actual installation conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless

\---

\## Derating

Reduction of permitted equipment or Cable loading because of temperature, grouping or other conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Factor or percent (%)

\---

\## Voltage Drop

The reduction in Voltage along a conductor caused by Current and circuit Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V) or percent (%)

\---

\## Short-Circuit Withstand

The ability of a conductor or Cable to survive specified fault Current until protection clears it.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: I²t

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere-squared second (A²s)

\---

\## Cable Joint

An electrical and mechanical connection between Cable lengths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Cable Termination

A prepared Cable end connected to equipment, an accessory or a Busbar.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Compression Lug

A terminal connector permanently compressed around a conductor with a specified tool and die.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Conductor area in mm²

\---

\## Cable Gland

A device that secures, seals and may Earth or bond a Cable at an enclosure entry.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Thread or Cable size

\---

\## Bimetallic Connector

A connector designed to join dissimilar conductor metals such as copper and aluminium.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Conductor area in mm²

\---

\## Terminal Torque

The manufacturer-specified turning moment applied to a terminal fastener.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: T

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton metre (N·m)

\---

\## Bend Radius

The minimum permitted radius of a Cable bend without unacceptable damage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: r

\*\*Unit\*\*: millimetre (mm)

\---

\## Pulling Tension

The mechanical force applied while drawing a Cable through a route.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: F

\*\*Unit\*\*: newton (N)

\---

\## Switch

A device that makes or breaks a circuit within its stated rating.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: S

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Socket-Outlet

A fixed accessory that receives a matching Plug to supply movable equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Plug

An accessory with pins that engages a matching Socket-Outlet.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Distribution Board

An assembly that receives a supply and divides it into protected outgoing circuits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DB

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Junction Box

An enclosure that houses and protects conductor joints or branch connections.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: JB

\*\*Unit\*\*: IP rating; dimensions in mm

\---

\## Ingress Protection Rating

A code describing enclosure protection against access, solid objects and water.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Code number

\---

\## Domestic Wiring

Electrical Wiring installed within a dwelling for lighting, Socket and fixed-appliance loads.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A)

\---

\## Industrial Wiring

Power, control and instrumentation wiring installed for industrial equipment and processes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); ampere (A)

\---

\## Motor Control Center

An assembly containing Motor switching, protection and control units.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCC

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A); volt (V)

\---

\## Circuit Schedule

A document or label identifying circuits, loads, Cable sizes and protective devices in a board.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Continuity Test

A low-resistance test used to confirm an unbroken conductor path.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Insulation-Resistance Test

A DC Voltage test used to measure electrical Resistance between Conductors and to Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR test

\*\*Unit\*\*: megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Insulation Tester

An instrument that applies a specified DC test Voltage and measures high Resistance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V); megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Polarity Test

A test confirming that Line, Neutral and switched Conductors are connected correctly.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Phase Sequence

The order in which Three-Phase Voltages reach their corresponding positive peaks.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ABC/RST

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Earth-Fault Loop Impedance

The total Impedance of the path taken by Earth-Fault Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Zs

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Short Circuit

An unintended low-impedance connection that may produce very high Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SC

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Open Circuit

A broken or disconnected path that prevents intended Current flow.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Overload

Current exceeding the designed normal capacity of a circuit without a Short Circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OL

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Earth Leakage

Unintended Current flowing from live parts toward Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IΔ

\*\*Unit\*\*: milliampere (mA)

\---

\## Loose Connection

A connection with inadequate contact pressure that may develop high Resistance and Heat.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Resistance in ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Residual Current Device

A device that disconnects when residual Current exceeds specified operating conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RCD

\*\*Unit\*\*: milliampere (mA)

\---

\## Lockout/Tagout

A controlled method of isolating Energy sources and preventing unauthorized reconnection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LOTO

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Permit-to-Work

A formal authorization defining the equipment, hazards, controls and work boundaries.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PTW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## As-Built Drawing

A drawing updated to show the installation as actually completed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Drawing scale

\---

\## Competent Person

A person with sufficient knowledge, skill, training and experience for the assigned electrical work.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## IEC 60364

The IEC standard series providing a framework for Low-Voltage electrical installations.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IEC 60364

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\# End of Chapter 23 Glossary
