\## Electrical Wiring, Cables and Installation

\---

\## About This Section

The following educational resources are recommended for strengthening this chapter. Select videos with clear wiring diagrams, Cable cutaways, current-rating and Voltage-drop calculations, approved termination demonstrations, domestic and industrial single-line diagrams, safe isolation, testing and systematic fault diagnosis.

Electrical installation, live testing and fault work must be demonstrated only by competent persons using approved isolation, PPE, instruments and work-control procedures. Students must not copy unsafe demonstrations or work on energized circuits without formal authorization and supervision.

\# Practical Learning Suggestions

Students are encouraged to watch supervised demonstrations on the following topics:

\- Tracing a circuit from supply to final load
\- Comparing Cleat, Batten and Conduit Wiring samples
\- Identifying common Cable types and core arrangements
\- Examining an armoured Cable cutaway
\- Reading Cable nameplate and drum data
\- Calculating Single-Phase and Three-Phase load Current
\- Applying Cable correction factors
\- Calculating Voltage drop using tabulated values
\- Comparing mm² conductor size with AWG
\- Identifying IEC-based conductor colours and legacy warnings
\- Stripping Cable without damaging conductor strands
\- Crimping a lug with the correct die and tool
\- Installing a Cable gland and armour bond
\- Reading a domestic Distribution Board schedule
\- Tracing an industrial MCC Feeder
\- Applying safe isolation and Lockout/Tagout
\- Performing continuity and Insulation-Resistance tests
\- Confirming polarity and Three-Phase sequence
\- Locating an Open Circuit on a fault board
\- Using thermography to recognize a high-resistance joint

\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Learn the complete supply-to-load wiring path.

2\. Compare all major wiring systems.

3\. Identify Cable types and constructional layers.

4\. Calculate design Current for common loads.

5\. Apply ampacity correction factors.

6\. Check Voltage drop and fault withstand.

7\. Distinguish metric conductor area from AWG.

8\. Memorize common conductor colours with the local-variation warning.

9\. Study correct jointing, crimping, gland and torque practice.

10\. Identify Switch, Socket, Plug, DB and Junction Box functions.

11\. Draw basic domestic and industrial single-line diagrams.

12\. Practice the complete safe installation sequence.

13\. Learn the purpose of each inspection and test.

14\. Diagnose Short, Open, leakage, overload and loose-connection faults.

15\. Review IEC and locally adopted installation requirements.

16\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

17\. Review the Glossary and prepare for Chapter-24 Earthing, Protection, Switchgear and Circuit Breakers.
