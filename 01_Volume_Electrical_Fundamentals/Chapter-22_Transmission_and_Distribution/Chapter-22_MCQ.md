\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

What is the main function of a Power Transmission system?

A. Bulk transfer of electrical Power
B. Final connection to one lamp only
C. Production of mechanical Energy only
D. Measurement of room temperature

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Bulk transfer of electrical Power\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Transmission moves large blocks of Power from generating stations or Grid interconnections to major Substations and load centers.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B describes a final utilization connection.
\- C is not the function of an electrical network.
\- D is unrelated to Power transfer.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Transmission transfers bulk Power; Distribution serves consumers.

\### MCQ-002

\*\*Question\*\*

For fixed Three-Phase active Power and Power Factor, increasing line Voltage causes line Current to:

A. Increase proportionally
B. Decrease inversely
C. Remain infinite
D. Become independent of Power

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*B. Decrease inversely\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

From I = P/(√3VLcosφ), Current decreases when Voltage increases for the same P and Power Factor.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- A reverses the relationship.
\- C has no physical basis.
\- D ignores the Power equation.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Higher V → lower I for the same transferred Power.

\### MCQ-003

\*\*Question\*\*

Line Copper loss is proportional to:

A. I²R
B. V/R only
C. 1/I² under all conditions
D. Frequency only

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. I²R\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Resistive conductor loss per phase is I²R; the balanced Three-Phase total is 3I²R.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is not the loss expression.
\- C is the opposite of Current-squared behavior.
\- D ignores Current and Resistance.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Doubling Current makes I²R loss four times for the same R.
