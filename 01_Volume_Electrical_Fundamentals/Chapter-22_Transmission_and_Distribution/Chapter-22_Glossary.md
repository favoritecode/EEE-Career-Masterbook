\# Chapter 22

\# Glossary

\## Transmission and Distribution

\---

\## Power Transmission

The bulk transfer of electrical Power at High Voltage between generating stations, Grid connections and major Substations.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Transmission

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in watt (W); Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Power Distribution

The network process of delivering electrical Power from Substations to consumer supply points.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Distribution

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in watt (W); Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## High Voltage

A Voltage classification above lower utilization levels, defined precisely by the applicable standard or utility.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Extra-High Voltage

A very high Transmission-Voltage class whose exact boundary is defined by the relevant standard or network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EHV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Medium Voltage

A Voltage class commonly used for Primary Distribution, with boundaries defined by standards and utilities.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Low Voltage

A Voltage class commonly used for Secondary Distribution and utilization, defined by the applicable standard.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: LV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Overhead Line

A line whose Conductors are supported above ground by Towers or Poles and Insulators.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OHL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in metre (m) or kilometre (km)

\---

\## Underground Cable

An insulated electrical Cable installed below ground in a trench, duct, tunnel or direct-buried arrangement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: UGC where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in metre (m) or kilometre (km)

\---

\## HVAC Transmission

Bulk Power Transmission using High-Voltage Alternating Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HVAC

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V), ampere (A) and watt (W)

\---

\## HVDC Transmission

Bulk Power Transmission using High-Voltage Direct Current and converter terminals.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: HVDC

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V), ampere (A) and watt (W)

\---

\## Converter Station

A station that converts AC to DC or DC to AC for an HVDC link.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Converter Station

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in watt (W), volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Transmission Conductor

A metallic path designed to carry line Current while satisfying electrical and mechanical limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Conductor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Area in square millimetre (mm²); Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Ampacity

The permitted continuous or specified Current-carrying capacity under defined thermal and installation conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Iz or applicable designation

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Transmission Tower

A structure that supports Overhead-Line Conductors, Insulators and Earth Wires at safe clearances.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Tower

\*\*Unit\*\*: Height in metre (m)

\---

\## Insulator

A component that mechanically supports an energized Conductor while limiting Current to a grounded structure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Insulator

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and mechanical load in newton (N)

\---

\## Insulator String

An assembly of Insulator units connected to obtain required electrical and mechanical performance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: String

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and newton (N)

\---

\## Earth Wire

A grounded Overhead Wire positioned to shield phase Conductors from lightning.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Shield Wire or GW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A) during surge or fault

\---

\## Optical Ground Wire

An Overhead Earth Wire containing optical fibers for communication and protection signaling.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OPGW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Right-of-Way

The controlled corridor required for safe construction, operation, access and clearance of a line.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ROW

\*\*Unit\*\*: Width or area in metre (m) or square metre (m²)

\---

\## Sag

The vertical distance between a Conductor support reference and the lowest point of the suspended Conductor.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Sag or d

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m)

\---

\## Span

The horizontal distance between adjacent Conductor supports.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: l or Span

\*\*Unit\*\*: metre (m)

\---

\## Corona

Partial ionization of air around a High-Voltage Conductor when the electric field exceeds a critical level.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Corona

\*\*Unit\*\*: Loss in watt (W); onset field in volt per metre (V/m)

\---

\## Resistance

The opposition of a Conductor to Current that produces Voltage drop and active-Power loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Resistivity

The material property relating conductor Resistance to length and cross-sectional area.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ρ

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm-metre (Ω·m)

\---

\## Skin Effect

The tendency of AC Current to concentrate toward a conductor's outer region as frequency increases.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Proximity Effect

The redistribution of AC Current in a Conductor caused by the magnetic fields of nearby Conductors.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Inductance

The property by which magnetic Flux linkage opposes a change in Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: L

\*\*Unit\*\*: henry (H)

\---

\## Inductive Reactance

The AC opposition produced by Inductance, equal to XL = 2πfL.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: XL

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Capacitance

The ability of separated Conductors to store electrical charge and electric-field Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C

\*\*Unit\*\*: farad (F)

\---

\## Charging Current

The AC Current drawn by line or Cable Capacitance when Voltage is applied.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IC

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A)

\---

\## Leakage Conductance

The Shunt parameter representing insulation leakage and dielectric conduction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: siemens (S)

\---

\## Series Impedance

The combined Resistance and Inductive Reactance of a line section.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Z = R + jωL

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Shunt Admittance

The combined leakage Conductance and Capacitive Susceptance of a line section.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Y = G + jωC

\*\*Unit\*\*: siemens (S)

\---

\## Short Transmission Line

A line for which Shunt Capacitance is neglected in the conventional basic model.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Short Line

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in kilometre (km)

\---

\## Medium Transmission Line

A line modeled with lumped Shunt Capacitance, commonly using Nominal-π or Nominal-T.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Medium Line

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in kilometre (km)

\---

\## Long Transmission Line

A line analyzed with continuously distributed Series and Shunt parameters.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Long Line

\*\*Unit\*\*: Length in kilometre (km)

\---

\## Nominal-π Model

A Medium-Line equivalent with Series Impedance between two equal halves of Shunt Admittance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: π Model

\*\*Unit\*\*: Z in ohm (Ω); Y in siemens (S)

\---

\## Nominal-T Model

A Medium-Line equivalent with Shunt Admittance between two equal halves of Series Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: T Model

\*\*Unit\*\*: Z in ohm (Ω); Y in siemens (S)

\---

\## ABCD Constants

Four network constants relating sending-end Voltage and Current to receiving-end Voltage and Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: A, B, C, D

\*\*Unit\*\*: A and D dimensionless; B in Ω; C in S

\---

\## Propagation Constant

A complex line quantity describing attenuation and phase change per unit length.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: γ

\*\*Unit\*\*: per metre (m⁻¹) or per kilometre (km⁻¹)

\---

\## Characteristic Impedance

The ratio associated with voltage and current waves on a uniform Transmission Line.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Zc

\*\*Unit\*\*: ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Ferranti Effect

The rise of receiving-end Voltage above sending-end Voltage on a lightly loaded or open long AC line because of line Capacitance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Not standardized

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage rise in volt (V) or percent (%)

\---

\## Primary Distribution

The Medium-Voltage network between Distribution Substations, Distribution Transformers and major MV consumers.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Primary Distribution

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Secondary Distribution

The Low-Voltage network between a Distribution Transformer and consumer Service Mains.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Secondary Distribution

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V)

\---

\## Distribution Transformer

A Transformer that reduces Primary Distribution Voltage to Secondary or utilization Voltage near loads.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DT

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Feeder

A Distribution circuit carrying Power from a Substation toward a distribution area or load point.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Feeder

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A); capacity in volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Distributor

A Distribution conductor or circuit with multiple load tappings along its route.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Distributor

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A); Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Service Main

The final electrical connection between a Distributor and an individual consumer supply point.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Service

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in ampere (A); Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Radial Distribution

A system in which a load normally has one supply path from its source.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Radial

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Ring-Main Distribution

A Distribution arrangement forming a loop that can provide an alternative supply direction.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ring Main or RMU network

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Interconnected Distribution

A network in which Feeders or Substations are connected to provide multiple supply paths.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Interconnected Network

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Normally Open Point

A switching point kept open during normal ring operation and used for reconfiguration or restoration.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NOP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Voltage Regulation

The change in Voltage between stated operating conditions, often expressed relative to a reference Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VR

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%) or volt (V)

\---

\## Voltage Drop

The reduction in Voltage caused by Current flowing through circuit Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔV

\*\*Unit\*\*: volt (V) or percent (%)

\---

\## Technical Loss

Electrical Energy loss caused by physical network and equipment behavior.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Etechnical loss

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt-hour (Wh) or percent (%)

\---

\## Non-Technical Loss

Apparent Energy loss caused by unauthorized use, metering, data or billing problems rather than physical conductor loss.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: NTL

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt-hour (Wh) or percent (%)

\---

\## Copper Loss

Current-dependent winding or conductor loss proportional approximately to I²R.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcu

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Core Loss

Hysteresis and eddy-current loss in an energized magnetic Core.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pcore or Pi

\*\*Unit\*\*: watt (W)

\---

\## Power-Factor Correction

The controlled supply or absorption of Reactive Power to improve Power Factor and reduce source Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PFC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Compensation in var (VAr)

\---

\## Phase Unbalance

A condition in which phase Voltages, Currents or angles are not equal according to the intended balanced system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VU or CU where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: percent (%)

\---

\## Harmonic

A sinusoidal component whose frequency is an integer multiple of the fundamental frequency.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: h

\*\*Unit\*\*: Order dimensionless; frequency in hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Electrical Grid

An interconnected network of generators, lines, Substations, Distribution systems and loads.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Grid

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Substation

A controlled network node where Power is transformed, switched, protected, measured and interconnected.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SS or S/S where defined

\*\*Unit\*\*: Capacity in volt-ampere (VA); Voltage in volt (V)

\---

\## Busbar

A common conductor that connects multiple incoming and outgoing circuits in a Substation or Switchboard.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Bus

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in ampere (A), volt (V) and fault Current

\---

\## Circuit Breaker

A switching device designed to make, carry and interrupt normal and specified fault Currents within its rating.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V), ampere (A) and breaking Current

\---

\## Disconnector

A mechanical switching device used to provide isolation and not normally intended to interrupt load Current unless specifically rated.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DS or Isolator

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and ampere (A)

\---

\## Current Transformer

An Instrument Transformer that reproduces a scaled Current for metering and protection with isolation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ratio in ampere/ampere (A/A)

\---

\## Voltage Transformer

An Instrument Transformer that reproduces a scaled Voltage for metering and protection with isolation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VT or PT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ratio in volt/volt (V/V)

\---

\## Surge Arrester

A protective device that limits transient overvoltage by diverting surge Current to Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SA or LA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated in volt (V) and surge Current in ampere (A)

\---

\## Protection Relay

A device or function that detects abnormal electrical conditions and initiates an appropriate trip or alarm.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Relay or ANSI device number

\*\*Unit\*\*: Setting depends on protection function

\---

\## Selectivity

The ability of protection to isolate the smallest practical faulty section while healthy sections remain energized.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Discrimination or Selectivity

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## SCADA

A system for supervisory monitoring, data acquisition and remote control of Power-System equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SCADA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Fault Level

The prospective fault magnitude or equivalent fault Power available at a network point under stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: If or Ssc

\*\*Unit\*\*: ampere (A) or volt-ampere (VA)

\---

\## Load Flow

The steady-state calculation of Bus Voltages, Power flows and losses in an electrical network.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Power Flow

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage in V; Power in W, var and VA

\---

\## End of Chapter 22 Glossary
