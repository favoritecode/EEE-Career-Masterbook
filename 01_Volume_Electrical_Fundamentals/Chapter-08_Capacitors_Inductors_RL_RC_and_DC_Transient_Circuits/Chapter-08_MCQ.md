\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

In which field does an ideal Capacitor store energy?

A. Electric Field
B. Magnetic Field
C. Gravitational Field
D. Mechanical Stress Field

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Electric Field\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

A Capacitor stores separated charge and energy in the Electric Field between its Conductive Plates.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B is incorrect because an Inductor stores energy in a Magnetic Field.
\- C is unrelated to ordinary Capacitor operation.
\- D does not describe the primary electrical energy-storage mechanism.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Capacitor → Electric Field

\### MCQ-010

\*\*Question\*\*

Which quantity cannot change instantaneously across an ideal Capacitor?

A. Capacitor Voltage
B. Capacitor Current
C. Resistor Current
D. Source Power

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Capacitor Voltage\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

An instantaneous Capacitor Voltage change would require infinite Current according to iC = C(dvC/dt).

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B may change abruptly if the external Circuit permits it.
\- C is not generally constrained by Capacitor Voltage continuity alone.
\- D can change when switching occurs.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Capacitor Voltage Is Continuous: vC(0⁺) = vC(0⁻)
