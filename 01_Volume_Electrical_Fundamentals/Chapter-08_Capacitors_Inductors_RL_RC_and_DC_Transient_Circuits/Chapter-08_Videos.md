\# Learning Tips

For best results, follow this learning sequence:

1\. Read the chapter.

2\. Review charge, Voltage, Current, Resistance, KCL and KVL.

3\. Watch animations of Electric-Field and Magnetic-Field energy storage.

4\. Learn Capacitor and Inductor combination formulas.

5\. Practice stored-energy calculations.

6\. Observe safe RC charging and discharging demonstrations.

7\. Observe safe RL energizing and Current-decay demonstrations.

8\. Practice identifying 0⁻, 0⁺ and final steady state.

9\. Calculate RC and RL Time Constants and key percentages.

10\. Solve first-order transient problems step by step.

11\. Complete the MCQs and practice the Viva questions.

12\. Review the Glossary and chapter summary.
