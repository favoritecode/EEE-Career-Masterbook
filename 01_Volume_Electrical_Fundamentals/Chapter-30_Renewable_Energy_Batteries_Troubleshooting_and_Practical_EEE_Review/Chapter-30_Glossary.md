\# Chapter 30

\# Glossary

\## Renewable Energy, Batteries, Troubleshooting and Practical EEE Review

\---

\## Renewable Energy

Energy obtained from natural sources that are replenished continuously or within a human timescale.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh), depending on context

\---

\## Installed Capacity

The rated maximum electrical Power of a generating plant or group of generators under specified conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P_rated

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W), commonly kW, MW or GW

\---

\## Capacity Factor

The ratio of actual Energy generated over a period to the Energy that would be generated at rated Power continuously over the same period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or Percent (%)

\---

\## Variable Renewable Energy

Renewable generation whose available output changes with weather or natural resource conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: VRE

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Dispatchable Generation

Generation whose output can be scheduled or adjusted within its technical and resource limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Distributed Generation

Electrical generation located near Loads or distributed throughout a Power Distribution System.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Solar Energy

Radiant and thermal Energy received from the Sun and converted into useful electrical or thermal Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Wind Energy

The kinetic Energy of moving air that may be converted into mechanical and electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E_wind

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Hydropower

Electrical Power produced by converting the potential or kinetic Energy of water through a turbine-generator system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: P_hydro

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt (W)

\---

\## Biomass Energy

Energy obtained from organic material through combustion, gasification, digestion or another conversion process.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Geothermal Energy

Useful Energy obtained from heat within the Earth.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E_geo

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Tidal Energy

Energy obtained from periodic tidal water-level changes or tidal currents.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E_tidal

\*\*Unit\*\*: Joule (J) or Watt-hour (Wh)

\---

\## Solar Photovoltaic

The direct conversion of light into electrical Energy using Semiconductor cells.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Solar PV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Photovoltaic Effect

The production of electrical Voltage and Current in a material after absorption of light and separation of charge carriers.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PV Effect

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## PV Cell

The smallest basic Semiconductor unit that converts light directly into DC electricity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cell

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## PV Module

An environmentally protected assembly of interconnected PV cells.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Module

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated Power in Watt-peak (Wp) under stated conditions

\---

\## PV String

A circuit of PV modules connected in series to obtain the required DC Voltage.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: String

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## PV Array

An assembly of electrically interconnected PV modules, strings and related supporting components.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Array

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated Power in Watt (W) or Watt-peak (Wp)

\---

\## Solar Irradiance

The Solar radiant Power received per unit area at a given instant.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: G

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt per Square Metre (W/m²)

\---

\## Solar Irradiation

Solar radiant Energy received per unit area over a period.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: H

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt-hour per Square Metre (Wh/m²) or Joule per Square Metre (J/m²)

\---

\## Maximum Power Point

The operating point on a PV current-voltage characteristic at which the product of Voltage and Current is maximum under the stated conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MPP

\*\*Unit\*\*: Power in Watt (W)

\---

\## Maximum Power Point Tracking

A control method that adjusts a PV operating point to extract near-maximum available Power.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MPPT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Performance Ratio

A normalized indicator comparing actual PV-system output with a reference output after accounting for available Solar resource.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PR

\*\*Unit\*\*: Dimensionless or Percent (%)

\---

\## On-Grid PV System

A Solar PV system connected to an electrical grid through a suitable grid-interactive Inverter and protection arrangement.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Grid-Tied PV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Off-Grid PV System

A Solar PV system operating independently of a utility grid, commonly with Battery storage or another local Energy source.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Stand-Alone PV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Hybrid PV System

A system coordinating PV with Battery storage and one or more additional sources such as grid or generator supply.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Hybrid PV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Charge Controller

A device that controls electrical charging from a source to a Battery within the intended operating limits.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Combiner Box

An enclosure in which multiple PV string circuits are combined and may be provided with switching, protection or monitoring.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CB or PV Combiner

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Solar Inverter

A Power Electronic Converter that changes PV or Battery DC into controlled AC for Loads or grid connection.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: INV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Rated Power in Watt (W) or Volt-Ampere (VA)

\---

\## Battery

An electrochemical device containing one or more cells that stores and supplies electrical Energy.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BAT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Battery Cell

A basic electrochemical unit containing positive and negative Electrodes, electrolyte and separator.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Cell

\*\*Unit\*\*: Nominal Voltage in Volt (V)

\---

\## Electrode

A conductive active interface at which an electrochemical reaction occurs in a cell.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Positive or Negative Electrode

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Electrolyte

An ion-conducting medium between Battery Electrodes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Separator

An ion-permeable insulating layer that helps prevent direct electrical contact between Battery Electrodes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Lead-Acid Battery

A rechargeable Battery using lead-based active materials and sulfuric-acid electrolyte.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Pb-Acid

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Lithium-Ion Battery

A family of rechargeable Batteries in which Lithium ions move between Electrodes during charging and discharging.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Li-ion

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Nickel-Cadmium Battery

A rechargeable alkaline Battery using Nickel and Cadmium active materials.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Ni-Cd

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Battery Capacity

The amount of electrical charge a Battery can deliver under specified discharge conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C_Ah

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ampere-hour (Ah)

\---

\## Battery Energy

The amount of electrical Energy a Battery can store or deliver under specified conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: E_bat

\*\*Unit\*\*: Watt-hour (Wh) or Joule (J)

\---

\## C-Rate

A charge or discharge Current expressed relative to rated Battery Capacity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: C-rate

\*\*Unit\*\*: Reciprocal Hour (h⁻¹) by dimensional interpretation; commonly stated as a multiple of C

\---

\## State of Charge

An estimate of the remaining available Battery charge relative to defined full Capacity.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SOC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Depth of Discharge

The proportion of defined Battery Capacity removed from a fully charged state.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DOD

\*\*Unit\*\*: Percent (%)

\---

\## Cycle Life

The number of specified charge-discharge cycles a Battery completes before reaching a defined end-of-life criterion.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: N_cycle

\*\*Unit\*\*: Cycle

\---

\## Calendar Life

The elapsed time before a Battery reaches a defined end-of-life condition, regardless of cycle count.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: t_cal

\*\*Unit\*\*: Year or Hour

\---

\## Internal Resistance

The effective internal opposition causing Battery Voltage drop and heat during Current flow.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R_int

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω)

\---

\## Battery Management System

An electronic system that monitors and controls Battery cells or modules for protection, balancing, estimation and communication.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: BMS

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Constant-Current Charging

A charging stage in which charger Current is regulated near a specified constant value.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Constant-Voltage Charging

A charging stage in which charger Voltage is regulated near a specified constant value while Current changes.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Voltage in Volt (V)

\---

\## Float Charging

Continuous or long-duration charging at a controlled Voltage intended to maintain a compatible stationary Battery near full charge.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: V_float

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V)

\---

\## Equalization Charge

A controlled charging treatment permitted for specified Battery designs to correct charge imbalance or electrolyte stratification according to manufacturer procedure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EQ

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Thermal Runaway

A self-accelerating condition in which heat generation increases temperature and causes further heat generation or damaging reactions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Electrical Troubleshooting

The safe systematic process of locating, identifying and correcting the cause of abnormal electrical operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Symptom

An observable indication of abnormal system behavior.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Root Cause

The underlying reason a failure or undesirable condition occurred and may recur if not corrected.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: RC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Hypothesis

A proposed fault explanation tested against evidence and expected system behavior.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Half-Split Method

A troubleshooting method that tests near the middle of a functional path to reduce the remaining search region.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Open Circuit

A circuit condition in which a break prevents intended Current flow.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: Open

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Short Circuit

An unintended low-impedance connection that can produce excessive Current.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: SC

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Overload

A condition in which equipment carries Current or mechanical Load above its normal rated operating value without necessarily having a short circuit.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A) or Load quantity as applicable

\---

\## Earth Fault

An unintended conductive connection between a live circuit and Earth or an Earthed conductive part.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: EF

\*\*Unit\*\*: Fault Current in Ampere (A)

\---

\## Phase Loss

Loss of one supply Phase in a Polyphase system.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PL

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Voltage Drop

The reduction in electrical Potential between two circuit points due to Current and circuit Impedance.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: ΔV

\*\*Unit\*\*: Volt (V) or Percent (%)

\---

\## Insulation Resistance

Resistance offered by insulation to leakage Current under specified test conditions.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: R_ins

\*\*Unit\*\*: Ohm (Ω), commonly Megohm (MΩ)

\---

\## Corrective Maintenance

Maintenance performed to restore equipment after a fault or failure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: CM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Preventive Maintenance

Planned maintenance performed at defined intervals or criteria to reduce failure probability.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Predictive Maintenance

Maintenance planned using measured equipment condition and trends to estimate developing faults.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PdM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Proactive Maintenance

Maintenance focused on eliminating underlying causes of recurring failure.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: PAM

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Thermography

The use of infrared measurement or imaging to evaluate surface-temperature patterns.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IR Thermography

\*\*Unit\*\*: Temperature in Degree Celsius (°C) or Kelvin (K)

\---

\## Vibration Analysis

Measurement and interpretation of mechanical vibration to assess machine condition.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Displacement, velocity or acceleration units as specified

\---

\## Motor Current Signature Analysis

A condition-monitoring technique that analyzes Motor Current patterns for indications of electrical or mechanical faults.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MCSA

\*\*Unit\*\*: Current in Ampere (A) and spectral Frequency in Hertz (Hz)

\---

\## Smart Grid

An electrical grid using sensing, communication, automation and intelligent control to improve monitoring and operation.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Microgrid

A group of interconnected Loads and distributed Energy resources operating as a controllable electrical entity, with grid-connected or islanded capability where designed.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: MG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Wide-Bandgap Semiconductor

A Semiconductor material with a larger Energy Bandgap than Silicon, such as Silicon Carbide or Gallium Nitride.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: WBG

\*\*Unit\*\*: Bandgap Energy in Electronvolt (eV)

\---

\## Industrial Internet of Things

Connected industrial devices and systems that exchange operational data for monitoring, control or analysis.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: IIoT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Operational Technology

Hardware and software that monitors or controls physical industrial processes and equipment.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: OT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Digital Twin

A digital model linked with data from a physical asset or system for analysis, prediction or decision support.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: DT

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Professional Competence

The demonstrated ability to apply knowledge, skills, judgment and responsibility within an engineering scope.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---

\## Engineering Ethics

Professional principles guiding safety, honesty, fairness, competence and responsibility in engineering work.

\*\*Symbol\*\*: —

\*\*Unit\*\*: Not applicable

\---
