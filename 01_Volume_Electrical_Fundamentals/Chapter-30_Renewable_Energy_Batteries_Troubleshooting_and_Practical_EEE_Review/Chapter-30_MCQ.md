\# Basic MCQ

\### MCQ-001

\*\*Question\*\*

Which statement best defines Renewable Energy?

A. Energy from naturally replenished sources
B. Energy available only from coal
C. Energy that has no environmental impact under any condition
D. Energy that can never be converted into electricity

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Energy from naturally replenished sources\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Renewable Energy comes from sources such as sunlight, wind, flowing water, Biomass, geothermal heat and tides that are naturally replenished.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B describes a finite fossil fuel.
\- C is false because every Energy project has some impact.
\- D is false because renewable resources are widely converted into electricity.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Renewable does not mean impact-free.

\### MCQ-008

\*\*Question\*\*

Why must PV DC equipment have suitable DC ratings?

B. PV arrays are always de-energized in daylight

\- C is unsafe and false.
\- D is false because DC Current causes resistive heating.

\*\*Remember\*\*

\### MCQ-012

\*\*Question\*\*

What does a Battery rating of 100 Ah principally describe?

A. Charge Capacity under specified conditions
B. Exactly 100 Wh of Energy
C. A fixed output of 100 V
D. A guaranteed 100-hour runtime for every Load

\*\*Answer\*\*

\*\*A. Charge Capacity under specified conditions\*\*

\*\*Explanation\*\*

Ampere-hour is a charge Capacity rating. Energy also depends on Voltage, while actual runtime depends on Load, rate, temperature, age and cutoff limits.

\*\*Why Not Others?\*\*

\- B confuses Ah with Wh.
\- C is not implied by the Capacity rating.
\- D ignores Current, chemistry and operating conditions.

\*\*Remember\*\*

Ah measures charge; Wh measures Energy.
