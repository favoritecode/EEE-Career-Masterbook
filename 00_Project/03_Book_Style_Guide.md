\# Writing Style



Write in simple Bengali.



Keep technical engineering terms in English.



Explain difficult concepts with simple language.



Avoid unnecessary paragraphs.



Avoid repeating the same information.



\---



\# Content Density



The objective is not to increase page count.



Recommended Length



Small Topic



2–4 Pages



Medium Topic



4–6 Pages



Major Topic



6–10 Pages



Only exceptionally large topics may exceed these recommendations.



\---



\# Standard Topic Structure



Every important topic should follow this order.



1\. Learning Objective



2\. Definition



3\. Easy Explanation



4\. Technical Explanation



5\. Practical Example



6\. Written Exam Note



7\. Technical Viva



8\. Common Mistakes



9\. Quick Revision

\# Diagrams



Use only when they improve understanding.



Prefer simple diagrams.



Avoid unnecessary decoration.



Label every important part.



\---



\# Tables



Keep tables simple.



Avoid large tables.



Compare concepts side by side whenever possible.



\---
