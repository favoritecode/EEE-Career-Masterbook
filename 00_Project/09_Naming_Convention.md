\# Purpose



This document defines the official naming conventions used throughout the EEE Career Masterbook project.



Consistent naming improves organization, readability and long-term maintenance.



All contributors and AI assistants must follow these conventions.



\---



\# General Rules



Use English only.



Use clear and descriptive names.



Maintain the same naming style across the project.



Avoid unnecessary abbreviations.



Keep names short but meaningful.



\---
