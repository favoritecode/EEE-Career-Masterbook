\# Purpose



This document defines how every chapter, topic, explanation and question must be written.



All contributors and AI assistants must follow these guidelines.



\---



\# Writing Philosophy



EEE Career Masterbook is NOT a university textbook.



It is a practical engineering career guide designed for:



• Job Preparation



• Technical Viva



• Written Examination



• Industrial Learning



The objective is to help readers learn faster, remember longer and perform better in interviews.



\---



\# Target Reader



Assume every reader is:



• A beginner EEE student



OR



• A fresh graduate preparing for jobs



Never assume prior knowledge.



Always build the concept step by step.



\---



\# Language Policy



Primary Language



→ Bengali



Technical Language



→ English



Example



Voltage



Current



Resistance



Transformer



Relay



Circuit Breaker



Power Factor



Do NOT translate internationally accepted engineering terminology unnecessarily.



\---



\# Writing Style



Every explanation should be



✓ Simple



✓ Professional



✓ Practical



✓ Accurate



✓ Easy to revise



Avoid robotic AI-style writing.



Write naturally like an experienced engineer mentoring a junior engineer.



\---



\# Explanation Order



Every important topic should follow this order whenever applicable:



1\. Learning Objective



2\. Easy Explanation



3\. Technical Explanation



4\. Practical Example



5\. Industry Application



6\. Viva Answer



7\. Written Exam Note



8\. Common Mistakes



9\. Quick Revision



10\. MCQ



11\. Recommended Videos



12\. References



Small topics may omit sections that are not applicable.



\---



\# Content Priority



Always prioritize:



⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Frequently Asked



⭐⭐⭐⭐ High Value



⭐⭐⭐ Important



⭐⭐ Good to Know



⭐ Extra Knowledge



\---



\# Interview Rule



For every important topic answer:



• Why is this question asked?



• What answer impresses the interviewer?



• What mistakes should be avoided?



\---



\# Practical Rule



Whenever possible connect theory with real engineering.



Examples:



• Power Plant



• Substation



• Factory



• Distribution Line



• Home Wiring



• PLC System



• Industrial Motor



\---



\# Formula Rule



Every formula should explain:



• Meaning



• Symbol



• Unit



• Application



• Common Mistakes



Never present formulas without explanation.



\---



\# Diagram Rule



Prefer simple educational diagrams.



Avoid unnecessary complexity.



Every diagram must support learning.



\---



\# Video Rule



Each chapter should include:



• Beginner Video



• Complete Playlist



• Advanced Learning Resource (if needed)



All links must be clickable.



\---



\# Reference Rule



Preferred sources:



1\. Standard Engineering Textbooks



2\. IEEE Publications



3\. Manufacturer Manuals



4\. Government Standards



Avoid unreliable blogs or copied notes.



\---



\# Page Rule



Never increase page count unnecessarily.



If a topic needs only two pages,



write two pages.



Do not force ten pages.



\---



\# Revision Rule



Every chapter should end with:



• Chapter Summary



• Quick Revision



• Top Viva Questions



• MCQ Practice



• Written Questions



• Recommended Videos



• References



\---



\# Final Goal



The reader should finish a chapter and confidently say:



"I understand this topic and I am ready for interviews."
